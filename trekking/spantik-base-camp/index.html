I Looked at Every Spantik Trek & Expedition Operator for 2026

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026

Spantik, the Golden Peak (7,027m), is one of the most accessible 7,000m climbs in the Karakoram - and there is also a shorter base-camp trek for those who just want the glacier and the views. Here is who runs both, and what they charge in 2026.

I separate the base-camp trek from the full climbing expedition and only compare operators publishing a real price, noting where oxygen, high-altitude porters and royalty fees are included.

About the Spantik Trek & Expedition

The base-camp trek is a few days up the Chogo Lungma glacier from Arandu; the climbing expedition runs 28-31 days. The mountain is graded a good first 7,000er, but it is a genuine high-altitude climb above base camp.

  • 6-8 days
  • Above 4,000m
  • Moderate
  • Jun-Sep

How I compared these packages

I did this the way I'd want a friend to do it for me. What's usually included - and what's not

On most of these treks the operator price includes guides and support staff, porters, camping equipment, in-trek meals and the trekking permit. It usually excludes international flights, travel insurance, personal climbing or trekking gear, tips for the crew, and on restricted peaks the government royalty fee. Always get the itemised inclusions, porter weight limit and the payment and cancellation terms in writing before you pay.

How to choose your operator

  • Published vs quote - a real posted price is easier to compare; for quote-only operators, get it itemised.
  • What's bundled - permits, domestic flights and porters are where cheap quotes cut corners.
  • Group size - most headline rates assume a group; confirm the solo or small-group rate.
  • Crew welfare - porter weight limits, wages and insurance say a lot about an operator.
  • Local vs down-country - Baltistan and Hunza-based crews know the routes and logistics best.

FAQ

Is Spantik a trek or a climb?

Both. There is a short base-camp trek (from about $1,300), and a 28-31 day climbing expedition to the 7,027m summit (from about $3,500).

What does the expedition cost in 2026?

Base-camp service expeditions start near $3,500; full-board with oxygen and high-altitude porters runs $4,500-$7,950 depending on operator and ratio.

Do I need climbing experience for the summit?

Spantik is considered a good first 7,000m peak, but you need solid fitness, basic glacier/rope skills and acclimatisation. The base-camp trek needs none of that.

When is the season?

June to September.
