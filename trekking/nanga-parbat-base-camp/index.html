Nanga Parbat Base Camp Trek 2026: Operators & Costs Compared

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026

From Fairy Meadows, the trek to Nanga Parbat's Raikot base camp puts you under the 8,126m north face - the most popular short trek in Gilgit-Baltistan. Here is who runs it and what they charge in 2026.

I compare operators with a published price (some local ones quote in rupees), and note that many sell it as a Fairy Meadows package with the base-camp leg as one day.

About the Nanga Parbat Base Camp Trek

The base camp on the Raikot side sits around 3,900m, reached on a day hike from Fairy Meadows; full packages run 3-8 days from Islamabad. Easy to moderate.

  • 3-4 days
  • ~3,900m
  • Easy-Moderate
  • May-September

How I compared these operators

I searched for every operator running this trek, opened each package page, and recorded only prices actually published there - 5 of 5 below show a real 2026 figure; the rest quote per group. Prices are per person, usually land-only and group-based; permits, flights, insurance and tips are often extra. The top-3 search-result operators are not featured. Operator links are nofollow.

What's usually included - and what's not

On most of these treks the operator price includes guides and support staff, porters, camping equipment, in-trek meals and the trekking permit. It usually excludes international flights, travel insurance, personal climbing or trekking gear, tips for the crew, and on restricted peaks the government royalty fee. Always get the itemised inclusions, porter weight limit and the payment and cancellation terms in writing before you pay.

How to choose your operator

  • Published vs quote - a real posted price is easier to compare; for quote-only operators, get it itemised.
  • What's bundled - permits, domestic flights and porters are where cheap quotes cut corners.
  • Group size - most headline rates assume a group; confirm the solo or small-group rate.
  • Crew welfare - porter weight limits, wages and insurance say a lot about an operator.
  • Local vs down-country - Baltistan and Hunza-based crews know the routes and logistics best.

FAQ

How much does the Nanga Parbat base camp trek cost in 2026?

International-facing operators charge about $1,400-$1,700 per person; local operators price in rupees (around PKR 165,000 for a full package) and per-group tiers drop sharply with group size.

How hard is it?

Easy to moderate - a day hike from Fairy Meadows to base camp at ~3,900m. The hard part is the jeep road to Tatu and the walk up to Fairy Meadows.

How many days?

3-4 days for the core trip; 6-8 days for packages from Islamabad.

When is the season?

May to September.
