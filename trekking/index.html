K2 Base Camp

📍 Skardu

The Baltoro Glacier route. Concordia, four 8,000m peaks in one frame. A Restricted-Area trek: a licensed operator and guide are mandatory, no solo. Most demanding non-technical trek in Pakistan.

Gondogoro La
⏱ 20–21 days
↑ 5,585m
📍 Hushe Valley

The classic K2 circuit extension. Cross Gondogoro Pass (crampons required) from Hushe Valley to Concordia. You descend into K2 base camp from the top. Outstanding.

Strenuous
Hispar–Biafo Glacier
⏱ 20–22 days
↑ 5,151m
📍 Nagar to Askole

The longest glacier traverse outside the polar regions. Hispar Pass connects Hunza (Nagar) to Baltistan (Askole). Remote, serious, and unforgettable. A route for experienced trekkers only.

Fairy Meadows & Nanga Parbat Base Camp

↑ 3,960m
📍 Raikot Bridge

Pakistan's most accessible mountain vista. Open zone, no permit. Jeep to Tato, 2h hike to meadow, optional 4–5h return to base camp. Families do this regularly.

Easy
Rakaposhi Base Camp
⏱ 2–3 days
↑ 3,800–4,150m
📍 Minapin, Hunza

Best short trek in Hunza. Orchards to glacier in one day. One of the clearest close-up views of a 7,000m+ peak anywhere in Pakistan. No permit, no guide required.

Easy
Naltar Lakes
⏱ 1–2 days
↑ ~3,050–3,150m
📍 Naltar, Gilgit

Three glacier-fed lakes at different elevations — each a different colour from blue to turquoise to green. Easy hike, dramatic scenery. Best in autumn when the larch trees turn gold.

Moderate
Laila Peak Base Camp
⏱ ~2–3 days walk-in
↑ BC altitude varies
📍 Hushe Valley

Laila Peak (height disputed, ~6,069–6,200m) has a near-vertical granite face that is one of the most dramatic in the Karakoram. The approach through Hushe Valley is stunning. Underrated and rarely crowded.

Deosai Traverse
⏱ 3–4 days
↑ 4,114m
📍 Skardu

Across the world's second highest plateau. Wildflowers, Himalayan brown bears, and a sky that feels closer than it should. No technical difficulty. Deosai park entry fee applies — confirm the current rate locally.

Strenuous
Shimshal Pass
⏱ 10–12 days
↑ 4,735m
📍 Shimshal Village

One of Pakistan's most remote trekking routes. Shimshal village is only accessible by a narrow road cut into cliff faces. The pass itself leads to yak pastures used by nomadic herders. NOC required.
