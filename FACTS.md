# GB Guide — Canonical Fact Sheet
> Verified 2026-06-27 via multi-source web research. Use this as the single source of truth when writing/editing pages. Where sources disagree, use the RANGE and add "confirm before travel". Never publish a hard number for anything in the LOW-CONFIDENCE list.

- Islamabad ↔ Gilgit (KKH/N-35): ~520–560 km, 12–16 h
- Islamabad ↔ Hunza/Karimabad: ~600–620 km, 14–20 h (often 2 days)
- Islamabad ↔ Skardu (road): ~635 km, 16–18 h private  *(NOT 24h)*
- Islamabad ↔ Naran: ~270–283 km, 6–9 h
- Islamabad ↔ Fairy Meadows (Raikot Bridge): ~445–480 km, 12–14 h to bridge (derived; verify)
- Lahore ↔ Islamabad: ~375 km (M2), 4–5 h
- Lahore ↔ Hunza: ~900–950 km, 18–22 h
- Lahore ↔ Skardu: ~973 km, 18–22 h
- Gilgit ↔ Hunza/Karimabad: ~95–110 km, 2–3.5 h
- Gilgit ↔ Skardu: ~170–210 km, **4–5 h on the new Jaglot–Skardu road** *(NOT 7h — that was the old road)*
- Gilgit ↔ Naltar: ~34–40 km, 2–3 h (last ~14 km rough, 4×4)
- Gilgit ↔ Fairy Meadows (Raikot Bridge): ~76–80 km, ~2 h to bridge
- Hunza ↔ Khunjerab Pass: ~120–135 km, 3–5 h each way
- Hunza ↔ Attabad Lake: ~20–35 km, 0.5–1 h
- Karimabad ↔ Passu: ~45–47 km, 1–1.5 h
- Skardu ↔ Deosai edge: ~30–40 km, 3–4 h 4×4 (Sheosar Lake is much deeper in)
- Skardu ↔ Khaplu: ~103 km, 3–4 h
- Skardu ↔ Shigar: ~45 km, ~2 h
- Skardu ↔ Askole (K2 roadhead): ~130 km, 6–8 h rough jeep
- Raikot Bridge ↔ Tato (jeep): ~10–15 km, 1.5–2.5 h (dangerous 4×4)
- Tato ↔ Fairy Meadows (on foot): ~5 km, 3–4 h uphill
- Chilas ↔ Babusar Top: ~110 km, 3–4 h | Naran ↔ Babusar Top: ~65–70 km, 1.75–2.5 h

## Trek durations / max altitude / difficulty
- K2 Base Camp: 18–21 days total (~12–15 trek days), max 4,950–5,150 m, Strenuous
- K2 BC + Gondogoro La: 20–21 days, max 5,585 m, Strenuous→Technical (fixed ropes)
- Concordia: part of K2 BC trip (not standalone), ~4,500 m, Strenuous
- Snow Lake / Biafo-Hispar: ~20–22 days, max 5,151 m (Hispar La), Strenuous (glacier)
- Rakaposhi BC (Minapin): 2–3 days, 3,800–4,150 m, Easy–Moderate
- Rush Lake: 4–6 days, 4,694 m, Moderate–Challenging — **from Hopar/Nagar, NOT a Minapin add-on**
- Nanga Parbat BC (Fairy Meadows/Raikot): 2–5 days, ~3,900 m, Easy–Moderate
- Nanga Parbat BC (Rupal): 3–4 days, ~3,550 m, Moderate
- Spantik BC: 3–5 days one-way, ~4,200–4,360 m, Moderate
- Masherbrum BC (Hushe): 5–6 days, ~4,280–4,500 m, Easy–Moderate
- Laila Peak BC (Hushe): walk-in ~2–3 days, Moderate (BC altitude not cleanly sourced)
- Haramosh/Kutwal Lake: 5–7 days, lake ~3,300 m, Moderate
- Karambar Lake: trek ~3–4 days, 4,272 m, Challenging (remote)
- Naltar Lakes: 1–2 days, ~3,050–3,150 m, Easy
- Hushe: NOT a trek — road-accessible gateway village (~3,050 m)

## Flights (both weather-dependent & frequently cancelled — always advise road backup)
- Islamabad↔Skardu (KDU): PIA / Airblue / AirSial, jets (A320/A321; PIA also ATR), ~1 h, ~20 flights/week seasonal. Skardu is an international airport (re-inaugurated Dec 2021).
- Islamabad↔Gilgit (GIL): **PIA only, ATR 42 turboprop**, ~50–60 min, Visual Flight Rules only → routine cloud/wind cancellations, often not retried same day.

## Seasons / openings (snowfall-dependent; shifts weeks year to year)
- Khunjerab Pass: practical tourist window May–Oct (closed ~Dec 30–Apr 1; "year-round" still transitioning)
- Babusar Pass: ~mid/late June–Sept/early Oct, daylight-only (gate hours disputed 7–6 vs 9–5)
- Deosai: ~mid/late June–late Sept/Oct, best Jun–Jul, snowbound Nov–May
- Fairy Meadows: ~Apr/May–Sept/Oct
- Hunza blossom: cherry/apricot peak late March–mid April; autumn colour late Sept–Oct
- Best overall: May–October

## Permits & fees
- NOC abolished 26 Mar 2019 for foreign tourists in GB/AJK/Chitral/open cantonments. Border buffer zones still restricted (Pak-China 10mi, Pak-Afghan 10mi, LoC 5mi, Siachen 10mi).
- Still permit/operator-required: K2 BC / Baltoro / Concordia / Gondogoro (Restricted Area, licensed operator + guide, no solo); Shimshal passes, Chapursan, Snow Lake. Closed: Siachen, LoC/Afghan-adjacent.
- Mountaineering royalties (>6,500 m, 2025 collective): K2 summer ~$3,500; trekking permit ~$150 (RE-VERIFY 2026+).
- Park/entry fees CONFLICTING — treat all as indicative, "confirm current rate": Deosai foreigners ~PKR 3,100 / $20–40; Khunjerab NP ~$8–40; CKNP trekking ~$150.
