## Trek durations / max altitude / difficulty
- K2 Base Camp: 18–21 days total (~12–15 trek days), max 4,950–5,150 m, Strenuous
- K2 BC + Gondogoro La: 20–21 days, max 5,585 m, Strenuous→Technical (fixed ropes; ACP-certified guide)
- Concordia: part of K2 BC trip (not standalone), ~4,500 m
- Snow Lake / Biafo-Hispar: ~20–22 days, max 5,151 m (Hispar La), Strenuous (glacier)
- Rakaposhi BC (Minapin → Tagaphari): 2–3 days, base camp ~3,300–3,500 m (higher viewpoints/Kacheli above), Easy–Moderate
- Rush Lake: 4–6 days, 4,694 m, Moderate–Challenging — **from Hopar/Nagar, NOT a Minapin add-on**. Hisper/Nagar roads were cut by GLOFs for ~2 months in 2026 — check access.
- Nanga Parbat BC (Fairy Meadows/Raikot): 2–5 days, ~3,900 m, Easy–Moderate
- Nanga Parbat BC (Rupal): 3–4 days, ~3,550 m, Moderate
- Spantik BC: 3–5 days one-way, ~4,200–4,360 m · Masherbrum BC (Hushe): 5–6 days, ~4,280–4,500 m · Laila Peak BC (Hushe): ~2–3 days walk-in

- Seasons: core Baltoro/CKNP glaciers effectively closed to trekking Oct–Apr; upper Baltoro closed in May; main window Jun–Sep.

## Flights (all weather-dependent, frequently cancelled — always advise a road backup + buffer day)
- **PIA is privately owned since 29 Jun 2026** (Arif Habib-led consortium). GB routes still operating (Oct 2026). Don't describe PIA as state-owned.
- Islamabad ↔ Skardu (KDU): **PIA, Airblue, AirSial — all A320 jets** (no PIA ATR on this route now), ~1 h–1 h 10, ~13–21 flights/week (thinner in winter). Skardu is an international airport (since Dec 2021).
- Islamabad ↔ Gilgit (GIL): **PIA only, ATR turboprop**, ~1 h, ~6 flights/week, visual-flight-rules → routine cloud/wind cancellations, often not retried same day. PIA has very few ATRs → the most fragile route.
- Karachi ↔ Skardu: PIA direct, ~3–4/week, ~2 h 20. Lahore ↔ Skardu: PIA nonstop listed (frequency unconfirmed).
- **No** Gilgit ↔ Skardu flights. **Fly Jinnah does not fly nonstop to Skardu/Gilgit. SereneAir is grounded (since Oct 2025) and never flew to Skardu** — never list it.
- Satellite (RNP-AR) approaches for Skardu/Gilgit were planned for 2026 but are NOT confirmed live — don't claim fewer cancellations.

## Passes / seasons (snowfall-dependent; shifts weeks year to year)
- **Khunjerab Pass:** officially open **year-round since 1 Dec 2024** (for trade). Winter passenger/tourist crossing not documented → practical tourist window **May–Oct**; winter access can be suspended for days. **Sost border: Mon–Fri only**, PK side ~09:00–17:00, closed on PK/China holidays; Chinese visa needed in advance; no self-drive in China.
- **Babusar Pass:** opened **~25–27 May in 2026** (and ~22–23 May 2025), first one-way; can close again for snow in early June (fully closed ~4–6 Jun 2026). Closes for winter **late Oct–Nov** (2025: snow closures from ~6 Nov, formal closure 28–29 Nov). Daylight only — no travel after 6 pm (gates ~7 am–6 pm, Med confidence). Publish as "open roughly late May/June → Oct/Nov".
- **Deosai:** ~mid-June → late Sep/early Oct; snowbound Nov–May; best Jun–Aug.
- **Fairy Meadows:** ~Apr/May–Sep/Oct.
- **Hunza blossom:** cherry/apricot peak late March–mid April (a season, not an official annual festival); autumn colour late Sep–Oct.
- **Monsoon risk:** late July–mid August brought multi-day closures of the KKH, Jaglot–Skardu (S-1), Babusar and valley roads in **both 2025 and 2026**. Best road months: May–June and Sep–Oct. Best overall: May–October.

## Permits, visas, advisories (Oct 2026)
- **Visa:** the free "Visa Prior to Arrival" for 126 nationalities was **suspended from 1 Jan 2026**. Apply for a paid **e-visa at visa.nadra.gov.pk**: ~USD 60 (US/UK/Canada), ~USD 35 (EU), others vary; some nationalities free (e.g. China, Malaysia, UAE, Saudi). Processing 7–20 working days → apply 4–6 weeks ahead. Hotel booking suffices; LOI only for private hosts.
- **NOC abolished (Mar 2019)** for GB tourism, BUT foreigners must **register / show passport at police checkposts** — carry several passport + visa copies. Border buffer zones restricted: **10 mi from LoC**, 10 mi China/Afghan borders (Wakhan belt possibly 30 mi — Low).
- Permit + licensed operator + guide needed: K2 BC / Baltoro / Concordia / Gondogoro La, Snow Lake / Biafo-Hispar, Shimshal passes, Chapursan beyond Baba Ghundi, Masherbrum Pass. No solo trekking in restricted zones. Permits can take 4 weeks–2 months.
- **Mountaineering royalties (since May 2025, per person, unchanged 2026):** K2 USD 3,500 (summer); other 8,000ers USD 2,500; 7,000 m peaks ~USD 500. Pakistanis K2 PKR 50,000. (NOT "collective".)
- **Advisories:** UK FCDO: GB is **not** under any "advise against" (GB-wide all-but-essential advice ran 12 Mar–15 Apr 2026 after unrest, then lifted); FCDO still advises against **all** travel on KKH Mansehra–Chilas. US: Pakistan Level 3 "Reconsider Travel"; GB not Level 4.
- **2026 events to reference carefully:** Mar 2026 unrest (Gilgit/Skardu curfews, mobile shutdown); GB elections 7 Jun 2026; Jul–Aug 2026 floods. Mobile/internet can be suspended during unrest, Muharram, and floods → carry offline maps and cash.

## SIM, cash, emergency
- SIM: passport + visa + biometric at franchise; max 2 SIMs per visit, valid while visa valid. Register phone free for up to 120 days via PTA DRS. **SCOM** has the widest GB coverage; Jazz/Zong/Telenor/Ufone work in towns and on the KKH.
- Cash: ATMs scarce/unreliable in Hunza, foreign cards don't always work; best for foreign cards: Dubai Islamic Bank, Faysal Bank (fees ~PKR 600–1,200/withdrawal). Carry PKR from Islamabad.
- Emergency: **Rescue 1122**; **Police 15** (GB helpline 05811-15); **Tourist police 1422** with area code (Gilgit 05811, Hunza/Nagar 05813, Astore 05817).
