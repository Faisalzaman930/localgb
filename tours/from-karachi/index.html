The Best Northern Pakistan Tour Packages from Karachi in 2026 - Flights, Routes & Real Costs

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026·13 min read

Karachi sends huge numbers of travellers north every summer to escape the heat - and the single biggest factor in a Karachi trip is distance. The north is about 1,500 km away, so a road journey means two-plus days each way; for almost everyone, the sensible move is to fly. That makes "tour packages from Karachi" really a question of flights plus a northern itinerary. I'm from Gilgit-Baltistan and I help Karachiites plan these trips; here's the honest 2026 picture - routes, real costs, and how to do it without wasting your leave on travel.

The Karachi reality

Fly. Karachi-Islamabad is about 2 hours with frequent flights; in season there are sometimes direct Karachi-Skardu/Gilgit options worth grabbing. From Islamabad you join the standard northern routes. Budget for flights on top of the package - that's the main difference from travelling out of Islamabad.

How to get north from Karachi

Fly to Islamabad, then north. The standard route: a 2-hour flight to Islamabad, then either the mountain flight onward to Gilgit/Skardu (about an hour, weather-dependent) or the Karakoram Highway by road. Direct to the mountains. When schedules allow, direct Karachi-Skardu or Karachi-Gilgit flights save a connection - grab them early, as they sell out and are seasonal. By road. Possible but brutal - two-plus days each way; only for road-trip purists with lots of time. For nearly everyone, fly in and, at most, drive one leg within the north.

Each northern trip from Karachi

Hunza (7-9 days). Fly to Islamabad or Gilgit, then the forts, Attabad, Passu and Khunjerab - the trip most Karachi first-timers should pick (Hunza guide). Skardu (7-9 days). Fly in (ideally direct when available) for the lakes and Deosai (Skardu guide). The full circuit (10-12 days). The best use of the long journey - Hunza and Skardu together (GB circuit). Naran (5-6 days) works too but is more travel-for-time given the distance (Naran guide).

What it costs from Karachi in 2026

Take the ex-Islamabad package price and add return flights - roughly Rs 30,000-50,000+ per person depending on season and route. So a Hunza trip lands around Rs 50,000-70,000 all-in, Skardu similar, and the full circuit around Rs 75,000-100,000+ - before upgrades. Private and honeymoon tours run Rs 160,000-190,000 plus flights. Booking flights early is the single biggest way to control the total. Always confirm whether a "from" price is the full cost or a deposit.

When to go & is it safe in 2026?

Summer (June-August) is peak - and exactly when Karachi most wants to escape the heat, so book flights and hotels early. Autumn (late September-October) is quieter and beautiful. Northern Pakistan is among the safest parts of the country for travellers; the real variables are seasonal road/pass access and weather-dependent mountain flights, not security. FAQ

How much is a northern tour package from Karachi in 2026?

The ex-Islamabad package plus return flights (~Rs 30,000-50,000+ pp): Hunza around Rs 50,000-70,000 all-in, Skardu similar, the full circuit around Rs 75,000-100,000+. Private/honeymoon Rs 160,000-190,000 plus flights.

Should I fly or drive from Karachi to the north?

Fly - it's about 1,500 km, so road means two-plus days each way. Karachi-Islamabad is a 2-hour flight; sometimes there are direct Karachi-Skardu/Gilgit flights worth grabbing.

Which northern trip is best from Karachi?

Because flights dominate the cost, longer trips make more sense - Hunza or Skardu (7-9 days) or the full circuit (10-12 days). A short Naran dash rarely justifies the airfare.

Is travel to the north from Karachi safe in 2026?

Yes - among the safest regions in Pakistan. The variables are seasonal access and weather-dependent flights, not security.
