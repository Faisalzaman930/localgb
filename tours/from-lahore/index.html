The Best Northern Pakistan Tour Packages from Lahore in 2026 - Routes, Costs & Honest Advice

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026·13 min read

Lahore is one of the biggest sources of northern travellers in Pakistan, and the good news is you're only one short leg further from the mountains than Islamabad. Most "tour packages from Lahore" simply add the Lahore-Islamabad connection to a standard northern itinerary - and for Naran, there are popular direct coaches. I'm from Gilgit-Baltistan and help Lahoris plan these trips; here's the honest 2026 picture of routes, real costs, and how to choose.

The Lahore reality

You're ~4-4.5 hours from Islamabad on the M-2 motorway (or a 45-minute flight). From there it's the same northern routes everyone uses. For Naran specifically, direct overnight coaches from Lahore exist - sometimes the simplest option of all.

Three ways north from Lahore

1) Direct coach to Naran (Naran only). Overnight Daewoo/private coaches run Lahore-Naran in season - cheapest and simplest for a Kaghan trip. 2) Motorway to Islamabad, then join. Drive or coach the M-2 (~4-4.5 hrs) to Islamabad and start any northern package there - the standard route for Hunza, Skardu and the circuit. 3) Fly Lahore-Islamabad (or onward). A 45-minute hop saves the road time; occasionally there are seasonal Lahore-Skardu/Gilgit options worth checking. Most Lahore travellers do (2) for the flexibility.

What it costs from Lahore in 2026

Take the ex-Islamabad prices above and add the connecting leg: roughly Rs 2,000-3,000 each way by coach, or the price of a short flight. So a Naran trip lands around Rs 13,000-15,000 all-in, Hunza around Rs 20,000+, and the circuit around Rs 45,000+. Direct Naran coaches can be cheaper still. Private and honeymoon tours run Rs 160,000-190,000 plus the connection. As always, confirm whether a quoted "from" price is the full cost or a deposit.

When to go & is it safe in 2026?

The seasons are the same wherever you start: summer (June-August) opens the whole north and the high passes; spring brings Hunza blossom; autumn brings the colour. Northern Pakistan is among the safest regions in the country for travellers - the real variables are seasonal road/pass access and weather-dependent flights, not security. FAQ

How much is a northern tour package from Lahore in 2026?

The ex-Islamabad rates plus a connecting leg: Naran around Rs 13,000-15,000 all-in (or a direct coach), Hunza around Rs 20,000+, Skardu around Rs 26,000+, the full circuit around Rs 45,000+. Private/honeymoon Rs 160,000-190,000 plus the connection.

Is there a direct tour from Lahore to Naran?

Yes - overnight coaches run Lahore-Naran in season (about 10-12 hours), so a Kaghan trip needn't route through Islamabad. For Hunza and Skardu, you connect to Islamabad first.

Should I drive or fly from Lahore?

The M-2 motorway to Islamabad is ~4-4.5 hours and the usual choice; a 45-minute flight saves time if your schedule is tight. From Islamabad it's the standard air-or-road routes north.

Is travel to the north from Lahore safe in 2026?

Yes - it's among the safest regions in Pakistan. The variables are seasonal access and weather, not security.
