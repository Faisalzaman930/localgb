What's usually included - and what's not

Across these operators, a Gilgit package typically includes transport with a driver, hotels, a guide, and at least breakfast; many add lunch and dinner. It usually excludes domestic flights, entry tickets and jeep surcharges (for example the Khunjerab permit or a Deosai 4WD), tips, and personal spending. Ask every operator for the itemised inclusions, the named hotels, and the payment & cancellation policy in writing before you pay.

How to choose

  • Pacing - count the real sightseeing days versus transfer days; a "8-day" trip with four driving days is really a short trip.
  • Meals - breakfast-only vs full board changes the real cost; it's in the table above.
  • Hotels - get the actual hotel names, not "3-star equivalent".
  • Inclusions - jeeps, boats, permits and entries are where cheap quotes cut corners.
  • Payment - confirm the advance, the balance, and the cancellation terms.

FAQ

How much is a Gilgit tour package in 2026?

A 5-day taste runs lower; a real 7-8 day Hunza+Skardu circuit is mid-range; private grand tours reach Rs 160,000-190,000. See the table.

Is Gilgit the same as Gilgit-Baltistan?

People use 'Gilgit' for both the city and the wider region (officially Gilgit-Baltistan, formerly the northern areas). This page covers the full-region circuit.

How many days for the full circuit?

Seven to eight for Hunza+Skardu; ten to twelve for a grand circuit with Deosai and Gilgit.

Is it safe and open in 2026?

Yes - among the safest regions in Pakistan; the full circuit is a summer trip because the high passes are seasonal.
