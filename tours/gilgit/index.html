Gilgit Tour Packages 2026: Prices and 8 Tours Compared

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026

"Gilgit" is how most travellers search for the whole northern region - also called Gilgit-Baltistan or the northern areas. A Gilgit tour usually means the grand circuit: Hunza, Skardu, Gilgit, Deosai and the Karakoram in one trip. Below are 2026 prices for each kind of trip, then real tours side by side.

I'm from Gilgit-Baltistan, so here's the honest comparison: 8 Gilgit-Baltistan tour packages from 5 different operators - short road trips to Hunza, flight-based circuits, and grand tours that take in Hunza, Skardu, Fairy Meadows, Deosai and the Ghizer valleys. I read every itinerary day by day and rewrote it below in plain words, so you can see exactly where each trip spends its time.

How I compared them

Packages, prices and dates were re-checked on each operator's own website on 21 October 2026. Many operators don't publish prices ("on request") - that's normal in Pakistan, and I say so rather than guess. Where an operator says where it's based, I show it.

UK travel advice (FCDO) warns against all travel on the KKH between Mansehra and Chilas. If that applies to you, choose a trip that flies, or uses the Babusar road (open roughly late May to October).

Gilgit tour package prices in 2026

Most people searching for a "Gilgit tour" mean a trip around Gilgit-Baltistan - Hunza, Skardu, Fairy Meadows and the valleys around them. Here is what those trips cost in 2026, per person unless noted (always confirm the current rate):

Type of tripTypical 2026 price
Budget group trip by road, 5-8 daysPublished from about Rs 17,500 (Hunza, 5 days) to Rs 25,500 (Skardu, 8 days)
Private trip with your own car and driver - HunzaAbout Rs 29,500 (3 days), Rs 49,500 (5 days), Rs 69,500 (7 days)
Skardu group trip by road, 7-8 daysAbout Rs 29,500-39,500
Skardu by air, 4-6 daysAbout Rs 63,000-145,000
Guided tours for foreign visitorsAbout US$850 (Hunza, 7-9 days) to US$1,050-1,850 (11-17 day circuits)
Honeymoon packages, 5-8 daysRs 120,000-190,000 per package, as listed

What changes the price: flying instead of driving (flights cost about PKR 18,000-30,000 each way), a private car instead of a shared group vehicle, the hotel level, and the season - blossom (late March to mid-April) and autumn (late September to October) are the busiest. Travelling from Lahore adds about PKR 6,900-8,400 each way by coach; from Karachi, PIA flies direct to Skardu about 3-4 times a week, or you can connect through Islamabad.

How many days do you need for a Gilgit-Baltistan tour?

  • 5-8 days: One or two regions by road - Hunza, or Hunza and Skardu - with long drives at each end. 5-Day Hunza, Gilgit & Khunjerab Trip, 8-Day Hunza & Skardu via Naran.
  • 10-13 days: Hunza plus Fairy Meadows, or Skardu, Khaplu and Hunza with a flight - the sweet spot for a first trip. Gilgit-Baltistan Autumn Tour, Northern Spring Tour, Gilgit-Baltistan Tour (Fairy Meadows, Skardu, Hunza).
  • 15 days or more: The full circuit: add Chapursan, the Ghizer valleys (Phander, Yasin, Ishkoman) or Deosai and Astore. Gilgit-Baltistan Tour (Fairy Meadows, Hunza, Ghizer), Gilgit-Baltistan Autumn Tour, Deosai Plateau Tour (Skardu, Deosai, Astore, Hunza).

Whatever the length, keep one spare day for a cancelled flight or a landslide - Machulo's spring tour builds one in, which is a good sign.

What's usually included - and what's not

Across these operators, a Gilgit package typically includes transport with a driver, hotels, a guide, and at least breakfast; many add lunch and dinner. It usually excludes domestic flights, entry tickets and jeep surcharges (for example the Khunjerab National Park fee or a Deosai 4WD), tips, and personal spending. Ask every operator for the itemised inclusions, the named hotels, and the payment & cancellation policy in writing before you pay.

How to choose

  • Pacing - count the real sightseeing days versus transfer days; a "8-day" trip with four driving days is really a short trip.
  • Meals - breakfast-only vs full board changes the real cost; it's in the table above.
  • Hotels - get the actual hotel names, not "3-star equivalent".
  • Inclusions - jeeps, boats, permits and entries are where cheap quotes cut corners.
  • Payment - confirm the advance, the balance, and the cancellation terms.

FAQ

How much is a Gilgit tour package in 2026?

Budget group trips by road start at about Rs 17,500 (5 days, Hunza) to Rs 25,500 (8 days, Skardu) per person. A private Hunza trip runs about Rs 29,500-69,500 for 3-7 days, Skardu by air about Rs 63,000-145,000, and guided tours for foreign visitors about US$850-1,850.

Is Gilgit the same as Gilgit-Baltistan?

People use 'Gilgit' for both the city and the wider region (officially Gilgit-Baltistan, formerly the northern areas). This page covers the full-region circuit.

How many days for the full circuit?

Seven to eight for Hunza+Skardu; ten to twelve for a grand circuit with Deosai and Gilgit.

Is it safe and open in 2026?

Yes - among the safest regions in Pakistan; the full circuit is a summer trip because the high passes are seasonal, and late July to mid-August brought multi-day monsoon road closures in 2025 and 2026, so build in buffer days.
