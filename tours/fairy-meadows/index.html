What's usually included - and what's not

Across these operators, a Fairy Meadows package typically includes transport with a driver, hotels, a guide, and at least breakfast; many add lunch and dinner. It usually excludes domestic flights, entry tickets and jeep surcharges (for example the Khunjerab permit or a Deosai 4WD), tips, and personal spending. Ask every operator for the itemised inclusions, the named hotels, and the payment & cancellation policy in writing before you pay.

How to choose

  • Pacing - count the real sightseeing days versus transfer days; a "14-day" trip with four driving days is really a short trip.
  • Meals - breakfast-only vs full board changes the real cost; it's in the table above.
  • Hotels - get the actual hotel names, not "3-star equivalent".
  • Inclusions - jeeps, boats, permits and entries are where cheap quotes cut corners.
  • Payment - confirm the advance, the balance, and the cancellation terms.

FAQ

How much is a Fairy Meadows trip in 2026?

Roughly Rs 15,000-35,000 per person for 3-4 days from Islamabad depending on group size and whether the base-camp hike is included; see the table.

How hard is the hike?

About 3-4 km uphill (2-3 hours) from Tato; ponies and porters are available.

Is the Raikot jeep safe?

It's exposed but safe with experienced local drivers - go with a reputable operator.

When is Fairy Meadows open?

Roughly late May to early October; snow closes the jeep track outside that.
