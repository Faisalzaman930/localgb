What's usually included - and what's not

Across these operators, a Chitral & Kalash package typically includes transport with a driver, hotels, a guide, and at least breakfast; many add lunch and dinner. It usually excludes domestic flights, entry tickets and jeep surcharges (for example the Khunjerab permit or a Deosai 4WD), tips, and personal spending. Ask every operator for the itemised inclusions, the named hotels, and the payment & cancellation policy in writing before you pay.

How to choose

  • Pacing - count the real sightseeing days versus transfer days; a "17-day" trip with four driving days is really a short trip.
  • Meals - breakfast-only vs full board changes the real cost; it's in the table above.
  • Hotels - get the actual hotel names, not "3-star equivalent".
  • Inclusions - jeeps, boats, permits and entries are where cheap quotes cut corners.
  • Payment - confirm the advance, the balance, and the cancellation terms.

FAQ

How much is a Chitral &amp; Kalash tour in 2026?

A 4-day Kalash & Chitral trip starts around Rs 14,500; combined Shandur or Chitral-plus-GB trips are custom-quoted.

When are the Kalash festivals?

Chilam Joshi in spring, Uchal in summer, Choimus in winter.

How do I reach Chitral?

A short flight from Islamabad or by road via the Lowari Tunnel (year-round).

Is Chitral safe in 2026?

Generally yes - peaceful and welcoming, with reliable Lowari Tunnel access; use a good local operator.
