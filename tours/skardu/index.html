What's usually included - and what's not

Across these operators, a Skardu package typically includes transport with a driver, hotels, a guide, and at least breakfast; many add lunch and dinner. It usually excludes domestic flights, entry tickets and jeep surcharges (for example the Khunjerab permit or a Deosai 4WD), tips, and personal spending. Ask every operator for the itemised inclusions, the named hotels, and the payment & cancellation policy in writing before you pay.

How to choose

  • Pacing - count the real sightseeing days versus transfer days; a "8-day" trip with four driving days is really a short trip.
  • Meals - breakfast-only vs full board changes the real cost; it's in the table above.
  • Hotels - get the actual hotel names, not "3-star equivalent".
  • Inclusions - jeeps, boats, permits and entries are where cheap quotes cut corners.
  • Payment - confirm the advance, the balance, and the cancellation terms.

FAQ

How much does a Skardu tour package cost in 2026?

Group trips over Babusar start lower; private Skardu/Deosai tours run higher; luxury/honeymoon packages reach Rs 160,000-190,000. See the table.

How many days do I need for Skardu?

Six to eight days for the lakes, Shigar, Khaplu and a Deosai day; longer for treks.

Is the Deosai jeep included?

Often separate - confirm it's bundled; Deosai is seasonal (roughly late June-September).

Should I fly or drive to Skardu?

The flight saves days but is weather-dependent; the road is reliable but long.
