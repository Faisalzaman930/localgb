Skardu Tour Packages 2026: 8 Tours Compared, Day by Day

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026

Skardu is the gateway to Baltistan - the cold deserts, Shangrila and the Kachura lakes, Shigar and Khaplu, the Deosai plateau, and the road-head for the great Karakoram treks. If you're deciding which Skardu trip to book, this page lays the real options side by side.

I'm from Gilgit-Baltistan, so here's the honest comparison: 8 Skardu tour packages from 7 different operators - a budget road trip from Islamabad, Skardu-based operators' own tours, flight-based blossom and autumn trips, and long routes that cross Deosai into Astore or Hunza. I read every itinerary day by day and rewrote it below in plain words, so you can see exactly where each trip spends its time.

How I compared them

Packages, prices and dates were re-checked on each operator's own website on 20 October 2026. Many operators don't publish prices ("on request") - that's normal in Pakistan, and I say so rather than guess. Where an operator says where it's based, I show it.

Deosai is only open from about mid-June to late September or early October; any trip that crosses it is summer-only. UK travel advice (FCDO) warns against all travel on the KKH between Mansehra and Chilas - trips that fly, or use the Babusar road (open roughly late May to October), avoid it.

How many days do you need for a Skardu tour?

  • 7-9 days: Enough for Skardu's lakes, Shigar, Khaplu and a Deosai day - fly at least one way or you lose two days on the road each way. 8-Day Skardu, Deosai, Shigar & Khaplu Trip, Skardu Cherry Blossom Tour, Skardu Tour (starts in Skardu), Deosai Plateau Tour (camping).
  • 10-11 days: Adds Hushe or Machulo in the Ghanche valleys, or a proper hike like Marsur Rock. Skardu Valley Tour, Baltistan Autumn Tour.
  • 15 days or more: Grand loops that cross Deosai to Astore and Rama Lake, then carry on to Hunza. Skardu, Deosai, Rama Lake & Fairy Meadows Tour, Deosai Plateau Tour (Deosai, Astore and Hunza).

Skardu flights use jets (Gilgit's use small turboprops), but they're still weather-dependent - keep a buffer day, and know the road plan B before you go.

What's usually included - and what's not

Across these operators, a Skardu package typically includes transport with a driver, hotels, a guide, and at least breakfast; many add lunch and dinner. It usually excludes domestic flights, entry tickets and jeep surcharges (for example the Deosai National Park fee or a Deosai 4WD), tips, and personal spending. Ask every operator for the itemised inclusions, the named hotels, and the payment & cancellation policy in writing before you pay.

How to choose

  • Pacing - count the real sightseeing days versus transfer days; a "8-day" trip with four driving days is really a short trip.
  • Meals - breakfast-only vs full board changes the real cost; it's in the table above.
  • Hotels - get the actual hotel names, not "3-star equivalent".
  • Inclusions - jeeps, boats, permits and entries are where cheap quotes cut corners.
  • Payment - confirm the advance, the balance, and the cancellation terms.

FAQ

How much does a Skardu tour package cost in 2026?

A published 8-day group road trip from Islamabad starts at about Rs 25,500. Skardu-based operators' guided tours for foreign visitors start around US$1,050 for 11 days, and many operators quote on request. Honeymoon packages cost more - see the honeymoon page.

How many days do I need for Skardu?

Six to eight days for the lakes, Shigar, Khaplu and a Deosai day; longer for treks.

Is the Deosai jeep included?

Often separate - confirm it's bundled; Deosai is seasonal (roughly mid-June to late September).

Should I fly or drive to Skardu?

The flight saves days but is weather-dependent; the road is long, the Jaglot-Skardu road still has flood-damaged sections (allow 1-2 hours extra), and monsoon slides in late July to mid-August can close it for days.
