Overview

Skardu Tour Packages,

A Skardu tour package bundles the things that are genuinely hard to arrange yourself from down-country — reliable transport over long mountain distances, hotels that are actually open in season, the 4WD you need for Deosai, and someone who knows when the Skardu flight is about to be cancelled. For most travellers coming from Islamabad, Lahore or Karachi in 2026, a package is simply the difference between a smooth trip and three days lost to logistics.

Getting there

By Air or By Road
in 2026

Almost every Skardu package starts in Islamabad, because that's where the airport and the road both begin. You have two ways in, and the one you choose shapes the whole trip — its length, its price, and how much can go wrong.

By air. The Islamabad–Skardu flight is about an hour and, on a clear morning, one of the most beautiful flights anywhere — it passes Nanga Parbat close enough to count the ridgelines. The problem is reliability: it is entirely weather-dependent and is cancelled often, sometimes for days in a row. Any honest by-air package builds in a buffer day and a road back-up plan. Never book a package that puts your flight on the last possible day before an onward international connection.

By road. The drive from Islamabad runs up the Karakoram Highway and then the Skardu Road along the Indus — roughly 20 to 24 hours of driving, normally split over two days with an overnight near Chilas or Besham. It's long, but it's reliable, far cheaper, and genuinely spectacular. Most by-road packages turn the journey itself into part of the trip rather than a transfer to endure.

In practice the smart 2026 choice for many groups is to fly one way and drive the other — you get the views and the buffer without two full days each way on the road.

Inclusions

What's Included
& Excluded

A fair Skardu package usually includes: all ground transport with a driver, accommodation for every night, breakfast (sometimes half-board), a guide or trip coordinator, and the local sightseeing around Skardu. Better packages also fold in the Deosai 4WD and any park fees so there are no surprises on the day.

Commonly excluded — and worth checking line by line: domestic flights and the cost of a back-up if they cancel, the Deosai jeep and park entry (if not bundled), lunches and dinners, entry tickets to forts and resorts, tips, and personal expenses. The single most useful question you can ask an operator is simply: "Send me the itemised inclusions and exclusions in writing." How they answer tells you everything.

Itineraries

Day-by-Day
Sample Itineraries

Two routes that work well in 2026 — copy them, or use them as a starting point and I'll adjust.

6-Day Skardu Classic (by road)

Day 1Islamabad to Chilas along the Karakoram Highway. Long driving day with stops at the Nanga Parbat viewpoint and the Indus–Gilgit river confluence. Overnight Chilas.
Day 2Chilas to Skardu via the Skardu Road along the Indus gorge. Arrive afternoon, settle in, easy evening at the bazaar. Overnight Skardu.
Day 3Lakes and forts: Shangrila (Lower Kachura), Upper Kachura, then Shigar Valley and Shigar Fort. Overnight Skardu.
Day 4Full-day Deosai jeep excursion — Sheosar Lake, the marmots and, if you're lucky, the brown bear. Back to Skardu by evening. Overnight Skardu.
Day 5Katpana cold desert, Kharpocho Fort viewpoint and Manthal Buddha rock in the morning, then begin the drive back to Chilas/Besham. Overnight en route.
Day 6Drive back to Islamabad, arriving evening.

8-Day Skardu & Deosai (fly in, drive out)

Day 1Morning flight Islamabad–Skardu (buffer plan if cancelled). Acclimatise, Katpana desert at sunset. Overnight Skardu.
Day 2Shangrila and Upper Kachura lakes, Manthal Buddha. Overnight Skardu.
Day 3Shigar Valley, Shigar Fort, and the apricot orchards. Overnight Shigar or Skardu.
Day 4Khaplu — Khaplu Palace and the Shyok valley, the gentler, greener side of Baltistan. Overnight Khaplu.
Day 5Return to Skardu, afternoon free for the bazaar and Kharpocho Fort. Overnight Skardu.
Day 6Full-day Deosai plateau — Sheosar Lake and the plains. Overnight Skardu.
Day 7Begin the drive out via the Skardu Road and KKH. Overnight Chilas/Besham.
Day 8Drive to Islamabad, arriving evening.
When to go

Best Time for a
Skardu Tour in 2026

Peak summer (June–August). The main season. Warm days, every hotel open, and the only reliable window for Deosai, which is typically passable from late June once the snow clears. Book ahead — this is also when domestic tourism peaks.

Autumn (September–October). My favourite. The crowds thin, the light turns golden, and the apricot and poplar foliage in Shigar and Khaplu is stunning. Deosai usually stays open into September. Nights get cold.

Winter (November–March). A specialist trip — bitterly cold, Deosai closed, and access mostly by the (often-cancelled) flight. The draw is frozen lakes, snow, and near-empty sights. Only worth it if you specifically want winter Baltistan.

Spring (April–May). Blossom season lower down and a good time for the lakes and valleys, though Deosai is still under snow. A quieter, cheaper shoulder.

Choosing

What to Check
Before You Book

Before you pay a deposit to anyone, get clear answers to five things: How is the Deosai jeep arranged, and is it in the price? What's the flight back-up plan if it cancels? Which named hotels are you staying in — not "3-star equivalent", actual names? Is the price per person and on what sharing basis? And is the operator local to Baltistan, who'll know the tracks and seasonal access, or a re-seller passing you down a chain?

The red flags are the mirror image: vague inclusions, no written itinerary, pressure to pay fast, a price that seems too low to be real, and reviews you can't verify. A trustworthy operator is happy to put everything in writing and happy to lose a sale to your questions.

Scope

On Its Own, or Part of
a Bigger Trip?

Skardu rewards a focused trip — there's easily a week of lakes, deserts, forts and the Deosai plateau without leaving Baltistan. But it also slots into a longer Gilgit-Baltistan circuit, and which you choose changes the route and the budget.

Skardu on its own (5–8 days). The best choice if your leave is limited or Deosai and the Baltistan valleys are the real reason you're coming. You go deeper rather than wider — time for Khaplu and the Shyok, a proper Deosai day, and an unhurried pace.

Skardu with Hunza (10–14 days). The full grand tour. Most people drive the Karakoram Highway up through Hunza, cross to Skardu, and loop back — two very different sides of Gilgit-Baltistan in one trip. It's the most rewarding way to see the region if you have the days; see the Hunza tour packages and the wider Gilgit-Baltistan circuit for how they join up.

Skardu and Deosai (6–7 days). A tighter loop for travellers whose main goal is the plateau, Sheosar Lake and the wildlife. Worth timing carefully — read the Deosai guide for the seasonal access window.

A note on altitude and packing. Skardu town sits around 2,200m and Deosai climbs above 4,000m, so even on a sightseeing tour you'll feel the height on the jeep day. Take it slowly the first 24 hours, drink more water than feels necessary, and pack a warm layer and sun protection regardless of season — Deosai can be cold and fiercely bright on the same afternoon. If anyone in your group has a heart or breathing condition, tell me in advance and I'll keep the route gentle.

FAQ

Common
Questions

How much does a Skardu tour package cost in 2026?

As a rough guide, a by-road group trip starts around Rs 55,000 per person, a fly-in mid-range trip around Rs 95,000, and a private premium trip around Rs 165,000 — all per person on twin-sharing from Islamabad, and all indicative. Group size, air vs road, hotels and the Deosai jeep move the number most, so request a current itemised quote.

How many days do I need for Skardu?

Five days is the practical minimum if you fly; six to eight is more comfortable, especially by road, and lets you add Deosai and Khaplu without rushing.

Is a Skardu tour package from Islamabad the standard?

Yes. Both the flight and the road start in Islamabad, so packages are normally priced from there. Travellers from Lahore or Karachi simply add a connecting leg, which we can include in the quote.

Should I fly or go by road to Skardu?

The flight saves days but is often cancelled by weather; the road is reliable, cheaper and scenic but takes two days each way. Flying one way and driving the other is the best of both for many groups.

Is Deosai included in a Skardu package?

Not always — it needs a 4WD and is seasonal, so the jeep and park fees are often quoted separately. Confirm it's bundled, and remember Deosai is generally only open from late June to September.

Are Skardu packages good for families?

Yes, especially the slower 7–8 day private version with comfortable hotels and a relaxed pace. Tell me the ages and I'll shape the route — for example, lighter days and lower altitude where it helps.

Are domestic flights included in the price?

Usually not, and neither is the cost of a back-up if the flight cancels. Always check whether flights are in or out of the quote so you're comparing like with like.
