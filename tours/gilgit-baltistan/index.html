I Compared the Best Gilgit-Baltistan Tour Packages for 2026 — the Full Northern Circuit, Honestly

By Faisal Zaman· Local, Gilgit-Baltistan· Updated June 2026·15 min read

"Gilgit-Baltistan tour packages" and "northern areas tour packages" are really the same search: people who want the whole north — Hunza, Skardu, Gilgit, Deosai and the Karakoram — in one trip, rather than a single valley. I'm from GB, and the honest truth is that the full circuit is the best way to see the region if you have the days, and a frustrating rush if you don't. This is the real 2026 picture: the actual circuit packages on the market, what they cost, how many days you really need, and how to do it without living on the road.

I won't push the big agencies already topping Google. I'd rather show you what's out there and arrange the right version for you.

How I compare GB circuit packages

Days-to-distance ratio first (GB is huge — a 6-day "grand tour" is mostly driving), then which regions are actually included (some "GB tours" never leave Hunza), the road-vs-air mix, transparency on price, and whether the operator knows Baltistan as well as Hunza. No fee changes the ranking.

The honest warning on the cheap ones. A "5-day Gilgit-Baltistan tour" from Rs 9,500–25,000 sounds great, but at that price and length it's mostly Naran plus a dash into Hunza — you won't really see Skardu or Deosai. They're good value for a first taste of the north, not a full circuit. And watch the deposits: some 7–8 day combined trips advertise "from Rs 18,500," which is a booking deposit, not the all-in price (realistically Rs 40,000+).

The real circuit — "Hunza & Skardu via Naran" (8 days, from ~Rs 40,000). This is the genuine two-region loop by road: up through Naran and Hunza, across to Skardu and Baltistan, and back. Eight days is the practical minimum to do both well without flying. The best value for actually seeing GB.

The comfortable version — "Naran, Hunza & Skardu" (7 days, from ~Rs 45,000). A slightly tighter routing with better hotels; good if you want the circuit with less roughing it.

Premium — "Private GB Grand Tour / Honeymoon" (8 days, Rs 160,000–190,000). Fly in to save road days, private vehicle, the best hotels in both Hunza and Skardu, and a pace that lets you enjoy it. This is what I book for couples, families and anyone short on time but not on budget.

International packages (USD 1,140–3,800). If you're coming from abroad, foreigner-facing operators run 7–14 day Pakistan tours in this range — more expensive than domestic rates because they include English-speaking guides, full board and international-standard logistics. I can often arrange an equivalent locally for less.

How many days do you really need?

This is the question that makes or breaks a GB trip. 5–6 days: pick one region — Hunza or Skardu — not both; anything more is a coach tour. 7–8 days: the sweet spot for Hunza + Skardu by road, or one region in real depth. 10–12 days: the proper grand circuit — Hunza, Skardu, Deosai, Gilgit and the side valleys, flying one leg to save time. 14 days: add treks, Fairy Meadows, or the remote valleys. If someone sells you "all of Gilgit-Baltistan in 6 days," they're selling you a lot of driving.

How the circuit works in 2026 — by air and road

GB is reached by air (Islamabad–Gilgit or Islamabad–Skardu, ~1 hour, weather-dependent and often cancelled) or by road up the Karakoram Highway (20–24 hours, split over two days). The full circuit almost always mixes both — fly one leg, drive the other — or, in summer, enters over Babusar Pass from Naran, which is how the budget combined tours keep costs down. Within GB, distances are long: Gilgit to Skardu is a full day's drive, Hunza to Khunjerab another. A good circuit package plans around these realities instead of pretending everything is close.

When to go in 2026

Summer (June–August) is the only window when the whole circuit is open — Babusar and Khunjerab passes clear, Deosai is passable, and you can combine regions freely. Spring (April–May) is blossom season in Hunza but Deosai and the high passes are still under snow. Autumn (late September–October) is stunning for colour in Hunza and Skardu, though Deosai and Babusar may close late in the season. Winter is for single-region specialists, not the circuit. For a true GB grand tour, aim for July to mid-September.

From Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi & beyond

Islamabad is the hub — every circuit starts here, by air or road. Lahore, Faisalabad and Multan add a motorway leg to Islamabad (coach, car or short flight). Karachi travellers fly to Islamabad (or straight to Gilgit/Skardu when seats allow) and join there. Is Gilgit-Baltistan safe and open in 2026?

Yes — GB is one of the safest regions in Pakistan for travellers, with a strong tourism economy and welcoming communities; Hunza in particular is a favourite for women and solo travellers. The honest caveats are about access, not security: the high passes (Babusar, Khunjerab, Deosai) are seasonal, the Gilgit/Skardu flights are weather-dependent, and the KKH can be briefly blocked by landslides after heavy rain. The towns and main valleys are open year-round; the full circuit is a summer trip. Check the live road & pass status before you finalise dates.

What to check before you book a circuit

For a multi-region trip, the questions matter even more: Does it actually reach the regions named, or is "GB tour" mostly Hunza? What's the air/road mix and the flight back-up? How many real sightseeing days versus driving days? Which named hotels in each town? And is the headline a full price or a deposit? Red flags: an implausibly short "grand tour," vague regions, and a price too low to cover the distances involved.

FAQ

How much does a Gilgit-Baltistan tour package cost in 2026?

From current listings: a 5-day taste of the north runs Rs 9,500–25,000; a real 7–8 day Hunza + Skardu circuit around Rs 40,000–45,000; and a private grand tour or honeymoon Rs 160,000–190,000 — per person, ex-Islamabad. Foreigner-facing international tours run roughly USD 1,140–3,800. Watch for deposit-only "from" prices.

What's the difference between "Gilgit-Baltistan" and "northern areas" tour packages?

None really — "northern areas" is the old name for Gilgit-Baltistan. Both searches mean the same region; this page covers both.

How many days do I need to see Gilgit-Baltistan properly?

Seven to eight days for Hunza + Skardu by road; ten to twelve for a proper grand circuit including Deosai and Gilgit. Anything under six days means picking one region.

Is Gilgit-Baltistan safe and open in 2026?

Yes — it's among the safest regions in Pakistan, open year-round in the towns and main valleys. The full circuit is a summer trip because the high passes (Babusar, Khunjerab, Deosai) are seasonal.

Can I combine Hunza, Skardu and Naran in one trip?

Yes — in summer the classic loop runs Islamabad → Naran → over Babusar → Hunza → Skardu → back, in 8–10 days. It's the best-value way to see the most of GB.
