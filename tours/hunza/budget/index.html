Hunza on a Budget: Cheap Packages vs Doing It Yourself

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026

Hunza doesn't have to be expensive. The two real choices are a budget group package or doing it yourself by coach - and which is cheaper depends mostly on whether you're travelling alone or with friends.

The cheapest Hunza packages in 2026

These are the lowest published prices in my Hunza tour packages comparison:

  • 5-Day Hunza & Khunjerab Pass Trip - from Rs 17,500 per person (breakfast and dinner).
  • 6-Day Hunza & Naltar Valley Trip - from Rs 19,500 (breakfast only).
  • Autumn Tour to Hunza - from Rs 22,000 (all meals).

Before you pay, ask: how many people share the vehicle, which hotels (by name), which meals, and whether the price is a deposit or the full amount.

On the ground, a budget traveller spends roughly PKR 5,000-8,000 a day. So a 6-day trip from Rawalpindi - two days on the coach and four in Hunza - comes to roughly PKR 30,000-45,000 per person for one person travelling alone.

So which is cheaper?

  • Travelling alone: a budget group package is usually cheaper, because you're sharing the vehicle with the group.
  • Three or four friends: doing it yourself often wins - you split one car and share rooms, and you choose your own pace and stops.

How to save money in Hunza

  • Avoid the peak weeks (blossom season and public holidays) if you can - rooms fill up and often cost more.
  • Stay in family-run guesthouses and eat local dishes - they're cheaper and the money stays in the valley.
  • Share a car for Khunjerab and Upper Hunza; split between four, a day's hire is very reasonable.
  • Carry enough cash - ATMs are limited outside Gilgit and Karimabad.

FAQ

What is the cheapest Hunza tour package in 2026?

The lowest published price I found is a 5-day trip by road from about Rs 17,500 per person, with a 6-day Hunza and Naltar trip from Rs 19,500. Confirm what's included before paying.

How much does a budget trip to Hunza cost if I do it myself?

Roughly PKR 30,000-45,000 per person for 6 days from Rawalpindi when travelling alone: about PKR 4,850-6,100 each way by coach, plus PKR 5,000-8,000 a day on the ground. Groups sharing a car and rooms pay less.

What is the cheapest way to get to Hunza?

The coach from Rawalpindi, about PKR 4,850-6,100 one way, taking 14-18 hours.

Is a cheap Hunza package worth it?

Often, if you're travelling alone - you share transport costs. Ask how many people share the vehicle, which hotels you'll stay in, which meals are included, and whether the price is a deposit or the full amount.
