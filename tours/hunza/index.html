Hunza Valley Tour

The classic introduction to Gilgit-Baltistan: ancient forts, the turquoise Attabad Lake, apricot orchards, and the Karakoram Highway running north to the Chinese border.

Overview

What a Hunza
Tour Covers

A Hunza tour is the most popular way into Gilgit-Baltistan, and for good reason — the valley packs heritage, scenery, and easy road access into a single, comfortable trip. Most tours base themselves in or around Karimabad, the historic centre of upper Hunza, and use it as a hub for day trips north and south along the Karakoram Highway.

A typical itinerary takes in the restored Baltit and Altit forts, the bright water of Attabad Lake, the Hopper and Hussaini areas, and a run up to Khunjerab Pass on the Chinese frontier — the highest paved border crossing in the world. Spring brings cherry and apricot blossom; autumn brings the famous gold-and-orange foliage. Because the roads here are sealed and the elevations moderate, this is one of GB's most accessible tours and suits families and first-time visitors well.

Itinerary

A Typical
Shape

Getting thereFly Islamabad–Gilgit (weather-dependent) or drive the Karakoram Highway over 1–2 days. Most tours include the road journey.
Days 1–2Arrive Gilgit, transfer to Karimabad. Settle in, explore the bazaar and nearby viewpoints.
Mid-tourForts (Baltit, Altit), Attabad Lake, Hopper Valley or Hussaini, Eagle's Nest viewpoint for sunrise on Rakaposhi.
Northern dayDay trip up to Passu, Gulmit, and on to Khunjerab Pass at the border.
ReturnDrive or fly back to Gilgit/Islamabad. Buffer a day for possible flight cancellations.

Treat this as a general shape, not a fixed plan. Good operators adjust pace, add a rest day, or swap stops based on weather and your interests.

Inclusions

Included &
Excluded

Packages vary, so read the quote line by line. Most Hunza tours include private transport with a driver, accommodation (often twin-share), some or all meals, fuel, and a guide or coordinator. Many bundle the road journey from Islamabad and key entry points.

Commonly excluded: domestic flights (and the cost of a backup if they cancel), personal expenses, tips, travel insurance, and entry fees to forts and sites. Costs swing with group size, vehicle type, hotel standard, and season, so any headline price is only a starting point — get an itemised, current quote before you commit.

Choosing

How to Pick
an Operator

Look for an operator who answers questions clearly, gives a written itinerary with named accommodation, and is upfront about what is and isn't included. Ask how they handle the Islamabad–Gilgit flight if it's cancelled, what vehicle you'll travel in, and whether the guide speaks your language. Local, GB-based operators often know the road and the seasons best.

Be wary of prices that look far below the rest of the market — something is usually being cut. When you're ready, get a vetted quote through our agencies page, or map your own route first with the free trip planner.

FAQ

Common
Questions

How many days do I need for a Hunza tour?

Most Hunza tours run 5 to 8 days. Five days is enough for the core highlights if you fly; allow more time if you travel by road or want to reach Khunjerab Pass at a relaxed pace.

When is the best time to go?

April to October is the main season. Spring brings blossom, summer is warm and green, and autumn (late September–October) brings the famous golden foliage. Winter is quiet, cold, and some high stops may be inaccessible.

What does a Hunza tour usually include?

Typically private transport with a driver, accommodation, some meals, and a guide or coordinator. Flights, entry fees, tips, and insurance are usually extra. Always check the quote line by line.

How much does a Hunza tour cost?

Costs vary widely with group size, season, vehicle, and hotel standard, so any figure is only a broad range. The reliable approach is to get a current, itemised quote from a vetted operator.

Can I do Hunza independently instead?

Yes. The roads are paved and public transport reaches the valley, so independent travel is very doable. A tour mainly saves you logistics and time. You can map a DIY route with our free trip planner.
