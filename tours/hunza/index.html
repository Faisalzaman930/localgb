Hunza Tour Packages 2026: 10 Tours Compared, Day by Day

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026

Hunza is the trip most people picture when they imagine northern Pakistan - Rakaposhi over Karimabad, the old forts, the turquoise of Attabad Lake, and the road to Khunjerab. If you're deciding which Hunza trip to book, this page lays the real options side by side.

I'm from Gilgit-Baltistan, so here's the honest comparison: 10 Hunza tour packages from 5 different operators - cheap road trips from Lahore and Islamabad, flight-based tours, and long overland loops through Chitral and Shandur. I read every itinerary day by day and rewrote it below in plain words, so you can see exactly where each trip spends its time.

How I compared them

Packages, prices and dates were re-checked on each operator's own website on 20 October 2026. Many operators don't publish prices ("on request") - that's normal in Pakistan, and I say so rather than guess. Where an operator says where it's based, I show it.

UK travel advice (FCDO) warns against all travel on the KKH between Mansehra and Chilas. If that applies to you, pick a trip that flies to Gilgit or uses the Babusar road (open roughly late May to October).

  • 5-6 days: Enough for Karimabad, the forts, Attabad and Khunjerab by road - but expect two long driving days. 5-Day Hunza & Khunjerab Pass Trip, Autumn Tour to Hunza ("Luxury"), 6-Day Hunza & Naltar Valley Trip.

Hunza Valley & Khunjerab Pass Tour, Hunza Valley & Attabad Lake Tour, Pakistan Spring Blossom Tour, Hunza Cherry Blossom Tour (fixed date), Hunza & Fairy Meadows Tour.

What's usually included - and what's not

Across these operators, a Hunza package typically includes transport with a driver, hotels, a guide, and at least breakfast; many add lunch and dinner. It usually excludes domestic flights, entry tickets and jeep surcharges (for example the Khunjerab National Park fee or a Deosai 4WD), tips, and personal spending. Ask every operator for the itemised inclusions, the named hotels, and the payment & cancellation policy in writing before you pay.

How to choose

  • Pacing - count the real sightseeing days versus transfer days; a "4-day" trip with four driving days is really a short trip.
  • Meals - breakfast-only vs full board changes the real cost; it's in the table above.
  • Hotels - get the actual hotel names, not "3-star equivalent".
  • Inclusions - jeeps, boats, permits and entries are where cheap quotes cut corners.
  • Payment - confirm the advance, the balance, and the cancellation terms.

FAQ

How much does a Hunza tour package cost in 2026?

Published group trips by road from Lahore or Islamabad start at about Rs 17,500-22,000 for 5-6 days. Guided tours for foreign visitors run around US$850 per person for 9 days, and many operators quote on request. Private honeymoon tours cost far more - see the honeymoon page.

How many days do I need for Hunza?

Five to six days covers the highlights by road; seven or more lets you add Khunjerab and Upper Hunza without rushing.

Should I fly or drive to Hunza?

Flying to Gilgit saves days but is weather-dependent; the road is more dependable but long, and monsoon slides (late July to mid-August) can close it for days. Flying one way and driving the other is the usual best choice.

When is the Hunza cherry blossom?

Roughly late March to mid-April - short and very popular, so book ahead.
