I Reviewed the Naran-Kaghan Tour Packages for 2026

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026

Naran-Kaghan is the most-booked mountain trip in Pakistan - Lake Saif-ul-Malook, Babusar Top, Lulusar and the Kaghan valley, close enough to the cities for a long weekend. It's also the most over-sold, so the operator and the pacing make all the difference.

I pulled the real, current itineraries from 3 operators selling Naran-Kaghan trips in 2026, read them day by day, and ranked 7 packages below - on pacing, what they actually cover, meals and inclusions, and value.

Each package is scored on its itinerary & pacing, value, inclusions (meals, transport, what's covered) and transparency. What's usually included - and what's not

Across these operators, a Naran-Kaghan package typically includes transport with a driver, hotels, a guide, and at least breakfast; many add lunch and dinner. It usually excludes domestic flights, entry tickets and jeep surcharges (for example the Khunjerab permit or a Deosai 4WD), tips, and personal spending. Ask every operator for the itemised inclusions, the named hotels, and the payment & cancellation policy in writing before you pay.

How to choose

  • Pacing - count the real sightseeing days versus transfer days; a "8-day" trip with four driving days is really a short trip.
  • Meals - breakfast-only vs full board changes the real cost; it's in the table above.
  • Hotels - get the actual hotel names, not "3-star equivalent".
  • Inclusions - jeeps, boats, permits and entries are where cheap quotes cut corners.
  • Payment - confirm the advance, the balance, and the cancellation terms.

FAQ

How much is a Naran tour package in 2026?

From around Rs 7,000 for a 2-day trip to Rs 25,000+ for 5-day routes adding Babusar and Lulusar; see the table for current per-package prices.

Is the Saif-ul-Malook jeep included?

Often separate - confirm it's bundled, as the last stretch needs a 4WD.

When is Babusar Pass open?

Roughly late June to September; the lower Kaghan valley opens from May.

Is Naran good for families?

Yes - it's the most family-friendly mountain trip in Pakistan; a private vehicle beats a group coaster for comfort.
