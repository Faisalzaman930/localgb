Naran Kaghan Tour Packages 2026: Prices and Tours Compared

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026

Naran-Kaghan is the most-booked mountain trip in Pakistan - Lake Saif-ul-Malook, Babusar Top, Lulusar and the Kaghan valley, close enough to the cities for a long weekend. It's also the most over-sold, so the operator and the pacing make all the difference.

I'm from Gilgit-Baltistan, so here's the honest comparison: 4 Naran Kaghan tour packages from 3 different operators - budget 3-day group trips from Islamabad and Lahore, a 4-day trip with Shogran, a slow 7-day tour, and couples' packages. I read every itinerary day by day and rewrote it below in plain words, so you can see exactly where each trip spends its time.

How I compared them

Packages, prices and dates were re-checked on each operator's own website on 21 October 2026. Many operators don't publish prices ("on request") - that's normal in Pakistan, and I say so rather than guess. Where an operator says where it's based, I show it.

Naran Kaghan tours from Islamabad, Lahore and Karachi

  • From Islamabad or Rawalpindi: Naran is 6-9 hours by road, so most tours start here and a 3-day trip works well.
  • From Lahore: most group trips pick up in Lahore as well; Lahore to Islamabad adds about 4-5 hours on the M2, and buses usually get back to Lahore around midnight on the last day.
  • From Faisalabad, Sialkot, Gujranwala or Multan: join a Lahore or Islamabad departure - ask the operator which pick-up points they use.
  • From Karachi: fly to Islamabad and join a trip from there.

Is Naran Kaghan open for tourists?

  • Main season: roughly May-June to October. June and September are the best months; July and August are the most crowded.
  • Babusar Pass: open roughly late May or June to October or November, depending on snow, and only in daylight (gates roughly 7am-6pm).
  • Autumn and winter: from October guesthouses in Naran start to close; the lower valley (Balakot, Kiwai and Shogran) stays open year-round.
  • Monsoon: floods closed Babusar and the Kaghan road for days in late July to mid-August in both 2025 and 2026 - keep a spare day.

How many days do you need for a Naran Kaghan tour?

  • 3-4 days: Shogran and Siri Paye, Lake Saif-ul-Malook, and Lulusar and Babusar Top - the classic weekend loop. 3-Day Shogran, Siri Paye, Naran & Saif-ul-Malook Trip, Naran, Kaghan & Shogran 4-Day Tour (Standard), 3-Day Honeymoon Tour to Naran & Kaghan.
  • 7 days: The same highlights at an easy pace, with a full day for each excursion. Naran & Kaghan Tour.

Three days is enough for the highlights from Islamabad. In July and August, add a day - traffic in the valley can triple driving times.

What's usually included - and what's not

Across these operators, a Naran-Kaghan package typically includes transport with a driver, hotels, a guide, and at least breakfast; many add lunch and dinner. It usually excludes domestic flights, entry tickets and jeep surcharges (for example the Khunjerab National Park fee or a Deosai 4WD), tips, and personal spending. Ask every operator for the itemised inclusions, the named hotels, and the payment & cancellation policy in writing before you pay.

How to choose

  • Pacing - count the real sightseeing days versus transfer days; a "8-day" trip with four driving days is really a short trip.
  • Meals - breakfast-only vs full board changes the real cost; it's in the table above.
  • Hotels - get the actual hotel names, not "3-star equivalent".
  • Inclusions - jeeps, boats, permits and entries are where cheap quotes cut corners.
  • Payment - confirm the advance, the balance, and the cancellation terms.

FAQ

How much is a Naran tour package in 2026?

Published 3-day group trips from Islamabad or Lahore start at about Rs 11,500 per person. Couples' packages are about Rs 40,000-60,000. Going independently, the Saif-ul-Malook jeep is about PKR 5,500 return and hotels run PKR 2,000-18,000 a night.

Is the Saif-ul-Malook jeep included?

Often separate - confirm it's bundled, as the last stretch needs a 4WD.

When is Babusar Pass open?

Roughly late May or June to October or November, depending on snow (it opened around 25-27 May in 2026), daylight hours only; the lower valley (Balakot to Shogran) stays open year-round.

Is Naran good for families?

Yes - it's the most family-friendly mountain trip in Pakistan; a private vehicle beats a group coaster for comfort.
