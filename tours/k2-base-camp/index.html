K2 Base Camp Trek

The walk up the Baltoro Glacier to the foot of the world's second-highest mountain is one of trekking's great journeys — and a serious, fully supported expedition that demands real preparation.

🗓 14–21 days typical
📅 Jun–Aug best season
🛫 Skardu (via Askole) start
⛰ ~5,150m at Concordia

Overview

What the K2 BC
Trek Covers

The K2 Base Camp trek follows the Baltoro Glacier through the heart of the Karakoram, passing the Trango Towers and Masherbrum before reaching Concordia — the junction where several giant peaks meet — and continuing to base camp at the foot of K2. It is remote, high, and physically demanding, and is almost always done as a fully supported expedition with porters, cooks, and a guide.

This is not a casual booking. Trekkers need good fitness, time to acclimatise, and an operator who handles permits, porter logistics, and camp supply over a route with no road, no shops, and no quick exit. The reward is a close-up view of some of the most dramatic mountain scenery on Earth.

For the route itself, distances, and stages, see our detailed K2 Base Camp trek guide.
Itinerary

A Typical
Shape

Getting thereReach Skardu by air or road, then a long jeep transfer to Askole, the last village and the trailhead.
AcclimatiseBuilt-in rest and acclimatisation days in Skardu and on the trail are essential at this altitude.
The trekSeveral days up the Baltoro Glacier via Paiju, Urdukas, and Goro to Concordia, then on to K2 Base Camp.
ReturnRetrace the route, or for experienced groups exit over the high Gondogoro La pass (more demanding, weather-dependent).
BufferSpare days for weather, flight cancellations, and altitude are standard, not optional.

Treat this as a general framework. The exact stages, pace, and any high-pass option are set by the operator according to conditions and your group's experience.

Inclusions

Included &
Excluded

A supported K2 Base Camp expedition usually includes guides, porters, cooks, camping equipment and tents, all trail meals, permits and fees, and transfers between Skardu and the trailhead. The logistics here are heavy, which is reflected in the cost.

Commonly excluded: international and domestic flights, personal trekking gear and sleeping bag, travel and evacuation insurance (strongly recommended at this altitude), tips for the crew, and contingency costs if weather forces extra days. Because so much hinges on group size, season, and crew ratios, costs span a wide range — get a detailed, current quote rather than relying on any headline figure.

Choosing

How to Pick
an Operator

For a high-altitude expedition, operator quality matters more than price. Ask about guide experience and qualifications, porter welfare and load limits, the safety and evacuation plan, and how acclimatisation is built into the schedule. A serious operator will be candid about the difficulty and about who should not attempt this trek.

Confirm what insurance you need and whether helicopter evacuation cover is required. Compare detailed quotes carefully. When you're ready, get a vetted quote through our agencies page; if you're still weighing the wider trip, sketch it with the free trip planner.

FAQ

Common
Questions

How long is the K2 Base Camp trek?

A full expedition typically runs 14 to 21 days, including travel to and from Skardu, acclimatisation, the trek up the Baltoro Glacier, and buffer days for weather and altitude.

When is the best season?

The main season is roughly June to August, when the high glacier route is most stable. Outside this window, snow, cold, and access make the trek far riskier.

How fit do I need to be?

Very fit. This is a strenuous, high-altitude trek with long days and camping in remote conditions. Good cardiovascular fitness, prior multi-day trekking experience, and careful acclimatisation are all important.

What does a supported K2 BC trek include?

Usually guides, porters, cooks, tents and camping gear, trail meals, permits, and Skardu–trailhead transfers. Flights, personal gear, insurance, and tips are normally extra. Check each quote in detail.

How much does the trek cost?

Costs vary widely with group size, season, crew ratios, and the exact route, so any figure is only a broad range. Get a detailed, current quote from a vetted expedition operator.
