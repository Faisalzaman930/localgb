Route Guide

Lahore to Skardu

Most trips from Lahore to Skardu route through Islamabad, then either fly or take the long road north. Here is how the journey actually breaks down, and how to plan around the weather.

The Route

Lahore
to Skardu

PIA lists a nonstop Lahore-Skardu flight (frequency unconfirmed, so check the current schedule) and through coaches run from Lahore, but most options add a Lahore-Islamabad leg onto the Islamabad-Skardu journey, so it helps to think of the trip in two parts. The first leg, Lahore to Islamabad, is straightforward: the M-2 motorway covers it in roughly four to five hours by car or coach, with frequent daily departures in both directions.

The second leg is the one that defines the whole trip. From Islamabad you either fly to Skardu (airport code KDU) on a flight of about an hour, or you travel by road for roughly sixteen to eighteen hours via the Karakoram Highway and the Skardu road. The flight is by far the fastest option, but it is heavily weather-dependent and is cancelled frequently, sometimes for several days in a row when cloud closes the valley.

The easiest plan for most travellers is simple: get yourself to Islamabad first, then fly to Skardu - and keep buffer days in your schedule for cancellations. If the flight does not operate, the road is always there as a fallback, but it is a long, full day of mountain driving. Before you book anything, check live conditions on our road and flight status page and the Gilgit-Baltistan weather guide.

Plan around the weather: Treat the flight to Skardu as a hope, not a guarantee. Build at least one or two spare days into your itinerary so a cancelled flight does not unravel the rest of your trip.

Planning

Breaks,
Hazards & Timing

If you are flying, Islamabad is the natural place to break the journey - arrive from Lahore, spend a night, and take a morning Skardu flight when visibility is best. If you end up driving the northern leg, the long road is usually split with a stop at Chilas or Gilgit rather than attempting it in one unbroken push.

Season matters more than distance here. The road leg crosses high, exposed terrain on the Karakoram Highway, where landslides and rockfall can close sections at short notice, and winter snow adds further delay. In late July-mid August 2025 and 2026, floods closed the KKH and the Jaglot-Skardu road for days, and foreigners following UK advice should avoid the KKH between Mansehra and Chilas (the FCDO advises against all travel there). Flights, meanwhile, depend entirely on clear weather over the mountains. Late spring through early autumn generally offers the most reliable conditions for both road and air.

Honest cancellation note: the Skardu flight has a long-standing reputation for being grounded by weather, sometimes for days at a stretch. Do not pin a tight, non-refundable itinerary to it. FAQ

Common
Questions

Is there a direct flight from Lahore to Skardu?

Sometimes. PIA lists a nonstop Lahore-Skardu flight (October 2026), but its frequency is unconfirmed and it can be seasonal. Most travellers go from Lahore to Islamabad first, then take one of the more frequent Islamabad-Skardu flights or the road. Confirm current routings with the airline.

How long does Lahore to Skardu take?

The Lahore-Islamabad motorway leg is about 4-5 hours. From Islamabad, the Skardu flight is roughly 1 hour, while the road takes around 16-18 hours. Most people allow at least a full day, plus buffer days for flight cancellations.

What is the best way to get to Skardu from Lahore?

The easiest approach is to reach Islamabad first, then fly to Skardu (airport code KDU). Keep one or two spare days in your plan in case the flight is cancelled.

Why do Skardu flights get cancelled so often?

The Skardu flight is weather-dependent and is frequently grounded when cloud closes the mountain valley, sometimes for several days. Always treat it as weather-dependent and keep the road as a fallback.

How much does the trip cost?

As an approximate guide, the Lahore-Islamabad leg runs around Rs.2,000-4,000, the Islamabad-Skardu flight roughly Rs.18,000-30,000 one way (2026, higher in peak season), and the road leg by coach around Rs.5,500-6,200 (a through coach from Lahore is roughly Rs.7,900). These ranges change often - confirm current prices before booking.
