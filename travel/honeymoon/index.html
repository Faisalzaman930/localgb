Honeymoon in Northern Pakistan: Hunza, Skardu or Naran?

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026

I'm from Gilgit-Baltistan, and couples ask me the same question every season: where should we go for our honeymoon? There are three real answers - Hunza, Skardu and Naran - and they suit very different couples. Here's how I'd choose.

Which one should you choose?

  • Choose Hunza if you want the classic: a room facing Rakaposhi, old forts, apricot blossom or golden poplars, and cosy cafes in Karimabad. Honeymoon in Hunza →
  • Choose Skardu if you want the most special hotels - a restored fort in Shigar, a palace in Khaplu, lakeside resorts - and you can fly. Honeymoon in Skardu →
  • Choose Naran if you only have 3-4 days or don't want to fly: Lake Saif-ul-Malook, Shogran's forests and Babusar are a day's drive from Islamabad. Honeymoon in Naran →

Can we combine two?

Yes - in summer (roughly late May to October) the Babusar road links Naran to Chilas, so you can do Naran and Hunza in one trip of about 8-9 days without coming back to Islamabad. Skardu and Hunza combine well too (Gilgit to Skardu is about 4-5 hours by road), but you'll want 10 days or more.

For adventurous couples: Fairy Meadows

If your idea of romance is a wooden cottage facing Nanga Parbat, Fairy Meadows is unforgettable - but it's rustic. You'll take a rough jeep track and hike 2-3 hours to reach it, rooms are simple (about Rs 3,500-10,000 a night in 2026) and there's no luxury option. Add it to a Hunza trip rather than making it the whole honeymoon.

When to go

  • Spring blossom (late March to mid-April) - Hunza and Skardu at their most romantic.
  • Summer (June to August) - everything is open, including Deosai and Babusar; late July to mid-August brings monsoon landslides, so keep spare days.
  • Autumn (late September to October) - golden colours, clear skies, fewer people. My favourite for couples.
  • Winter - quiet and snowy; many hotels close, and Naran's high roads shut.

What does a honeymoon in northern Pakistan cost?

Published honeymoon packages run from about Rs 40,000 for a 2-day Shogran break to Rs 120,000-190,000 for 5-8 days in Hunza or Skardu with a private car. If you plan it yourselves, a private car and driver costs about PKR 8,000-15,000 a day in Hunza, and a mid-range to luxury hotel is the biggest part of the budget - see each destination guide for real hotel options.

Tips for couples travelling in Gilgit-Baltistan

  • Carry your CNICs or passports - hotels ask for ID at check-in, and so do checkposts on the way.
  • It's a conservative, friendly region: dress modestly and keep public affection low-key. In your hotel and on a private jeep, you'll have all the privacy you want.
  • Book the view room early for blossom (late March to mid-April) and autumn (late September to October) - the best rooms go first.
  • Carry enough cash; ATMs are limited outside the main towns.
  • Keep one spare day for weather, especially if you're flying.

FAQ

Which is better for a honeymoon: Hunza or Skardu?

Hunza is better for classic mountain views, cosy heritage stays and blossom or autumn colours. Skardu is better for special hotels - a restored fort at Shigar, a palace at Khaplu, lakeside resorts - and wide open landscapes. If you can fly, either works; Hunza is the easier first choice.

What is the best month for a honeymoon in northern Pakistan?

Late March to mid-April for blossom, or late September to October for autumn colours and clear skies. June and September are best for Naran.

How many days do we need for a honeymoon in Hunza or Skardu?

About 5-7 days for Hunza and 6-8 for Skardu. Naran works in 3-4 days.

How much does a honeymoon package in northern Pakistan cost?

Published packages run from about Rs 40,000 for a 2-day Shogran break to Rs 120,000-190,000 for 5-8 days in Hunza or Skardu with a private car. Confirm what's included and the hotel names before paying.
