Skardu Flights

A one-hour flight from Islamabad over some of the highest mountains on earth - when the weather lets it fly. Here's how the route really works.

🛫 KDU airport code
📍 from Islamabad
⏱ ~1 hour flight
☁ Weather-dependent
Home/Travel/Skardu Flights
By Faisal Zaman·Local from Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026
Overview

The Honest
Picture

Skardu Airport (code KDU) is served by flights from Islamabad - operated by PIA (privately owned since June 2026), Airblue and AirSial, all on A320 jets - taking roughly an hour in the air. It is widely regarded as one of the most scenic flights in the world - the route threads between Nanga Parbat and the wall of the Karakoram, and on a clear morning the views are genuinely unforgettable. That is the good news, and it is worth flying for.

The honest news is that this is a difficult route to rely on. Flights are weather-dependent and approach Skardu through a narrow mountain valley with no instrument landing margin, so they are frequently cancelled or delayed when cloud closes in - and that can happen on otherwise sunny days. Cancellations cluster across consecutive days, and seats are often over-booked, so a confirmed ticket is not the same as a confirmed journey. Treat the flight as a bonus rather than a fixed plan.

Always have a road backup. The overland route from Islamabad to Skardu via the Karakoram Highway and the Skardu Road takes roughly 16-18 hours. If your trip can't absorb a multi-day weather delay, build the drive into your plan from the start. Foreigners should note the UK FCDO advises against all travel on the KKH between Mansehra and Chilas, so check your own government's advice (the seasonal Babusar road avoids that stretch).

Booking

Flying It
Smartly

Book as early as you can. The Skardu route fills quickly, particularly in the May-October travel season, and last-minute seats are scarce. Morning flights tend to have the best weather window, since cloud often builds over the mountains as the day warms up - an early slot gives you the best odds of actually getting off the ground.

Build buffer days into your itinerary at both ends. If you must connect to an international flight out of Islamabad, never plan to fly out of Skardu on the same day; give yourself at least a day of slack, ideally two. When a flight is cancelled, head to the airline counter or your booking agent promptly to be rebooked - seats on the next operating flight go fast, and over-booking means the calmest, quickest travellers tend to get away first. Keep an eye on conditions and have the road option ready so a string of cancelled days doesn't strand your whole trip.

The flight is at its most reliable in the clearer stretches of spring and autumn, and least reliable through the deep winter and monsoon-affected periods. When it does fly, take a window seat: the line over Nanga Parbat and into the Karakoram is the reason this route has the reputation it does, and on a clear day it is worth every bit of the uncertainty getting there.

FAQ

Common
Questions

Which airline flies to Skardu and how long does it take?

PIA, Airblue and AirSial run scheduled services between Islamabad and Skardu (KDU), all on A320 jets. The flight takes about an hour in the air.

How often do flights to Skardu run?

Between them the three airlines schedule roughly 13-21 flights a week (fewer in winter), but actual operation depends entirely on the weather. Always confirm the current timetable when you book, and don't assume a daily flight means a guaranteed one.

Why are Skardu flights cancelled so often?

Skardu sits in a narrow mountain valley and the approach needs clear visibility, so flights are cancelled or delayed whenever cloud closes in. Cancellations often run across several consecutive days.

How much does a Skardu flight cost?

Fares vary widely with season and demand, often around Rs.18,000-30,000 one-way in 2026 and more in peak season. Confirm the current price at the time of booking.

What's the backup if my flight is cancelled?

The road journey from Islamabad to Skardu via the Karakoram Highway takes roughly 16-18 hours. It is slower but far more dependable, so build it into your plan in case the flight doesn't operate. In late July-mid August floods can close the road for days too (as in 2025 and 2026), so add buffer days then.
