Sample Itinerary

10 Days in GB:
The Best First Visit

This route covers the highlights without rushing. It's doable on a mid-range budget of Rs. 15,000-22,000/day total (2026 prices).

1-2

Arrive Islamabad → Fly to Gilgit

Morning flight to Gilgit (about 50-60 min, weather permitting - cancellations are common). Check into a guesthouse in Gilgit city. Visit the old bazaar, try local food. Day trip to Kargah Buddha carvings (free). Early night.

Flight: Rs. 19,000-28,000
3-4

Gilgit → Hunza (Karimabad)

NATCO bus or shared jeep (2-3.5h, fares vary - confirm locally). Settle in Karimabad. Afternoon: Baltit Fort. Sunset from the rooftop. Day 4: Eagle's Nest sunrise hike (2h return, free).

2 nights: Rs. 4,000-13,000
5

Attabad Lake & Passu Day Trip

Hire a jeep or car for the day from Karimabad (around Rs. 8,000-15,000, split between 4 people). Attabad Lake boat ride (paid per ride, confirm the rate), lunch at Passu, walk to base of Passu Cones. Return by evening.

Total: Rs. 3,000-5,000 each
6

Hunza → Raikot Bridge

Bus south past Gilgit to the Raikot Bridge junction (allow most of the morning). Register with the police at Raikot, then take a union jeep (fixed fares, no haggling) up to Tato village (rough 4×4, ~1.75-2h), then it's a steep ~5 km walk uphill (2-3h) to Fairy Meadows. Camp or stay in a hut. Clear night = incredible stargazing.

Jeep: Rs. 8,000+ per jeep one way, plus your room
7

Nanga Parbat Base Camp Hike

Full day hike to Raikot Base Camp and back (4-5h total). Start early for morning light on the face. No guide needed, trail is clear. Picnic lunch at the base camp moraine.

Cost: small park fee if charged (confirm locally)
8

Fairy Meadows → Skardu

Walk down to Tato, jeep to Raikot Bridge, then drive to Skardu via Gilgit and the Jaglot-Skardu road. It's a long day - plan a near-full travel day rather than a quick hop (in 2026 the Jaglot-Skardu road still has flood-damaged, one-lane sections, so allow 1-2h extra). Check into a hotel in Skardu. Evening walk along the Indus river; Skardu Bazaar for local shopping.

Bus/van: fares vary - confirm locally
9

Deosai Plains Day Trip

Hire jeep from Skardu to Deosai Plains and back (Rs. 15,000-30,000 for the day). Bara Pani, Himalayan brown bear habitat (Sheosar Lake is much further in, on the Astore side). Pay the Deosai park entry fee at the gate (rates vary - confirm the current rate locally). Deosai is summer-only, roughly mid-June to late September/early October.

Jeep hire: Rs. 15,000-30,000
10

Skardu → Islamabad (Fly)

Morning flight back to Islamabad (Rs. 18,000-30,000, higher in peak season). Or take the Skardu-Gilgit road and fly from Gilgit. Build in a one-day buffer - Skardu flights cancel often.

Flight: Rs. 18,000-30,000
Permits & Regulations

NOC Permits:
What You Need

Updated October 2026: Rules change. Always verify current requirements with the DC office in Gilgit or the Ministry of Tourism before departing for restricted zones.
No permit neededKarimabad, Attabad Lake, Khunjerab Pass, Fairy Meadows, Gilgit, Skardu, Naran, Chilas, Naltar, Rakaposhi BC, Passu. Just CNIC/passport.
Deosai National ParkEntry fee payable at the park gate; no advance booking needed. Rates differ for Pakistanis and foreigners and change often - confirm the current rate locally before you go. Open summer only (roughly mid-June to late Sept/early Oct).
NOC requiredShimshal passes, Chapursan beyond Baba Ghundi, and areas near the Afghan border (check Karambar Lake and Darkot Pass locally). Arrange through a licensed operator or the DC office (Gilgit/Hunza) and allow several weeks.
Trekking permitRequired for K2, Gondogoro La, Hispar Pass, Biafo Glacier, Spantik. Apply through a licensed Pakistani operator. Allow 4 weeks to 2 months. Fees roughly $50-$200 USD plus park fees - confirm with your operator.
ForeignersAll foreign tourists need to report to the police/FC checkpost at their first destination. They will enter your passport details. Standard procedure, not a red flag - carry several passport and visa copies. You also need a paid e-visa in advance (the free visa prior to arrival was suspended in January 2026).
Khunjerab PassPakistani CNIC holders can enter freely. Foreign passport holders: entry allowed, but you cannot cross into China without a Chinese visa obtained in advance. The Sost border opens Monday-Friday only.
Getting There

Reaching
Gilgit-Baltistan

Fastest · ~50-60 min

Fly to Gilgit (GIL)

PIA flies Islamabad-Gilgit on ATR turboprops (verify the current operator before booking). Rs. 19,000-28,000 one way (2026). These flights fly visual rules only and cancel frequently for cloud or wind - never book tight connections. Best April-October.

Most scenic · ~1h

Fly to Skardu (KDU)

Direct flight from Islamabad to Skardu (around an hour), flown by PIA, Airblue and AirSial on jets - verify current operators before booking. Rs. 18,000-30,000 one way (2026). Slightly more reliable than Gilgit flights but still weather-dependent. Stunning approach over the Karakoram. Book weeks ahead in peak season.

Epic · 14-18 hours by bus

Karakoram Highway

The KKH from Islamabad via Abbottabad, Mansehra, Besham, Chilas to Gilgit. NATCO bus Rs. 3,800-6,000 (2026). Break the journey at Besham or Chilas. All-year route, but late July-mid August brought multi-day closures in 2025 and 2026. The UK FCDO advises against all travel on the Mansehra-Chilas stretch, so many foreigners come via Naran-Babusar (open roughly late May/June to Oct/Nov) or fly.
