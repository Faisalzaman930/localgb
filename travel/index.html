Sample Itinerary

10 Days in GB:
The Best First Visit

This route covers the highlights without rushing. It's doable on a mid-range budget of Rs. 7,000-10,000/day total.

1-2

Arrive Islamabad → Fly to Gilgit

Morning flight to Gilgit (about 50-60 min, weather permitting - cancellations are common). Check into a guesthouse in Gilgit city. Visit the old bazaar, try local food. Day trip to Kargah Buddha carvings (free). Early night.

Flight: Rs. 8,000-15,000
3-4

Gilgit → Hunza (Karimabad)

NATCO bus or shared jeep (3-4h, Rs. 400-600). Settle in Karimabad. Afternoon: Baltit Fort. Sunset from the rooftop. Day 4: Eagle's Nest sunrise hike (2h return, free).

2 nights: Rs. 3,000-8,000
5

Attabad Lake & Passu Day Trip

Hire a shared jeep from Karimabad (Rs. 1,500-2,000 for 4 people). Attabad Lake boat ride (Rs. 500), lunch at Passu, walk to base of Passu Cones. Return by evening.

Total: Rs. 2,000-4,000
6

Hunza → Raikot Bridge

Bus south past Gilgit to the Raikot Bridge junction (allow most of the morning). Negotiate a jeep up to Tato village (rough 4×4, ~1.5-2.5h), then it's a steep ~5 km walk uphill (3-4h) to Fairy Meadows. Camp or stay in a hut. Clear night = incredible stargazing.

Jeep + camp: Rs. 4,000-6,000
7

Nanga Parbat Base Camp Hike

Full day hike to Raikot Base Camp and back (4-5h total). Start early for morning light on the face. No guide needed, trail is clear. Picnic lunch at the base camp moraine.

Cost: Rs. 500 (park entry)
8

Fairy Meadows → Skardu

Walk down to Tato, jeep to Raikot Bridge, then drive to Skardu via Gilgit and the Jaglot-Skardu road. It's a long day - plan a near-full travel day rather than a quick hop. Check into a hotel in Skardu. Evening walk along the Indus river; Skardu Bazaar for local shopping.

Bus: Rs. 600-800
9

Deosai Plains Day Trip

Hire jeep from Skardu to Deosai Plains and back (Rs. 6,000-8,000 for full day). Sheosar Lake, Bara Pani, Himalayan brown bear habitat. Pay the Deosai park entry fee at the gate (rates vary - confirm the current rate locally). Deosai is summer-only, roughly mid/late June to late September.

Jeep hire: Rs. 6,000-8,000
10

Skardu → Islamabad (Fly)

Morning flight back to Islamabad (Rs. 10,000-18,000). Or take the Skardu-Gilgit road and fly from Gilgit. Build in a one-day buffer - Skardu flights cancel often.

Flight: Rs. 10,000-18,000
Permits & Regulations

NOC Permits:
What You Need

Updated May 2026: Rules change. Always verify current requirements with the DC office in Gilgit or the Ministry of Tourism before departing for restricted zones.
No permit neededKarimabad, Attabad Lake, Khunjerab Pass, Fairy Meadows, Gilgit, Skardu, Naran, Chilas, Naltar, Rakaposhi BC, Passu. Just CNIC/passport.
Deosai National ParkEntry fee payable at the park gate; no advance booking needed. Rates differ for Pakistanis and foreigners and change often - confirm the current rate locally before you go. Open summer only (roughly mid/late June-late Sept).
NOC requiredShimshal Valley, Chapursan Valley, Karambar Lake, Darkot Pass. Apply at DC Gilgit or DC Gojal (Gulmit). 3-5 days processing. Free for Pakistanis.
Trekking permitRequired for K2, Gondogoro La, Hispar Pass, Biafo Glacier, Spantik. Apply via Ministry of Tourism, Islamabad. Allow 2 weeks minimum. Fees vary by route ($50-$200 USD).
ForeignersAll foreign tourists need to report to the police/FC checkpost at their first destination. They will enter your passport details. Standard procedure, not a red flag.
Khunjerab PassPakistani CNIC holders can enter freely. Foreign passport holders: entry allowed, but you cannot cross into China without a Chinese visa and bilateral travel permit.
Getting There

Reaching
Gilgit-Baltistan

Fastest · ~50-60 min

Fly to Gilgit (GIL)

PIA flies Islamabad-Gilgit on ATR turboprops (verify the current operator before booking). Rs. 8,000-15,000 one way. These flights fly visual rules only and cancel frequently for cloud or wind - never book tight connections. Best April-October.

Most scenic · ~1h

Fly to Skardu (KDU)

Direct flight from Islamabad to Skardu (around an hour), flown by PIA, Airblue and AirSial on jets - verify current operators before booking. Rs. 10,000-18,000. Slightly more reliable than Gilgit flights but still weather-dependent. Stunning approach over the Karakoram. Book weeks ahead in peak season.

Epic · 16-20 hours

Karakoram Highway

The KKH from Islamabad via Abbottabad, Mansehra, Besham, Chilas to Gilgit. NATCO bus Rs. 1,200-1,800. Break the journey at Besham or Chilas. All-year route (delays in landslide season Jun-Aug).
