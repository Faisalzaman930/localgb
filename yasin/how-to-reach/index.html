Transport Guide

How to Reach
Yasin Valley

Routes, fares, journey times, and road conditions for getting to one of GB's most remote destinations.

📍 ~150km from Gilgit
🚗 5-6h total journey
💰 Rs.700-900 shared
Home / Yasin Valley / How to Reach
By Faisal Zaman·Local from Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026
Route Overview

The Road
to Yasin

Key waypoints: Gilgit → Gahkuch (~72km, 2h on the paved Gilgit-Shandur road) → Gupis (~45km more, 1-1.5h) → Yasin town (~30km more, about 1h). The road is paved throughout but narrows and roughens beyond Gupis. A standard sedan can reach Yasin town in dry weather - in wet weather a 4WD is safer.
Total distance~150km from Gilgit to Yasin town
Total time (shared)5-6h including transfer at Gupis
Total cost (shared)Rs.700-900 (NATCO + Suzuki; pre-2026 fares, confirm locally)
Total time (private jeep)4-5h direct
Private jeep costRs.12,000-18,000 return (confirm the 2026 rate)
All Options

Transport
Options

Option 1: Shared Transport (Budget)

Step 1NATCO bus from Gilgit General Bus Stand → Gupis. Daily departure approximately 7-8am. Fare Rs.400 (confirm). Journey 3-4h. The bus leaves Gilgit westward on the Gilgit-Shandur road through Gahkuch.
Step 2From Gupis bazaar, take a shared Suzuki pickup or van to Yasin. These depart when full - usually 1-2 per day. Fare Rs.300-500 (confirm). Journey about 1h on a rougher road through the Ghizer gorge then into the Yasin valley.
Timing adviceArrive at Gupis bazaar by 9-10am to find a vehicle to Yasin. Vehicles are unreliable in the afternoon. If no vehicle, stay the night in Gupis (basic guesthouses Rs.600-1,000) and depart next morning.
Total costRs.700-900 for the complete journey Gilgit → Yasin town.

Option 2: Private Jeep from Gilgit

CostRs.12,000-18,000 return including driver and fuel. Negotiate beforehand - agree on return date and any side trips.
AdvantageTravel on your schedule, stop anywhere, go directly without transfers. Can reach upper Yasin valley beyond Yasin town.
How to arrangeAsk at your Gilgit hotel or contact local transport agencies. Drivers familiar with the Yasin route are available in Gilgit.

Option 3: Via Chitral (Darkot Pass - Summer Trek Only)

RouteFrom Chitral, cross Darkot Pass (4,702m) into upper Yasin Valley. A 3-day trek, not a road route.
SeasonJune-September only. Pass is snowbound outside these months.
RequirementsExperienced trekker, local guide (agree a written rate in advance), full camping equipment, 3-4 days. The pass is close to the Afghan border - check current access rules with police first.
PracticalVery few travellers take this route. Historically used for trade and military movement. Outstanding scenery but logistically demanding.

Option 4: Fly to Gilgit then Road

FlightPIA: Islamabad → Gilgit (ATR 42 turboprop, ~1h, about Rs.19,000-28,000 one way in 2026, higher in peak season). Book the morning flight - afternoon flights cancel more often. These are Visual-Flight-Rules flights that cancel readily in cloud or wind, so always build in one buffer day.
ThenFrom Gilgit airport or city, follow Option 1 or 2 above to reach Yasin.
From Islamabad by road: It is also possible to drive from Islamabad via the KKH (~520-560km to Gilgit, 12-16h) then continue to Yasin. Break the journey overnight at Besham or Chilas. Note the UK FCDO advises against all travel on the KKH between Mansehra and Chilas.
Road Details

Road Conditions
& Notes

Gilgit → Gahkuch~72km on the Gilgit-Shandur road. Paved, generally good. Views of Ghizar River gorge.
Gahkuch → Gupis~45km on the Ghizer road. Paved but narrower. Some sections close to cliff edge - take care on bends.
Gupis → Yasin town30km. Partly paved, partly rough. Passable by sedan in dry conditions. After rain, 4WD advisable.
Beyond Yasin townRoad becomes increasingly rough toward Darkot and the upper valley. 4WD or jeep required. Some sections may be impassable after heavy rain or early/late in season.
Winter closureThe Gupis-Yasin road can be blocked by snow November through March. Check conditions locally before attempting in shoulder months.
LandslidesThe road between Gahkuch and Gupis is prone to landslides in spring snowmelt (April-May). Allow extra time and check locally for recent blockages.
