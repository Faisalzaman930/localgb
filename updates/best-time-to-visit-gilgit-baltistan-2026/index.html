Best Time to Visit Gilgit-Baltistan in 2026 (Month by Month)

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·15 June 2026

Timing makes or breaks a GB trip. Here's how the 2026 season breaks down.

  • April-May: spring. Hunza's cherry blossom (late March to mid-April) gives way to green valleys. Deosai and the high passes are still under snow.
  • June-August: peak season. Everything open, the high passes through, warm days. Busiest and best for the full circuit.
  • September-October: autumn. Thinning crowds and Hunza's famous gold-and-crimson foliage (peak around mid-October). Deosai and Babusar may close late in the window.
  • November-March: winter. Cold and quiet, Khunjerab closed, access mostly by the weather-dependent flights. A trip for snow and frozen lakes, not the circuit.

For the full circuit (Hunza plus Skardu plus Deosai), aim for July to mid-September. See Gilgit-Baltistan tour packages and the live road status.
