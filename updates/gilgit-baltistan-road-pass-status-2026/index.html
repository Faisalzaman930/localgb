Gilgit-Baltistan Road & Pass Status: 2026 Season Update

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·28 June 2026·Updated October 2026

This is the live-season snapshot for Gilgit-Baltistan's main roads and passes. Conditions change with weather, so always check before you travel.

Updated October 2026: the 2026 season has been rough on the roads. Late July floods cut the KKH at several points, the Jaglot-Skardu road for several days and valley roads to Astore, Naltar and Hisper; the KKH closed again near Chilas in early August. Main routes have reopened, but the Skardu road still has damaged sections. With autumn here, Deosai's season is ending and Babusar can close with snow from late October. (Our original 28 June snapshot had Babusar, Deosai and Khunjerab all open.)

Pass by pass

  • Karakoram Highway (KKH): open year-round, but not immune. In late July-mid August 2025 and 2026, floods and landslides closed it for days (Gini near Chilas, Hassanabad and Gulmit are repeat trouble spots), and protests blocked it at Chilas in April and June 2026. The UK FCDO advises against all travel on the Mansehra-Chilas section.
  • Babusar Pass (4,173m): the Naran-to-Chilas shortcut. Opened 25-27 May 2026 (one-way at first), shut again for snow around 4-6 June, then stayed open through the summer. Daylight travel only. Usually closes with snow in late October-November (in 2025 snow shut it from ~6 November and it formally closed 28-29 November).
  • Khunjerab Pass (4,693m): the China border. Officially open year-round for trade since December 2024, but the practical tourist window is May to October and the Sost border runs Monday-Friday only. Landslides closed the Sost-Khunjerab road for a time in early September 2026.
  • Deosai Plateau: the jeep track from Skardu/Astore is seasonal, roughly mid-June to late September or early October, so by mid-October the season is over or ending - confirm before going. July 2026 floods damaged part of the Deosai road.
  • Skardu & Astore roads: open year-round in principle, but the July 2026 floods cut both (the Jaglot-Skardu road reopened to light traffic on 28 July). As of September 2026 the Skardu road still has a diversion, a one-lane stretch and a Bailey bridge in its first 25 km, so allow 1-2 hours extra.
