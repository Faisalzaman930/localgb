Hunza Autumn Foliage 2026: When the Valley Turns Gold

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·26 June 2026·Updated October 2026

Every October, Hunza's poplar and fruit orchards turn brilliant gold and crimson against the snow peaks. It is short, spectacular, and worth planning around.

Updated October 2026: the colour season runs from late September through October, so if you are reading this in mid-October you are in the window now. The KKH through Hunza has reopened after the summer floods, but check the road status on the day you drive.

2026 timing

  • Peak colour: typically around mid-October, give or take a week with the weather.
  • Where: Karimabad and the orchards below Baltit Fort, Eagle's Nest (Duikar) at sunrise, Upper Hunza around Gulmit and Passu.
  • Bonus: crisp air, thin crowds, and clear mountain views compared with summer haze.

Khunjerab is usually still open to visitors through October (it is officially open year-round for trade, but the practical tourist window ends around then, and landslides closed the Sost-Khunjerab road for a time in September 2026), but book ahead - autumn is increasingly popular.
