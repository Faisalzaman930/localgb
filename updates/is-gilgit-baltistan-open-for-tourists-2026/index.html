Is Gilgit-Baltistan Open for Tourists in 2026?

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·24 June 2026

One of the most-asked questions every summer, so here's the straight answer for 2026.

Yes - Gilgit-Baltistan is open to tourists, including foreign travellers, and it is consistently rated among the safest parts of Pakistan to visit. Hunza in particular is a favourite for women and solo travellers.

What "open" means in practice

  • Towns and main valleys (Hunza, Skardu, Gilgit, Karimabad) are open and reachable year-round.
  • High passes are seasonal: Babusar and Deosai roughly late June-September, Khunjerab roughly May-November.
  • Permits: most areas need no special permit; a few border-adjacent zones do. Foreign nationals should carry passport copies.

The practical risks are weather and seasonal road access, not safety. Check the current road & pass status before you finalise dates, and see tour packages for trips timed to the open season.
