Is Gilgit-Baltistan Open for Tourists in 2026?

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026

Short answer: yes. As of October 2026 Gilgit-Baltistan is open to tourists, including foreign travellers. After unrest in early March 2026 (curfews in Gilgit and Skardu and a mobile shutdown) the UK FCDO advised against all but essential travel to GB from 12 March; it lifted that on 15 April 2026, and GB is not under any FCDO "advise against" warning now. The FCDO still advises against all travel on the KKH between Mansehra and Chilas. The real limits are seasonal: high passes and plateaus open and close with the snow.

Is Hunza open?

Yes, all year. Karimabad and the main Hunza valley are reachable year-round. Khunjerab Pass is officially open all year for trade, but the practical window for tourists is May to October, and the Sost border works Monday to Friday. See my Hunza guide.

Is Skardu open for tourism?

Yes, all year. Skardu, Shigar and Khaplu are open year-round, with flights from Islamabad when the weather allows. Deosai is only open from about mid-June to late September or early October. See my Skardu guide.

Is Naran Kaghan open for tourists?

Naran's main season is roughly May-June to October; from October, guesthouses in Naran start to close, while the lower valley (Balakot, Kiwai and Shogran) stays open year-round. Babusar Pass opens around late May or June and closes in October or November, depending on snow, and is open in daylight only. See my Naran and Kaghan guide.

Is Fairy Meadows open?

Roughly April or May to September or October - snow closes the jeep track outside that. See my Fairy Meadows guide.

Visas, permits and checkposts

  • Visa: the free "visa prior to arrival" was suspended from 1 January 2026. Apply for a paid e-visa at visa.nadra.gov.pk 4-6 weeks ahead.
  • No NOC is needed for tourism (abolished in 2019), but foreign nationals must register at police checkposts - carry several passport and visa copies.
  • Permits are needed for some treks (such as K2 Base Camp and Snow Lake) and for border-adjacent zones.

What can close roads

  • Monsoon: from late July to mid-August, floods closed the KKH, the Gilgit-Skardu road, Babusar and valley roads for days in both 2025 and 2026.
  • Snow: Babusar, Deosai and Fairy Meadows close for winter; early snow can close Babusar without warning.
  • Unrest: occasional, and usually short - but mobile data can be cut, so carry offline maps and cash.

FAQ

Is Gilgit-Baltistan open for tourists in 2026?

Yes. As of October 2026 GB is open to domestic and foreign tourists, and the UK FCDO has no warning against travel there (a temporary warning after March unrest was lifted on 15 April 2026).

Is Skardu open for tourism?

Yes, all year. Deosai, near Skardu, is only open from about mid-June to late September or early October.

Is Naran Kaghan open for tourists?

Naran's main season is roughly May-June to October, and the lower valley (Balakot to Shogran) is open year-round. Babusar Pass is open from about late May or June to October or November, in daylight only.

When is Babusar Pass open?

Roughly late May or June to October or November, depending on snow. It opened around 25-27 May in 2026 and is open in daylight hours only.
