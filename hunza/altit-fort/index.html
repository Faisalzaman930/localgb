Heritage Site

Altit Fort

Hunza's oldest fort — 900 years of history preserved in stone. Less visited than Baltit, but more intimate and with dramatic gorge views.

📍 Altit Village, Hunza
🏰 900+ years old
🎟 Rs.300–600 entry
🚗 4km from Karimabad
Home/Hunza/Altit Fort
History

Older Than
Baltit

Altit Fort predates Baltit Fort by around 200 years, making it over 900 years old and one of the oldest surviving structures in Gilgit-Baltistan. For centuries it served as the primary seat of the Mirs of Hunza before Baltit Fort was expanded and became the main royal residence.

The fort was restored by the Aga Khan Trust for Culture in the early 2000s, and the attached old village of Altit — a cluster of traditional stone and timber houses — was also preserved as a living heritage site. The combination of fort and village is unique in Hunza.

Visiting

Entry & What
to See

Entry feeRs.300 (Pakistanis) · Rs.600 (foreigners). Guided tour included.
LocationAltit village, 4km southeast of Karimabad. 20-minute drive down the valley road.
What to seeThe fort itself: three towers, throne room, ceremonial spaces. The old village: stone lanes, traditional houses, views over Hunza River gorge.
ViewsThe gorge views from Altit Fort are different to Baltit — looking down into the deep Hunza River canyon rather than across the valley. Equally dramatic.
Combine withBaltit Fort (both on same day is very doable). Spend 1h at Altit, 1h at Baltit.
