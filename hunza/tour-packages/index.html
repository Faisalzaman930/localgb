What's Included

What a Package
Typically Covers

Hunza packages are usually built around the same core: transport for the duration, accommodation in guesthouses or hotels, entry fees to the main sites, and a set itinerary covering the valley's highlights. The 3-day option focuses on Karimabad and its forts plus a day at Attabad Lake; the 5-day adds the run north toward Passu; the 7-day stretches as far as Khunjerab Pass and an optional trek. Most quoted costs assume two people sharing and exclude flights to and from Gilgit.

What is generally not included is worth checking before you commit: domestic flights, meals beyond breakfast, personal expenses, boat rides at Attabad, and the cost of any guide for serious treks. Reading the inclusions line by line is the single best way to compare one quote against another, because the headline price often hides where the differences actually are.

FAQ

Common
Questions

How many days do I need for Hunza?

Three days covers Karimabad, the forts, and Attabad Lake at a brisk pace. Five days adds upper Hunza around Passu, and seven days lets you reach Khunjerab Pass and fit in a trek.

What do tour packages usually include?

Typically transport for the trip, accommodation, entry fees to the main sites, and a set itinerary. Most quotes assume two people sharing and exclude flights.

What is usually not included?

Commonly excluded items are domestic flights, most meals beyond breakfast, boat rides, personal expenses, and guides for serious treks. Always check the inclusions line by line.

Do I need an agency, or can I plan it myself?

These itineraries can be self-planned at roughly 60–70% of package cost if you arrange transport locally and book guesthouses direct. Agencies add the most value for complex multi-region trips, K2 trekking, or guaranteed logistics.

What does a package cost?

As a rough guide for two people sharing: the 3-day from around Rs.20,000–35,000 per person, the 5-day Rs.35,000–55,000, and the 7-day Rs.50,000–80,000, excluding flights.

Are flights included in the price?

No. Quoted package costs generally exclude flights to and from Gilgit, which you book separately.
