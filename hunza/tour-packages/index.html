Hunza Tour
Packages

Three itinerary lengths, honest costs, and the honest answer about whether you actually need a tour agency.

📍 Hunza Valley, GB
📅 3, 5 & 7 day options
💰 From Rs.20,000/person
🚗 Transport included
Home/Hunza/Tour Packages
3-Day Package

Weekend in
Hunza

Day 1Arrive Gilgit by flight or overnight bus. Drive to Karimabad (2h). Check in, explore bazaar, sunset view from guesthouse terrace.
Day 2Morning: Eagle's Nest sunrise hike/jeep. Baltit Fort (2h). Altit Fort (1.5h). Evening walk in old Altit village.
Day 3Drive to Attabad Lake (boat trip Rs.500–800). Drive back to Gilgit for evening flight or bus.
Typical costRs.20,000–35,000/person (2 people). Includes transport, accommodation, entry fees. Excludes flights.
5-Day Package

Hunza
Essentials

Days 1–3As above for 3-day package.
Day 4Drive north to Passu (105km). Hussaini Bridge, Passu Cones views. Walk to Borith Lake. Overnight in Passu guesthouse.
Day 5Morning: Batura Glacier walk (optional). Drive back to Gilgit or Karimabad.
Typical costRs.35,000–55,000/person (2 people). Includes all transport, accommodation, entries.
7-Day Package

Full Hunza
Experience

Days 1–5As above for 5-day package.
Day 6Karimabad rest day: Rush Lake trek from Nagar (8–10h, strenuous), or Baltit Fort afternoon light photography, or shopping.
Day 7Drive to Khunjerab Pass (240km, 3.5h each way). Return to Karimabad or Gilgit.
Typical costRs.50,000–80,000/person (2 people). Permits, all transport, all accommodation, guide for Rush Lake.
DIY vs agency: These packages can be self-planned at 60–70% of the above cost if you organise transport locally in Hunza (haggle) and book guesthouses direct. Agencies add value primarily for complex multi-region itineraries, K2 trekking, or if you want guaranteed logistics.
