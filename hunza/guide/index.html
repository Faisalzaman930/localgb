Overview

Hunza in
Plain Terms

Hunza is a valley in the upper Karakoram, running along the Hunza River from Gilgit to the Chinese border at Khunjerab Pass. The main town, Karimabad, sits at 2,438m with direct views of Rakaposhi (7,788m), Lady Finger (6,000m), and Ultar Peak (7,388m).

It is Pakistan's most internationally visited destination — the combination of mountain scenery, Ismaili hospitality, and historical Silk Road heritage is genuinely exceptional. But it is also heavily touristed in peak season, and prices reflect this.

Crowd reality: July and August see very heavy domestic tourism. April (blossom) and October (autumn foliage) are the best times — comparable scenery, 60% fewer people.

Getting There

How to
Reach Hunza

By road from ISB460km via KKH. NATCO bus Rs.1,500–2,000 (18–20h). Private car Rs.20,000–30,000. Stop in Besham or Chilas overnight for the scenic approach.
Fly to GilgitPIA/Air Sial ISB→Gilgit (45min, Rs.5,000–12,000). Then 2h drive to Karimabad. Flights cancel often — always have 1 day buffer.
Within the valleyShared Suzuki vans connect villages (Rs.50–150). Jeep hire: Rs.5,000–10,000/day. Everything in Karimabad is walkable.
