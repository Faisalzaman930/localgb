Hunza Hotels
by Budget

Hunza has 30+ guesthouses and hotels concentrated in Karimabad, with a smaller selection in Aliabad (5km below) and Passu (105km north). Quality varies enormously. Booking ahead is essential in April (blossom) and July–August.

Booking tip: Call directly or book through the guesthouse WhatsApp — Booking.com and similar sites charge 15–18% commission which gets passed to the price. Direct bookings save money for both parties.
