I Checked Out Where to Stay in Hunza - the Best Hotels for 2026

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026

Hunza has some of the most spectacular places to stay in Pakistan: heritage hotels by Altit and Baltit forts, view hotels terraced above Karimabad facing Rakaposhi, and friendly guesthouses for a fraction of the price. I'm from Hunza, so here's the honest ranked list for 2026.

I've ranked real, open places on view, location, comfort and value, with each one's real public rating where it has one.

How I picked these Hunza hotels

I went through the places actually worth booking in Hunza across luxury, mid-range and budget, judging each on location, comfort, value and the view. 8 of 9 have a real public rating, shown with the review count and a maps link; the rest I rate from local knowledge and mark with a dash rather than invent a score.

The 9 best, reviewed

#1

Luxus Hunza Attabad Lake Resort

Luxury · Aliabad
📍 Aliabad🏨 Luxury💷 $$$9.0/10 Booking.com (361)

Why stay: Attabad Lake-front views; spa, sauna, fitness center; the most-publicized 5-star resort in the valley

What's good

  • Direct turquoise Attabad Lake frontage and dramatic mountain backdrop
  • Full spa/wellness facilities (sauna, steam room, hot tub) rare for the region
  • Strong marks for staff attentiveness and cleanliness

Could be better

  • Recurring guest complaints about sewage smell in some rooms
  • Sits at Aina Abad on the lake, ~45 min from Karimabad's town/forts
My take: The closest thing Hunza has to a true luxury lake resort, with knockout views; just temper expectations on maintenance versus the 5-star billing. (TripAdvisor scores it lower, ~3/5 from 70 reviews.)
#2

Old Hunza Inn

Budget · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🏨 Budget💷 $8.5/10 Booking.com

Why stay: Backpacker institution since 1981 run by owner 'Leo', great views and traveler hub vibe

What's good

  • Among the cheapest beds in Karimabad with clean rooms and hot showers
  • Spectacular mountain/glacier views on a quiet edge of town
  • Owner Leo and the social, info-sharing atmosphere are a real draw

Could be better

  • Basic, simple rooms (it's a backpacker guesthouse, not a hotel)
  • Limited facilities
My take: The classic budget pick in Hunza for value, views and meeting other travelers; rated ~8.5/10 on Booking.com.
#3

Baltit Heritage Inn

Mid-range · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🏨 Mid-range💷 $$8.0/10 Expedia

Why stay: Walkable to Baltit Fort with clean rooms and mountain views at fair value

What's good

  • Excellent location steps from Baltit Fort and Karimabad's restaurants
  • Clean, comfortable rooms with mountain views
  • Helpful, accommodating staff and good value

Could be better

  • Electricity and Wi-Fi can be unstable (common across Hunza)
  • Smaller property with limited facilities
My take: A dependable, good-value base for exploring Karimabad on foot; guest rating around 8.0/10.
#4

Passu Tourist Lodge

Budget · Passu
📍 Passu🏨 Budget💷 $★ 4.1 Tripadvisor (71)

Why stay: Quiet year-round lodge with front-row Passu Cones views on the Karakoram Highway

What's good

  • Superb, isolated views of the Passu Cones and snow peaks
  • Friendly, English-speaking staff and calm atmosphere
  • Open year-round, unlike many Hunza hotels that close in winter

Could be better

  • Simple lodge-style rooms and limited amenities
  • Remote Passu location, far from Karimabad's sights
My take: The best-reviewed base in Passu for trekkers and Cones-chasers wanting quiet and value; ~4.1/5 from 71 Tripadvisor reviews.
#5

Serena Altit Fort Residence

Luxury · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🏨 Luxury💷 $$$★ 4.0 Tripadvisor (12)

Why stay: Heritage stay beside Altit Fort with the treehouse-style Kha Basi cafe and complimentary fort tour

What's good

  • Quiet, historic Altit setting away from Karimabad's bustle
  • Phenomenal valley views and the standout Kha Basi cafe
  • Complimentary Altit Fort tour for in-house guests

Could be better

  • Described as a temporary/interim property while Serena's permanent Hunza hotel is built
  • Very small (only a handful of reviews so far)
My take: A characterful heritage residence for travelers who want history and calm over resort scale; #1 of 1 inn in Altit on Tripadvisor.
#6

Hunza Serena Hotel

Luxury · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🏨 Luxury💷 $$$★ 4.0 Tripadvisor

Why stay: Polished international-brand service in the heart of Karimabad with valley and Rakaposhi views

What's good

  • Most consistent, professional service of any Karimabad hotel (Serena-managed)
  • Central Karimabad location with terrace/restaurant views and a spa
  • Clean, comfortable rooms with reliable amenities

Could be better

  • Some guests find rooms smaller/less grand than other Serena properties
  • Premium pricing for the area
My take: The safest high-end pick inside Karimabad itself; ranked #2 of 11 hotels in Karimabad on Tripadvisor and rated up to 9.4-9.8 on booking platforms.
#7

Hunza Embassy Hotel

Mid-range · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🏨 Mid-range💷 $$★ 3.4 Tripadvisor (54)

Why stay: Long-running Karimabad hotel with terraces overlooking the valley and a well-liked restaurant

What's good

  • Most rooms have private terraces with sweeping valley views
  • On-site restaurant praised for tasty food and helpful staff
  • Reliable hot water and central Karimabad location

Could be better

  • Rooms can get very hot in July/August
  • Reports of unreliable Wi-Fi and patchy cleanliness/upkeep
My take: A solid old-guard mid-range choice for the view and the food, but somewhat dated; rated ~3.4/5 from 54 Tripadvisor reviews.
#8

Eagle's Nest Hotel

Mid-range · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🏨 Mid-range💷 $$★ 3.0 Tripadvisor (139)

Why stay: Duikar viewpoint above Karimabad with the valley's most famous Rakaposhi and Lady Finger panorama

What's good

  • Arguably the single best sunrise/sunset viewpoint of any hotel in Hunza (Duikar)
  • Rooms and terrace face Rakaposhi, Lady Finger and the whole valley
  • Owners/staff repeatedly described as warm and welcoming

Could be better

  • Recurring complaints about management/service and only average food
  • Up a steep road well above Karimabad town
My take: You stay here for the legendary Duikar view, not the service; #3 of 17 Hunza hotels on Tripadvisor with notably mixed reviews.
#9

Hunza Marcopolo Inn

Guesthouse · Gulmit
📍 Gulmit🏨 Guesthouse💷 $$

Why stay: 40+ year-old Gulmit inn with orchards and Passu Cones views; the main stay option in Gulmit

What's good

  • Magnificent views of the Passu Cones from a peaceful Gulmit setting
  • Hands-on GM and personal hospitality frequently praised
  • Orchard grounds, 24-hr hot water and on-site restaurant

Could be better

  • Some guests found it shabby/ill-maintained
  • Not recommended in winter (running-water issues in cold months)
My take: The established, characterful choice if you want to base in Gulmit near the Passu Cones, with mixed reviews on upkeep.

How to choose where to stay in Hunza

  • Karimabad vs Aliabad - Karimabad for views, forts and cafes on foot; Aliabad is the busier service town on the highway.
  • The view is the point - pay for a Rakaposhi/valley-facing room; it's why you came.
  • Upper Hunza (Gulmit/Passu) - quieter, dramatic, but fewer services; good for a night near the Cones.
  • Book the heritage/view rooms early - the best Karimabad rooms sell out in spring blossom and autumn.

FAQ

Where should I stay in Hunza?

Karimabad for the views, forts and walkable cafes; Aliabad for highway convenience; Gulmit/Passu for a quiet upper-Hunza night. The ranked list above covers each.

Which Hunza hotels have the best Rakaposhi views?

The terraced hotels above Karimabad and the heritage properties near the forts have the best valley and Rakaposhi views - noted per hotel above.

How much is a hotel in Hunza?

Guesthouses are inexpensive; mid-range view hotels are moderate; heritage and luxury properties are the priciest. Marked $ to $$$ in the table.

When should I book Hunza hotels?

For cherry-blossom (late March-April) and autumn (October), book weeks ahead - those are the busiest, most beautiful windows.
