I Ate My Way Around Hunza - the Best Restaurants for 2026

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026

Hunza is one of the most rewarding places to eat in Pakistan: organic Hunzai dishes you won't find elsewhere, apricot and walnut everything, and cafes terraced above Karimabad with Rakaposhi filling the window. I'm from Hunza, so this is the real ranked list for 2026.

I judged each place on the food, the view and setting, value and consistency, and listed its real public rating where it has one.

How I picked these Hunza restaurants

I went through the places that are actually open and worth your time in Hunza, judging each on the food, the setting, value and consistency rather than hype. 5 of 9 have a real public rating, which I show with the review count and a maps link; the rest are local spots I rate from experience, shown with a dash rather than an invented score.

The 9 best, reviewed

#1

Cafe Culture Hunza

Central Hunza traditional (farm-to-table) · Karimabad (Altit village)
📍 Karimabad (Altit village)🍴 Central Hunza traditional (farm-to-table)💷 $$★ 4.7 Tripadvisor (7)

Must try: Walnut chapati, rose dumplings, burus-e-barikutz, chapshuro

What's good

  • Almost everything made from scratch with ingredients from their own garden
  • Highest-rated of the Hunza cafes on Tripadvisor (#6 of 19)
  • Warm family hosting; owner will show you the garden

Could be better

  • Very few total reviews so the rating is thinly sampled
  • Tucked in Altit village, not on the main bazaar strip
My take: A small family-run gem for organic Central Hunza dishes. Unique items like walnut chapati and rose dumplings set it apart.
#2

Cafe de Hunza

Cafe / continental (coffee, cakes) · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🍴 Cafe / continental (coffee, cakes)💷 $$★ 4.4 Tripadvisor (125)

Must try: Walnut cake with Hunza tea or real espresso

What's good

  • Famous honey-caramelized walnut cake, often sells out (order a day ahead)
  • One of the few places in Hunza serving genuine brewed/espresso coffee
  • Ranked #1 of 19 restaurants in Hunza on Tripadvisor; central in Karimabad Bazaar with wifi

Could be better

  • A few reviewers find the walnut cake dry/biscuit-like rather than moist
  • Small bazaar-front spot, can get crowded
My take: The iconic Karimabad pit stop. Come for the walnut cake and real coffee; it earns its #1 ranking even if the cake divides opinion.
#3

Pizza Pamir

Pizza / continental with local touches · Karimabad (also Aliabad, Attabad)
📍 Karimabad (also Aliabad, Attabad)🍴 Pizza / continental with local touches💷 $$★ 4.4 Tripadvisor (20)

Must try: Yak pizza

What's good

  • The yak pizza is a genuinely local specialty and a crowd favorite
  • Balcony with stunning Hunza Valley views
  • Friendly staff; ranked #4 of 19 in Hunza

Could be better

  • Yak meat takes time to cook, so expect a wait
  • A pizzeria, so it's the non-traditional option if you want local food
My take: When you want a break from Hunzai food, this is the valley's best pizza, and the yak pizza keeps it regional.
#4

Hunza Food Pavilion (by Laal Shehzadi)

Traditional Hunzai (organic, home-style) · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🍴 Traditional Hunzai (organic, home-style)💷 $★ 4.4 Tripadvisor (18)

Must try: Chapshuro, Dawdo soup, giyaling, burus shapik

What's good

  • Run by Laal Shehzadi, serving organic local food for over a decade
  • On the path up to Baltit Fort with mountain-backdrop seating
  • Genuinely home-cooked, cheap, warm hospitality (#1 of 3 in Karimabad listing)

Could be better

  • Very small / simple setup
  • One reviewer called it a tourist trap
My take: The most home-cooked, no-frills Hunzai food in Karimabad. Cheap, organic and personal; great stop on the Baltit Fort walk.
#5

Hidden Paradise Hunza

Traditional Hunzai / Pakistani / continental · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🍴 Traditional Hunzai / Pakistani / continental💷 $$★ 4.3 Tripadvisor (70)

Must try: Chapshuro (meat-filled flatbread) and yak steak; local platter (supra, hoilo garma, burus)

What's good

  • Authentic local platters cooked fresh in front of you
  • Seating with valley views or in a fruit orchard
  • Ranked #2 of 19 in Hunza; generous organic portions

Could be better

  • Long waits since food is cooked to order
  • At least one reviewer reported off-smelling meat in the chapshuro
My take: A go-to for a full traditional Hunzai spread in Karimabad. Order ahead and be patient; the platter is the move.
#6

Glacier Breeze Restaurant

Continental / local (cafe + restaurant) · Passu
📍 Passu🍴 Continental / local (cafe + restaurant)💷 $$

Must try: Apricot cake with tumoro (green) tea or French-press coffee

What's good

  • Its warm, syrupy apricot cake is locally legendary
  • 360-degree views of Passu Glacier and the Passu Cones from the glacier moraine
  • Ranked #1 of 2 restaurants in Passu; clean and well-run

Could be better

  • Some report the cake served not-warm or local mains overcooked
  • Remote Passu location, mainly a stop en route to/from upper Gojal
My take: The signature Passu stop for apricot cake and a view of the Cones. Worth timing a Gojal day-trip around. (Rated highly on Tripadvisor; exact star value not confirmed.)
#7

Kha Basi Cafe

Traditional Hunzai (women-run) · Karimabad (Altit Fort gardens)
📍 Karimabad (Altit Fort gardens)🍴 Traditional Hunzai (women-run)💷 $$

Must try: Dawdo soup, chapshuro, berikutz, apricot juice, tumuro chai

What's good

  • Entirely women-operated, in the gardens beside Altit Fort
  • Standout veranda views over the Hunza Valley
  • Cozy vintage interior; consistently praised local dishes

Could be better

  • On the pricier side for the area
  • Slow serving times noted by several visitors
My take: Pair it with an Altit Fort visit. The all-women kitchen and garden setting make it as much an experience as a meal.
#8

Jahaan Restaurant

Wakhi-Pamirian / Pakistani / Chinese · Gulmit
📍 Gulmit🍴 Wakhi-Pamirian / Pakistani / Chinese💷 $$

Must try: Gral (sweet wheat with apricot oil), molida, ghilmindi, dowdo

What's good

  • One of the few places highlighting Wakhi-Pamirian dishes like gral and molida
  • Scenic terrace over the Hunza River with Passu Cones and the suspension-bridge view
  • Broad menu (also karahi, biryani, chow mein) for mixed groups

Could be better

  • No verifiable rating yet (effectively 0 reviews on Tripadvisor)
  • In Gulmit, off the main Karimabad circuit
My take: The best bet for rarer Wakhi-Pamiri cooking in Gulmit, with a knockout terrace. Ratings aren't established yet, so go for the cuisine and the view.
#9

Hunza Embassy Hotel Restaurant

Hunzai / Pakistani / continental · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🍴 Hunzai / Pakistani / continental💷 $$

Must try: Local Hunzai set meal

What's good

  • Long-standing Karimabad establishment with valley views
  • Reliable mixed menu (local, Pakistani, continental)
  • Central, easy to combine with a bazaar/fort visit

Could be better

  • Hotel restaurant rather than a destination eatery
  • No specific star rating verified
My take: A dependable, centrally located fallback in Karimabad when the smaller cafes are full or closed.

What to eat in Hunza

  • Chapshuro - the Hunza meat-filled pastry, the one dish to not miss.
  • Harissa, diram fitti (sweet sprouted-wheat bread) and hoi garma - traditional Hunzai comfort food.
  • Apricot & walnut - apricot soup, oil, cakes, and walnut cake at the view cafes.
  • Tumuro (mountain thyme) chai and Hunza water - the local drinks to try.

FAQ

What food is Hunza famous for?

Chapshuro (meat pastry), harissa, diram fitti, apricot soup and cakes, walnut cake, and tumuro herbal tea. The food is largely organic and local.

Where are the best cafes in Karimabad?

The terraced view cafes above Karimabad bazaar - several look straight at Rakaposhi. The ranked list above covers the best for food and for view.

Can you get traditional Hunzai food in restaurants?

Yes - several places do a Hunzai set or platter (chapshuro, harissa, local breads). A few need a little notice for the full traditional spread.

How much does eating in Hunza cost?

Chapshuro and cafe plates are inexpensive; a full sit-down meal with a view costs more. The table marks each place $ to $$$.
