I Ate My Way Around Hunza - the Best Restaurants for 2026

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026

Hunza is one of the most rewarding places to eat in Pakistan: organic Hunzai dishes you won't find elsewhere, apricot and walnut everything, and cafes terraced above Karimabad with Rakaposhi filling the window. I'm from Hunza, so this is the real ranked list for 2026.

I judged each place on the food, the view and setting, value and consistency, and listed its real public rating where it has one.

How I picked these Hunza restaurants

I went through the places that are actually open and worth your time in Hunza, judging each on the food, the setting, value and consistency rather than hype. 10 of 10 have a real public rating, which I show with the review count and a maps link; the rest are local spots I rate from experience, shown with a dash rather than an invented score.

The 10 best, reviewed

#1

Gingerfort Cafe

Cafe / Italian-American · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🍴 Cafe / Italian-American💷 $$ · 500-1200 pp PKR👍 coffee & cozy hangout / afternoon-evening🗓 seasonal - Karimabad cafe, quiet in deep winter★ 5.0 Tripadvisor (19)

Must try: Coffee, snacks and light bites

What's good

  • Currently the top-rated cafe in Hunza (5.0), rising fast
  • Cozy wooden decor with Hunza Valley views
  • Reasonable prices, drinks + snacks + fuller bites

Could be better

  • Only open afternoons/evenings
  • Newer, so still a small review base
My take: The freshest addition to Karimabad's cafe scene and the current darling of reviewers - a strong coffee-and-chill alternative to Cafe de Hunza.
📍 Google Maps · Apple Maps (approx.)
#2

Cafe Culture Hunza

Authentic central-Hunza, organic · Altit
📍 Altit🍴 Authentic central-Hunza, organic💷 $$ · 800-1600 pp PKR👍 Hunzai food / organic local specialties🗓 seasonal - quiet/closed in deep winter★ 4.7 Tripadvisor (7)

GB local specialty Burus berikutz / hani-xe-barikutz (apricot-kernel chapati)

Must try: Chapshuro; burus-e-berikutz

What's good

  • Highest-rated of the local-cuisine cafes (4.7)
  • Genuinely rare dishes (burus berikutz, apricot-kernel chapati)
  • Warm owners, homemade organic food

Could be better

  • Small and low-volume, so few reviews / limited hours
  • In Altit, not central Karimabad (need to go to it)
My take: A quiet standout for travelers who want the real central-Hunza dishes done well - worth the short trip to Altit.
📍 Google Maps · Apple Maps (approx.)
#3

Glacier Breeze Restaurant

Cafe / continental + local · Passu
📍 Passu🍴 Cafe / continental + local💷 $$ · 800-1800 pp PKR👍 Passu Cones/Glacier view + apricot cake pit-stop on the KKH🗓 seasonal - upper Hunza; closes roughly Nov-Mar (very cold)★ 4.6 Tripadvisor (47)

GB local specialty Apricot cake (local apricots)

Must try: Apricot cake (served warm from the oven)

What's good

  • Family-run since 2003, 360° Passu Cathedral/glacier views
  • Famous warm apricot cake and good tea
  • Reliable clean stop on the KKH

Could be better

  • 100+ stairs up from the road parking
  • Remote/cold; limited winter operation
My take: The essential Upper Hunza stop - climb the stairs for the view and a warm slice of apricot cake.
📍 Google Maps · Apple Maps (approx.)
#4

Jahaan Restaurant

Wakhi-Pamirian + Pakistani/Chinese · Gulmit
📍 Gulmit🍴 Wakhi-Pamirian + Pakistani/Chinese💷 $$ · 1000-2000 pp PKR👍 Wakhi cuisine with Passu Cones / river views🗓 seasonal - upper Hunza, closes in winter★ 4.5 Tripadvisor (30)

GB local specialty Gral, molida, ghilmindi, dowdo (Wakhi-Pamirian)

Must try: Molida / gral (Wakhi specialties)

What's good

  • Rare, well-executed Wakhi-Pamirian dishes (gral, molida, ghilmindi)
  • Modern cozy interior, strong hygiene
  • Views of Passu Cones, river and Friendship Bridge

Could be better

  • Pricier and more polished than village norm
  • Out in Gulmit - a detour from central Hunza
My take: The best modern showcase of Wakhi cuisine in Upper Hunza; go for the dishes you can't get in Karimabad.
📍 Google Maps · Apple Maps (approx.)
#5

Cafe de Hunza

Cafe / coffee, cake, Hunza tea · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🍴 Cafe / coffee, cake, Hunza tea💷 $$ · 600-1400 pp (cake + coffee) PKR👍 coffee & cake, most reliable WiFi in Karimabad🗓 seasonal - closes/goes quiet in deep winter (reported shut mid-December)★ 4.4 Tripadvisor (125)

GB local specialty Walnut cake

Must try: Walnut cake with Hunza tea

What's good

  • Iconic honey-caramel walnut cake, made fresh
  • Rakaposhi/valley views from upper balcony
  • Reliable coffee and WiFi hub

Could be better

  • Some items feel overpriced for portion
  • Cake sweetness not for everyone
My take: Still the signature Karimabad cafe stop; go for the walnut cake and the view, not a full meal.
📍 Google Maps · Apple Maps (approx.)
#6

Hunza Food Pavilion (Laal Shehzadi)

Traditional Hunza, organic · Karimabad
📍 Karimabad🍴 Traditional Hunza, organic💷 $$ · 700-1400 pp PKR👍 authentic Hunzai home cooking beside Baltit Fort★ 4.4 Tripadvisor (60)

GB local specialty Chapshuro / dowdo / diram fitti

Must try: Chapshuro and dowdo (daudo) soup

What's good

  • Woman-run (Laal Shehzadi), organic local ingredients
  • Beside Baltit Fort, made famous by Mark Wiens

  • Can feel touristy and a touch pricey

Kha Basi Cafe

Traditional Hunza, women-run · Altit
📍 Altit🍴 Traditional Hunza, women-run💷 $$ · 1000-2000 pp PKR👍 Hunzai food in a royal apricot orchard under Altit Fort🗓 seasonal - orchard cafe, closes in winter★ 4.3 Tripadvisor (40)

GB local specialty Dowdo / diram fitti / burus shapik / apricot juice

Must try: Dowdo soup and chapshuro

What's good

  • Wide spread of genuine traditional dishes + fresh apricot juice

Try something you can't get elsewhere

The dishes worth crossing Hunza for - genuinely local to Gilgit-Baltistan, not the usual Pakistani menu:

  • Burus berikutz / hani-xe-barikutz (apricot-kernel chapati) - at Cafe Culture Hunza
  • Apricot cake (local apricots) - at Glacier Breeze Restaurant
  • Gral, molida, ghilmindi, dowdo (Wakhi-Pamirian) - at Jahaan Restaurant
  • Walnut cake - at Cafe de Hunza
  • Chapshuro / dowdo / diram fitti - at Hunza Food Pavilion (Laal Shehzadi)
  • Yak pizza; also serves chapshuro - at Pizza Pamir
  • Chapshuro (yak also available) - at Hidden Paradise Hunza
  • Dowdo / diram fitti / burus shapik / apricot juice - at Kha Basi Cafe

What to eat in Hunza

  • Chapshuro - the Hunza meat-filled pastry, the one dish to not miss.
  • Harissa, diram fitti (sweet sprouted-wheat bread) and hoi garma - traditional Hunzai comfort food.
  • Apricot & walnut - apricot soup, oil, cakes, and walnut cake at the view cafes.
  • Tumuro (mountain thyme) chai and Hunza water - the local drinks to try.

FAQ

What food is Hunza famous for?

Chapshuro (meat pastry), harissa, diram fitti, apricot soup and cakes, walnut cake, and tumuro herbal tea. The food is largely organic and local.

Where are the best cafes in Karimabad?

The terraced view cafes above Karimabad bazaar - several look straight at Rakaposhi. The ranked list above covers the best for food and for view.

Can you get traditional Hunzai food in restaurants?

Yes - several places do a Hunzai set or platter (chapshuro, harissa, local breads). A few need a little notice for the full traditional spread.

How much does eating in Hunza cost?

Chapshuro and cafe plates are inexpensive; a full sit-down meal with a view costs more. The table marks each place $ to $$$.

Where can I try Burus berikutz / hani-xe-barikutz in Hunza?

Cafe Culture Hunza is the spot locals point to. See the full list above for other genuinely local dishes and where to find them.
