Honeymoon in Hunza: A Local's Guide for Couples

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026

A Hunza honeymoon is one of the most beautiful you can have in Asia: snow peaks outside your window, old forts above apricot orchards, and evenings in quiet cafes. I'm from Gilgit-Baltistan, so here's how I'd plan it - when to go, where to stay, and what's worth doing as a couple.

Best time for a honeymoon in Hunza

  • Late March to mid-April: cherry and apricot blossom across Karimabad and Altit - the most romantic window, and the busiest, so book early.
  • Late September to October: golden poplars, clear skies and fewer visitors. My pick for couples.
  • Summer: everything is open and green, but late July to mid-August brings monsoon landslides on the road - keep spare days.
  • Winter: beautiful and silent, but several hotels close (Luxus at Attabad shuts for winter) and evenings are very cold.

Romantic things to do in Hunza

  • Watch sunrise or sunset at Eagle's Nest above Duikar - eleven peaks turning pink.
  • Take the guided tour of Altit Fort and have tea in the royal garden below it.
  • A boat ride on Attabad Lake, then lunch in Gulmit.
  • Walk around Borith Lake and drive past the Passu Cones in the late afternoon light.
  • Cross the Hussaini suspension bridge together - if you're both brave.
  • Wander Karimabad's lanes and cafes in the evening, below Baltit Fort.

A 5-day Hunza honeymoon plan

Day 1Fly Islamabad to Gilgit (about 1 h, weather permitting) and drive to Karimabad (2-3.5 h). Slow evening.
Day 2Baltit Fort in the morning, Altit Fort and its garden in the afternoon, sunset at Eagle's Nest.
Day 3Attabad Lake by boat, lunch in Gulmit, Borith Lake and the Passu Cones. One night at Attabad or Passu.
Day 4A lazy morning, then back to Karimabad for a last evening in the old town.
Day 5Drive to Gilgit and fly home - or take two days by road via Chilas.

Want more? Add Khunjerab Pass (4,693m - keep it short) or two nights at Fairy Meadows on the way in.

Tips for couples travelling in Gilgit-Baltistan

  • Carry your CNICs or passports - hotels ask for ID at check-in, and so do checkposts on the way.
  • It's a conservative, friendly region: dress modestly and keep public affection low-key. In your hotel and on a private jeep, you'll have all the privacy you want.
  • Book the view room early for blossom (late March to mid-April) and autumn (late September to October) - the best rooms go first.
  • Carry enough cash; ATMs are limited outside the main towns.
  • Keep one spare day for weather, especially if you're flying.

FAQ

What is the best month for a honeymoon in Hunza?

Late March to mid-April for cherry and apricot blossom, or late September to October for autumn colours and clear skies. Avoid late July to mid-August, when monsoon landslides can close the road.

Which is the most romantic hotel in Hunza?

Serena Altit Fort Residence for heritage and quiet, Luxus at Attabad Lake for a luxury lake view (closed in winter), and Eagle's Nest at Duikar for the famous sunrise. Hunza Serena is the most consistent all-rounder.

How much does a Hunza honeymoon package cost?

Published 5-day honeymoon packages by road cost Rs 120,000, Rs 135,000 or Rs 160,000 depending on the hotel level and meals. Ask for the hotel name in writing.

How many days do we need for a honeymoon in Hunza?

Five days covers it if you fly to Gilgit; allow seven by road, or if you want Khunjerab Pass and Upper Hunza at a relaxed pace.
