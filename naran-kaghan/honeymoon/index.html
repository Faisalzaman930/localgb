Honeymoon in Naran and Kaghan: The Short Mountain Escape

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026

Naran is the honeymoon for couples short on time: it's 6-9 hours by road from Islamabad, there's no flight to worry about, and the scenery - glacier lakes, pine forests and the Babusar Pass - is some of the best in Pakistan. The catch is crowds, so timing is everything.

Best time for a honeymoon in Naran

  • June: snow just cleared, cool days and wildflowers everywhere.
  • September: clear, quiet and golden. My favourite.
  • Avoid July-August if you can: Naran fills up, and a 2-hour valley drive can take 6 in traffic.
  • Season: the Babusar road is open roughly late May to October; outside that, stay lower down at Shogran.

Romantic places to stay

  • PTDC Log Huts, Shogran - government log cabins in the forest, about PKR 10,000-18,000 a night. Quiet and cosy.
  • Pine Park Hotel, Shogran - reliable, with views of Malika Parbat, about PKR 6,000-12,000.
  • Lalazar Hotel, Naran - the best-appointed hotel in Naran town, with river views and reliable heating.
  • PTDC Motel, Naran - basic but dependable, about PKR 2,000-4,000, if you'd rather spend on experiences.

For a quieter honeymoon, stay at Shogran rather than in Naran town - it's lower (2,362m), forested and much calmer. Prices are 2026 guides; they rise sharply in August.

Romantic things to do

  • Lake Saif-ul-Malook early in the morning, before the crowds (jeep about PKR 5,500 return from Naran). It's the lake of the prince-and-fairy legend.
  • Lulusar Lake - bigger and quieter, perfect reflections on a calm morning.
  • Babusar Top at dawn (4,173m) - stay near Lulusar and drive up before the tour jeeps arrive.
  • Siri Paye meadows above Shogran - a 2-hour walk or a horse ride (about PKR 800-1,500 one way).
  • Lalazar plateau for wildflowers and birch forest, and a trout lunch by the Kunhar River.

A 3-day Naran honeymoon plan

Day 1Islamabad to Shogran (via Kiwai). Afternoon walk towards Siri Paye; night in a log hut.
Day 2Up the valley to Naran: Lake Saif-ul-Malook in the morning, Lulusar and Babusar Top in the afternoon.
Day 3A slow morning by the Kunhar, then home.

Got more time? In summer, cross Babusar to Chilas and carry on to Hunza - see honeymoon in Hunza.

Tips for couples travelling in Gilgit-Baltistan

  • Carry your CNICs or passports - hotels ask for ID at check-in, and so do checkposts on the way.
  • It's a conservative, friendly region: dress modestly and keep public affection low-key. In your hotel and on a private jeep, you'll have all the privacy you want.
  • Book the view room early for blossom (late March to mid-April) and autumn (late September to October) - the best rooms go first.
  • Carry enough cash; ATMs are limited outside the main towns.
  • Keep one spare day for weather, especially if you're flying.

FAQ

Is Naran good for a honeymoon?

Yes, especially if you have only 3-4 days: it's 6-9 hours by road from Islamabad with no flights to worry about. Go in June or September to avoid the summer crowds, and stay at Shogran for a quieter base.

What is the best month for a honeymoon in Naran?

June or September. July and August are the busiest months, with heavy traffic in the valley.

How much does a Naran honeymoon package cost?

Published packages cost about Rs 60,000 for 3 days in Naran and Kaghan, or Rs 40,000 for 2 days in Shogran, with breakfast and dinner.

Where should we stay in Naran for a honeymoon?

The PTDC log huts or Pine Park Hotel at Shogran for quiet forest views, or Lalazar Hotel in Naran town for comfort and river views.
