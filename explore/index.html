How are you
getting to GB?

Your entry point determines your entire route — this is the most important question.

✈️
Fly into Gilgit
PIA from Islamabad. 45 mins. Best start for Hunza and upper KKH.
Most common
Cancellation rate 40–60%. Always have a road backup.
✈️
Fly into Skardu
PIA from Islamabad or Lahore. Gateway to K2, Deosai, and the Baltistan region.
K2 region
Scenic mountain views on approach. Weather-dependent too.
🚗
Drive via KKH
Islamabad → Besham → Chilas → Gilgit. 15–18h. The classic Karakoram Highway.
Classic route
Kohistan section is rough. Drive in daylight only.
🏔
Via Babusar Pass
Islamabad → Naran → Babusar Top → Chilas → GB. The scenic mountain alternative.
Summer only · Jun–Oct
4,173m pass. Jeep only above Naran. More dramatic than the KKH.

When are
you going?

The season completely changes what's possible — some passes close, some valleys are at their best.

🌸
Spring
March – May. Blossom season. Cool days, cold nights. Khunjerab opens in May. Peak photography.
Best for blossom

June – August. All passes open. Hot in Gilgit (36°C). All treks accessible. Busiest season.

Best overall

November – February. Cold and quiet. Hunza and Gilgit accessible. Naltar for skiing. Few tourists.
