Overview

Ghizer in
Plain Terms

Ghizer is the westernmost district of Gilgit-Baltistan, running 240km along the Ghizer River from Gahkuch to Shandur Pass. The valley connects GB to Chitral (KPK) over the pass and is the only land route between these two mountain regions in summer.

Three distinct sections define the valley: lower Ghizer from Gahkuch to Gupis, middle Ghizer from Gupis to Phander with its glacial lake, and upper Ghizer from Phander up to Shandur Pass at 3,734m. Each section has a different character — lower is more settled and arid, middle is lush and lake-dotted, upper is a high alpine plateau.

Why come to Ghizer: The annual Shandur Polo Festival (July) is the biggest cultural event in GB. Even outside festival season, Phander Lake and the drive to Shandur are among the most scenic and uncrowded journeys in Pakistan.

Valley Sections

Three Parts
of Ghizer

Lower GhizerGahkuch to Gupis. District HQ, bazaars, ATM. Semi-arid hillsides with irrigated terraces. Most settled and accessible section.
Middle GhizerGupis to Phander. Phander Lake (2,800m) is the centrepiece — a 5km glacial lake with poplar-lined shores and snow peaks. Most scenic stretch of the valley.
Upper GhizerPhander to Shandur Pass (3,734m). Alpine plateau country. Sparse population. The Shandur polo ground sits on a broad plateau with two glacial lakes. Road open July–October.
Polo Festival: The Shandur-Phander Polo Festival (mid-July, 3 days) is played on the world's highest polo ground at 3,734m. Teams from Chitral and Gilgit compete. Free entry. Thousands attend and camp on the plateau.
Practical Info

What to Know
Before You Go

Money & Connectivity

The only ATM in Ghizer is in Gahkuch. Withdraw enough cash in Gilgit before departure. Jazz mobile network reaches main towns (Gahkuch, Gupis, Phander) but disappears between towns and above Phander. No connectivity at Shandur.

Fuel

Petrol stations exist in Gahkuch and Phander only. If driving to Shandur, fill a full tank in Phander. Carry a spare jerry can for the plateau.

Accommodation

Simple guesthouses operate in every main town. Standards are basic — dal, chapati, egg, and a bed. PTDC Motel in Gahkuch is the most reliable. Phander Lake View Hotel is the best-positioned for scenery. At Shandur, accommodation only exists during the July festival (tent city). Outside festival season, Shandur is camping only.

Festival booking: If visiting during the July polo festival, book Phander or Teru accommodation months in advance. Everything fills up.
