Home/ ATM Locator
Cash is king in GB. Most markets, guesthouses, and smaller hotels are cash-only. There are no ATMs on trekking routes — Fairy Meadows, K2 Base Camp, Deosai. Withdraw in Gilgit or Skardu before departing. The maximum single withdrawal at most ATMs is Rs.25,000–50,000. HBL and UBL have the best network.
