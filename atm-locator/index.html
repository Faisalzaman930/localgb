Before you go remote: Withdraw cash in Gilgit or Skardu — there are no ATMs past Karimabad in Hunza or on any trekking route. Fill up petrol before Chilas northbound and before Skardu for Deosai. Carry basic medication; pharmacies thin out sharply after main towns.
