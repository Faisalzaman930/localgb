Budget Guide

GB On Any Budget

Forget what the agencies charge. Here's what GB actually costs — from backpacker hostels to comfortable guesthouses.

Backpacker
Rs.2,500 – 4,500/day
All-in · food, bed, transport
  • Dormitory guesthouses (Hunza, Skardu)
  • Local dhabas for every meal
  • Shared jeeps between valleys
  • No guided treks — self-navigate
  • Free camping at Fairy Meadows
Most Popular
Mid-Range
Rs.7,000 – 12,000/day
All-in · comfortable style
  • Private room in family guesthouses
  • Mix of local and tourist restaurants
  • Private jeep sharing with 3–4 people
  • Day guides for major treks
  • 1–2 night hotel upgrade in Hunza
Comfortable
Rs.18,000 – 30,000/day
All-in · no compromises
  • Serena Hotels or Eagle's Nest level
  • Full restaurant meals, no dhabas
  • Dedicated private jeep + driver
  • Full-time certified mountain guide
  • Porter support on all treks
