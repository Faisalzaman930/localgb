Why Gilgit-Baltistan

Where three great ranges collide

The Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindu Kush meet here - holding five of the world's fourteen 8,000-metre peaks, glaciers, turquoise lakes and Silk Road valleys. It's also one of the safest, most hospitable corners of Pakistan, and a favourite for solo and female travellers.
