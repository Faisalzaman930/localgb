# Operator shortlist for Faisal to vet (October 2026)

**What this is:** tour operators whose own website gives an office in Gilgit-Baltistan, with their public contact details. Checked on their websites on 20 October 2026.

**What it is not:** a recommendation. An office address shows where a company works, not who owns it or how it treats guests and staff. Only you can say "locally owned, and I trust them".

**How to publish one:** add it to `recommended_operators.json` with your own one-line "why", set `"approved": true`, then run `python3 recommended_operators.py`. It appears on the matching tour and agency pages as "Recommended by Faisal". Nothing shows until you approve it.

The three top Google competitors for "hunza tour packages" (Apricot Tours, Nature Adventure Club, Pakistan Tours) are left out on purpose, the same rule the site's research has always used.

## Questions worth asking before you approve anyone

1. Who owns the company, and where are they from?
2. Are the drivers, guides and porters local, and how are they paid? Porter wages and loads are a known issue on Baltoro treks.
3. Which hotels or guesthouses do they use? Family-run ones keep the money in the valleys.
4. What do they do about waste on treks and at campsites?
5. What is their cancellation and refund policy, in writing?
