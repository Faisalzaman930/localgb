## 2. Venue and attraction status
- **Serena in GB (H):** Serena lists six GB properties: Serena Altit Fort Residence, Hunza Serena Hotel (the photo caption still says "Hunza Serena Inn"), Gilgit Serena, Serena Shigar Fort, Serena Khaplu Palace and Sost Serena. Serena has no Skardu-town hotel. Source: https://serenahotels.com/ (read 16 Oct 2026).
  - One listing says the Hunza Serena Hotel opened under AKFED in 2024 (L): https://www.enprimeurclub.com/hotels/hunza-serena-hotel-aliabad-hotel. Older OTA listings use "hunza-serena-inn", so it is probably a rebrand or rebuild of the old Inn.
- **Shangrila Resort Skardu (M):** Still trading. No reports of closure or damage found.
  - Founded 1983, at Kachura: https://shangrilaresorts.com.pk/about-us (read 16 Oct 2026).
  - Day-visitor entry is PKR 1,000. That covers the lake, boating, zoo and hatchery, but not Pagoda or the DC-3 cafe. The source is undated, about 2024: https://blog.wego.com/shangrila-resort-skardu/.
  - Kachura Lake is about 20 km from Skardu city, 30–40 min (GB Tourism, M): https://e-tourism.gbit.gov.pk/tourist-destinations/kachura-lake-shangrila-lake.
- **Luxus Hunza Attabad, Himmel Skardu by Luxus, Ramada Resort by Wyndham Gilgit (M):** All are live on Booking, Expedia, Hotels.com and Wyndham.com in 2026. No closure news. Source: https://www.wyndhamhotels.com/ramada/gilgit-pakistan/ramada-resort-gilgit/overview.
- **Eagle's Nest Hotel, Duikar (M):** Operator listings are still live (https://www.wildfrontierstravel.com/en_AU/destination/pakistan/hotels/the-eagles-nest/5101). Founder Ainullah died July 2019 (gbee.pk). No closure news.
- **Hunza restaurants (M):** Wanderlog list, updated 27 Jul 2026 (https://wanderlog.com/list/geoCategory/311151/):
  - Listed with no closure flag: Café de Hunza, Hunza Food Pavilion by Laal Shehzadi, Kha Basi, Hidden Paradise, Pizza Pamir, Cafe Culture, Gingerfort.
  - **Jahaan Restaurant Gulmit is flagged "temporarily closed".**
  - **Glacier Breeze (Passu) is not on the list.** No closure report was found, but its 2026 status is unconfirmed (L).
- **Skardu and Gilgit restaurants:** Not checked one by one. No sources for them were found online, and no closure news either. Treat them as unverified.
- **Baltit Fort (M/L):**
  - On UNESCO's World Heritage *Tentative* List since 2004 (H): https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/pk.
  - Restoration finished in 1996. UNESCO Asia-Pacific Award for Excellence 2004 (H): https://the.akdn/en/where-we-work/south-asia/pakistan/conservation-and-development-in-gilgit-baltistan-pakistan.
  - Fee: PKR 1,000 for foreigners including guide, per a 2020–22 blog (L): https://backpackingman.com/?p=31909. No current official fee was found.
  - Hours: the site's 9am–5pm could not be confirmed.
- **Altit Fort (M):**
  - Built in the 11th century; the Shikari tower is about 1,100 years old. Open as a tourist site since 2007.
  - **As of April 2025 the fee was PKR 1,600 for non-local Pakistanis and foreigners (guide included) and PKR 200 for locals.** Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altit_Fort.
  - UNESCO Asia-Pacific Award of Distinction 2011; restoration finished 2010 (AKDN, H).
- **Shigar Fort (H):** UNESCO Asia-Pacific Award of Excellence **2006**; Virgin Responsible Tourism Award 2008 (AKDN link above). Run by Serena as a museum and hotel. GB Tourism lists no fee or hours and says to check with Serena: https://e-tourism.gbit.gov.pk/tourist-destinations/shigar-fort.
- **Khaplu Palace (H):** Restored palace opened July 2011; UNESCO Asia-Pacific Award 2013 (AKDN link above). No official day-visit fee was found.
- **Hussaini Bridge:**
  - Wikipedia: 194 m (635 ft), "Toll: yes", rebuilt after 2010, briefly sealed after a drowning in July 2022 (M): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hussaini_Suspension_Bridge.
  - Graana (2023/24): about PKR 200 entry, zipline PKR 2,000, length "~900–1,000 ft" (L): https://www.graana.com/blog/hussaini-bridge-a-detailed-guide/amp/.
  - Length estimates disagree, so publish none.
  - No reports of 2025–26 damage found.
- **Kharpocho Fort (M):** Built late 16th century by Ali Sher Khan Anchan; razed by Zorawar Singh in 1840; now partly ruined. A new access track was built in 2022 (propakistani.pk 5 Aug 2022). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skardu_Fort.
- **Manthokha Waterfall (M):** In Kharmang Valley, about 80 km from Skardu, about 55 m (180 ft) high: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manthokha_Waterfall. Kharmang is up the Indus (Skardu–Kharmang road), not the Shyok road to Khaplu.
- **Sarfaranga cold desert (M):** At the Shigar–Indus confluence, about 2,300 m. The jeep rally has run every year since 2017: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarfaranga_Desert. No confirmed 2026 rally dates found.
- **Places with nothing new found:** Borith, Upper Kachura, Katpana, Basho, Phander, Minimarg, Rama. No 2025–26 closure or damage reports turned up in the searches (L, absence of evidence).
- **Naltar (M):** On 4 Aug 2026, floods in upper Naltar damaged 12 houses, and the Naltar road was blocked at several points, stranding tourists: https://www.dawn.com/news/2020690/kkh-closed-ghizer-worst-hit-by-more-flash-floods (5 Aug 2026).

## 3. Events
- **Shandur Polo Festival (H). It has moved earlier in the year:**
  - 2024: started 8 July (three days): https://tribune.com.pk/story/2470883/polo-festival-from-july-8 (11 Jun 2024).
  - 2025: **20–23 June 2025**: https://tourismdept.gbit.gov.pk/pages/latest-events/32. Chitral won.
  - 2026: **11–13 June 2026**. GB beat Chitral 6–5 in the final on 13 June, ending Chitral's 12–13-year winning run.
    - https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2647638 (18 Jun 2026)
    - https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1226022 (14 Jun 2026)
    - https://www.thenews.pk/print/1420115-three-day-shandur-polo-festival-gets-underway (12 Jun 2026)
  - **2027:** No dates announced as of 16 Oct 2026. The last two years point to mid/late June, but July is possible. Say "June–July, dates announced in spring".
- **Jashn-e-Baharan Polo Tournament (M):** Aga Khan Shahi Polo Ground, Gilgit. GB Tourism lists 7 Mar 2026 but also says it is "usually 1st/2nd week of April": https://e-tourism.gbit.gov.pk/festivals-and-events/jashn-e-baharan-polo-tournament.
- **Cherry blossom (L):** No official 2026 "Cherry Blossom Festival" in Karimabad was confirmed. Present blossom season as a season, not a fixed festival.
- **Navroz (H):** 21 March, celebrated across Hunza, Gilgit and Baltistan: https://pamirtimes.net/2017/03/21/pictorial-navroz-celebration-in-karimabad-hunza/. GB Tourism lists Navroz but gives no date.
- **Ginani harvest festival (M):** Usually in June when the crops ripen; no fixed 2026 date: https://pamirtimes.net/2026/07/24/ginani-the-soul-of-hunza-the-hope-of-life/ (24 Jul 2026).
- **Winter festival (M):** The "Sevendays Frozen Fairy Tale Winter Feast" ran 15–25 Jan 2025, with Skardu (Hussainabad) on 19–20 Jan and Hunza (Murtazabad, ice-hockey finals) on 22–25 Jan: https://e-tourism.gbit.gov.pk/festivals-and-events/shandoor-polo-festival/sevendays-winter-feast-2025. The 2024 edition also ran 15–25 Jan: https://pamirtimes.net/?p=81083. No 2026 or 2027 details found.
- **Silk Route Festival (M):** The last major editions found were 2013–2014; nothing for 2025–26: https://pamirtimes.net/?p=37316. Do not present it as a current annual event.
- **Babusar, Khunjerab and Shimshal festivals:** No 2025–26 editions found (L). Shimshal's summer "Kuch" (yak and flock migration to the Pamir) is a tradition, not a ticketed event.

## 4. Administration
- **Chief Minister (H):** Amjad Hussain Azar (PPP), elected unopposed 22 Jun 2026 and sworn in **6 Jul 2026**.
  - https://www.thenews.pk/story/1424373-ppps-amjad-hussain-sworn-in-as-gilgit-baltistan-chief-minister
  - https://www.geo.tv/latest/671731-amjad-hussain-takes-oath-as-gilgit-baltistan-cm
- **Election (H):** GB Assembly election held **7 Jun 2026**, delayed from 24 Jan 2026. PPP 15, PML-N 9, IPP 7, PTI-backed independent 1, MWM 1 (33 seats). Caretaker CM before the vote: Justice (r) Yar Muhammad, from Nov 2025. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Gilgit_Baltistan_Assembly_election.
- **Governor (M):** Syed Mehdi Shah, appointed 2022 (https://www.dawn.com/news/1705163). He administered the July 2026 oath. The PPP–PML-N deal reportedly gives the governorship to PML-N, so this may change. Check again before publishing.
- **Capital (H):** Gilgit.
- **Divisions (H):** 3 — Gilgit, Baltistan, Diamer (Diamer–Astore).
- **Districts (M, sources conflict):**
  - 14 districts were notified in June 2019. Press still says "14 districts", e.g. TNS 2 Aug 2026: https://www.thenews.pk/tns/detail/1429427-year-after-year.
  - Only **10 districts are fully functional**:
    - Gilgit division: Gilgit, Ghizer, Hunza, Nagar
    - Baltistan division: Skardu, Shigar, Kharmang, Ghanche
    - Diamer division: Diamer, Astore
  - The 4 notified but not yet operational are Gupis-Yasin, Roundu, Darel and Tangir. Wikipedia lists no DCs for them, and a GB Tourism event in Jan 2024 was held "across all ten districts".
    - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_districts_in_Gilgit-Baltistan
    - https://www.pakistantoday.com.pk/2019/06/17/gb-notifies-four-more-districts-total-number-of-districts-now-14/
  - Suggested wording: "10 districts (14 notified)".
- **Population (H):** 1,709,049 (2023 census). Area 72,496 km². Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilgit-Baltistan.
- **Languages (H):** Urdu and English are official. Regional languages: Shina, Balti, Burushaski, Wakhi, Khowar, Domaki, Purgi.
- **National parks (H):** 7 — Khunjerab, Deosai, Central Karakoram (CKNP), Himalaya, Nanga Parbat, Qurumbar, Shandur. Deosai was declared in 1993, as the site says. Khunjerab NP is about 2,269 km², so the site's "~2,200 km²" is acceptable.
- **UNESCO (H):** GB has **no inscribed World Heritage Site**. Tentative List: Baltit Fort (2004), Central Karakorum NP (2016), Deosai NP (2016): https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/pk.

## 5. Natural hazards and road news, 2025–26
- **Shishper / Hassanabad (M):** A GLOF from the Shishper glacier lake about 8 Aug 2025 caused the worst Hassanabad Nullah flood since 2018. It took out a KKH section, damaged Aliabad water channels, destroyed a bridge at Tokorkot and damaged the Shimshal road: https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/world%2Fglacial-fury-in-pok-floods-sever-karakoram-highway-landslide-kills-nine-3675363 (11 Aug 2025). The 2025 monsoon cut July–Aug bookings by about 90%: https://english.aaj.tv/news/amp/330429239 (13 Aug 2025). No specific 2026 Shishper surge report found.
- **2026 monsoon (H):**
  - June: Shigar floods.
  - July: Chhamugarh cloudburst temporarily dammed the Gilgit River; the Jaglot–Skardu road lost 2 bridges and was closed for more than a week, stranding tourists in Skardu.
  - Hisper road in Nagar washed out; Astore road closed at Khachak.
  - Upper Shigar trekking was halted.
  - Source: https://www.thenews.pk/tns/detail/1429427-year-after-year (2 Aug 2026).
- **4 Aug 2026 (H):**
  - Ishkoman (Mayon) flood created a lake at Hasis; the Ghizer–Ishkoman road was cut.
  - KKH near Gulmit was damaged by Juchar Nallah and later reopened.
  - KKH closed near Chilas (Gini).
  - Source: Dawn, 5 Aug 2026 (link above).
- **~5 Sep 2026 (M):** KKH Sost–Khunjerab closed by landslides; Murtazabad–Hassanabad blocked and then reopened: https://asianews.network/?p=299285 (7 Sep 2026).
- **Khunjerab Pass (M):** In Dec 2024, Pakistan and China agreed to keep it open year-round for trade: https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2024/12/03/khunjerab-pass-achieves-milestone-with-year-round-operation/. Tourist access in winter is still not assured.
- **Attabad Lake and Passu glacier:** No new 2025–26 change found (L).
