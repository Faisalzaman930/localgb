## 2. Road works & corridor status

- **KKH relocation at Dasu (RKKH-1):** a 20.4 km relocated section with 7 tunnels and 3 bridges. WAPDA targeted opening to traffic in **June 2026**. As of 10 Sep 2026 the **opening is NOT confirmed**. The Barseen arch bridge (225 m, Pakistan's longest arch) and the Dogah arch bridge are complete, and work continues. The wider alternative stretch Kamila–Summer Nullah is ~52–62 km with 15 tunnels. **[NEW]** Conf: Med.
  - https://www.brecorder.com/news/40384781 (2025-09-27)
  - https://www.nation.com.pk/10-Sep-2026/ccecc-completes-barseen-bridge-dasu (2026-09-10)
  - https://propakistani.pk/2025/06/30/new-section-of-karakoram-highway-to-remain-open-even-during-landslides-and-floods/amp/ (2025-06-30)
- **Thakot–Raikot KKH realignment (CPEC):** 241 km, ~$2 bn, with China funding 85%. Phase 1 is the ~82 km stretch affected by Diamer-Bhasha. FY2026-27 PSDP gives only **Rs 5 bn** for a 102 km stretch, against an estimated total of Rs 502.6 bn. **No construction underway. Years away; do not mention as a travel factor except "realignment planned".** **[NEW]** Conf: Med.
  - https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/?p=213453 (undated, ~2025)
  - https://www.pakwheels.com/blog/5-bn-karakoram-highway-relocation-psdp-2026-27/ (2026-09-23)
- **KKH km 350–467 repair programme** plus rigid pavement at Tattapani. Recurring mudflow at **km 426–427 (Gini, Chilas)**. **N-15 Naran–Babusar–Chilas km 186–240**: structural overlay planned, with slides at km 214–229 near Lushi. Slide shelters are proposed for the S-1. **No completion dates.** **[NEW]** Conf: High. https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1276756 (2026-09-14)
- **New road projects announced (Apr 2026):** Astore–Chilam Chowki (54 km) and Deosai–Skardu (31 km). No timeline. **[NEW]** Conf: Med. https://www.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/04/17/federal-govt-announces-new-road-projects-in-gb (2026-04-17)
- **Kaghan (N-15):** the widened steel **Mahandri Bridge**, the key Kaghan–Naran link, is reported eroding (May 2026). Earlier versions of this bridge washed away in recent floods. Flag it as a monsoon weak point. **[NEW]** Conf: Med. https://www.thenews.pk/print/1413069-kaghan-valley-mahandri-bridge-faces-safety-risks-amid-erosion (2026-05-01)

### Khunjerab (Sost border)
- **Officially year-round since 1 Dec 2024.** This replaced the 1985 protocol window of 1 Apr–30 Nov. **[CHANGED]**: FACTS says "closed ~Dec 30–Apr 1; year-round still transitioning". Conf: High.
  - https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/traders-welcome-decision-to-keep-khunjerab-pass-linking-pakistan-china-open-year-round-2581513 (2024-12-02)

- **Winter 2025-26:** cargo trade ran "uninterrupted for the 2nd consecutive year". Sost dry port handled 12,000 t in Dec 2025 and 11,000 t in early Jan 2026. Passenger and tourist crossings in winter are **not documented**. **[NEW]** Conf: Med (NLC press release). https://www.dailyindependent.com.pk/2026/01/21/a-shining-example-of-national-service-nlc-dry-port-sost-ensures-uninterrupted-trade-for-second-consecutive-year/ (2026-01-21)

Wikipedia still quotes the historic closures (heavy vehicles 30 Nov–1 May; all vehicles 30 Dec–1 Apr). Conf: Med. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khunjerab_Pass (accessed 2026-10-16)
- **Border hours [NEW]:** **Mon–Fri only**. Pakistani side ~09:00–17:00 PKT; Chinese side ~11:00–20:00 Beijing time. Closed on Pakistani and Chinese public holidays. A Chinese visa is required in advance (no visa on arrival). You cannot self-drive in China; a group or guide is required. Sost–Tashkurgan takes most of a day. Conf: Med. https://www.saigatours.com/article/A-guide-to-Pakistans-land-borders-in-2026 (2026-07-10)

## 6. Major disruptions 2025–2026 (affect advice)
- **Mar 2025:** KKH at **Lotar, Upper Kohistan** had 5 landslide blockages in 10 days, with deaths. Conf: High. https://www.dawn.com/news/1897233/karakoram-highway-blocked-in-lotar-area-of-kohistan-due-to-landslide (2025-03-11)

- **19 Jan 2026:** an **Mw 5.6 earthquake** hit Chapursan/upper Hunza, killing 2. The **KKH was blocked by landslides and reopened**. The Chapursan road was cut at multiple points. Conf: High. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Gilgit-Baltistan_earthquake

- **Jun 2026:** S-1 blocked by landslides near **Maloopa, Roundu**. (PakWheels 2026-09-30)

  - **Hisper road (Nagar)** was cut for ~2 months by repeated GLOFs.
  - The Naltar, Haramosh, Astore (Khachak) and Hushe (Rout Longba) roads were closed at times.
