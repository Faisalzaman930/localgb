# GB Guide fact sheet: prices and costs (refresh of 16 Oct 2026)

Scope: what travel in Gilgit-Baltistan costs in Oct 2026, in PKR with USD.
**FX: USD 1 = PKR 277.0.** Sources: open.er-api.com live feed, updated 16 Oct 2026 00:02 UTC (276.98); ARY interbank close of 6 Oct 2026 (Rs 277.05; the article misprints it as "Rs.77.05"), https://arynews.tv/pakistani-rupee-gains-against-us-dollar. USD figures below are rounded at 277.
Confidence levels: **H** = official, or two or more recent sources agree. **M** = one recent source that looks credible. **L** = a single, older, inconsistent or anecdotal source. "(2025)" means the figure is pre-2026 and should be treated as a floor, because fuel rose sharply in 2026.

General context: 2026 fuel prices have been very volatile. Petrol jumped to Rs 458.40/L and HSD to Rs 520.35/L on 3 Apr 2026 (https://keralakaumudi.com/en/news/news-amp.php?id=1726194, Middle East conflict) and have since fallen back to about Rs 394–399. Any transport price written before 2026 is likely too low.
