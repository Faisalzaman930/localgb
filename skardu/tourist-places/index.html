How to Plan

Sorting the
Day Trips

The attractions around Skardu fall naturally into two groups. The close ones — Satpara Lake, Kharpocho Fort, and the Katpana cold desert — are all within 17km of the city and easy to combine into a single relaxed day. Shangrila Resort and Upper Kachura Lake sit a little further out at 35 to 50km, but lie on the same road, so they pair well together as a second day.

The bigger excursions need their own day each. Shigar Fort is 60km away, and Deosai Plains and Khaplu Palace are full-day trips reached by longer drives, with Deosai requiring a 4WD. If your time is short, prioritise the free, high-impact stops first — Kharpocho Fort for the views and the cold desert for its strangeness — and treat the distant palaces and the plateau as the reasons to stay longer.

FAQ

Common
Questions

Which tourist places in Skardu are free to enter?

Kharpocho Fort, the Katpana cold desert, Upper Kachura Lake, and Basho Valley all have free entry.

What is the closest attraction to Skardu city?

Satpara Lake at 9km, about a 15-minute drive.

Which places can I combine in one day?

Satpara Lake, Kharpocho Fort, and the Katpana cold desert are all close together and easy to do in a single day.

Do I need a 4WD for any of these places?

Yes, Deosai Plains requires a 4WD and is reached by a longer drive of about 2.5 hours or more.

How far is Khaplu Palace from Skardu?

It is 180km away, around a 3.5-hour drive, so it is best treated as a full-day trip.
