Travel Guide

How to Reach
Skardu

The most important thing to know: the flights cancel 40-60% of the time. Here is how to plan around that.

✈ Fly: 1h from ISB
🚗 Road: 270km from Gilgit
⚠ Frequent cancellations
📍 IATA: KDU
Home/Skardu/How to Reach Skardu
By Air

Fastest but
Unreliable

AirlinesPIA, Air Sial, Serene Air. From Islamabad (ISB) and Lahore (LHE).
Duration~1h from Islamabad. ~1.5h from Lahore.
CostRs.7,000-15,000 one way from ISB. Rs.9,000-18,000 from Lahore.
Cancellation rate40-60% in cloudy months. Visual flight rules only — any mountain cloud = cancelled.
Critical ruleNEVER book international connections within 72h of a Skardu domestic flight. Always allow 2 extra days.
RefundFull refund or free reschedule on weather cancellations. PIA and Air Sial both honour this.
By Road

Guaranteed
Option

From Gilgit270km on Gilgit-Skardu road via Indus gorge. 7-9h. NATCO bus Rs.600-800. Shared jeep Rs.400-600/person.
From Islamabad600km, 20-22h. Via KKH to Gilgit (12h) then Gilgit-Skardu road (8h). Overnight in Chilas or Gilgit recommended.
Road conditionsGenerally good. Indus gorge section is dramatic. Some narrow sections and rockfall zones.
