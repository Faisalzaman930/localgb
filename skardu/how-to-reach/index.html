How to Reach
Skardu

The most important thing to know: the flights are weather-dependent and cancel often. Here is how to plan around that.

🚗 Road: 4-5h from Gilgit
⚠ Frequent cancellations

Home/Skardu/How to Reach Skardu
By Faisal Zaman·Local from Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026
By Air

Fastest but
Unreliable

AirlinesPIA (privately owned since June 2026), Airblue and AirSial fly Islamabad (ISB) → Skardu (KDU), all on A320 jets, roughly 13-21 flights a week (fewer in winter). PIA also flies Karachi direct. Confirm current routes and any Lahore (LHE) service before booking, as schedules change seasonally. There are no Gilgit-Skardu flights.
Duration~1h-1h 10min from Islamabad. ~1.5h from Lahore. ~2h 20min from Karachi.
CostRs.18,000-30,000 one way from ISB in 2026 (peak season higher). Lahore and Karachi fares are higher - check live.
Cancellation rateHigh and weather-dependent in cloudy months - mountain cloud routinely grounds flights for days at a stretch. Always keep a road backup.
Critical ruleNEVER book international connections within 72h of a Skardu domestic flight. Always allow 2 extra days.
RefundAirlines normally offer a refund or free rebooking on weather cancellations - check your fare rules when booking.
By Road

Dependable
Option

From Gilgit~200-210km door to door on the Jaglot-Skardu road (S-1) via the Indus gorge. 4-5h, plus 1-2h while 2026 flood repairs continue. NATCO buses and shared vans run daily; fares rose sharply in 2025-26, so confirm at the counter.
From Islamabad~635-646km, 16-18h by private vehicle (bus ~18-24h, ~Rs.5,500-6,200 in 2026). Via KKH toward Gilgit/Jaglot, then the Jaglot-Skardu road (4-5h for the final leg). Overnight in Chilas or Gilgit recommended. (Landslides and checkposts can add several hours.) The UK FCDO advises against all travel on the KKH Mansehra-Chilas section; foreigners following that advice use the Naran-Babusar road (open roughly late May/June to Oct/Nov) or fly.
Road conditionsGenerally good, but the S-1 still had flood-damaged sections in Sep 2026 (a diversion, one-lane stretches and a Bailey bridge), and late July-mid August floods closed it for days in both 2025 and 2026. Some narrow sections and rockfall zones.
Local Tips

Planning Around
the Weather

The single most important fact about reaching Skardu is that the flights are weather-dependent and cancel a great deal of the time, especially in cloudy and winter months. The aircraft fly under visual rules through the mountains, so any significant cloud means the flight does not go. This is well established and not bad luck; it is simply how flying into Skardu works.

The practical response is to build slack into your plan. Never connect an international flight within 72 hours of a Skardu domestic flight, and always allow two spare days at each end. If the flight cancels, you get a full refund or a free reschedule, but you still need somewhere to be - so it helps to treat the road as your real backup rather than an emergency. The Gilgit-Skardu road is the dependable option outside monsoon closures, and many seasoned travellers simply fly one direction and drive the other to be sure of seeing the Indus gorge regardless.

FAQ

Common
Questions

How long is the flight to Skardu?

About 1 hour from Islamabad and around 1.5 hours from Lahore.

How often do Skardu flights get cancelled?

Often, especially in cloudy and winter months - there is no reliable official rate. The flights depend on mountain visibility, so cloud can ground them for days.

Do I get a refund if my flight is cancelled?

Usually. Airlines normally offer a refund or free rebooking on weather cancellations, but check your fare rules when you book.

How do I reach Skardu by road?

From Gilgit it is roughly 200-210km and 4 to 5 hours on the Jaglot-Skardu road via the Indus gorge (allow 1-2 hours extra for 2026 flood repairs). From Islamabad it is about 635-646km and 16 to 18 hours by private vehicle, usually with an overnight stop in Chilas or Gilgit.

What is the safest way to plan my trip?

Allow two buffer days and never book an international connection within 72 hours of a Skardu domestic flight, since cancellations are common.
