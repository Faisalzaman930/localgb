Skardu

Gateway to K2 and the world's greatest concentration of high peaks. Ancient forts, cold desert, and rivers the colour of glacial silt.

Overview

The Capital of
Baltistan

Skardu sits at the confluence of the Indus and Shigar rivers at 2,228m. It's the largest town in Baltistan and the administrative base for all K2 expeditions. But Skardu is more than a staging post — the valley itself is extraordinary.

The Katpana Cold Desert — one of the highest cold deserts in the world — sits just east of town. The Deosai Plains, two hours south by jeep, are a high-altitude plateau at 4,114m covered in wildflowers each July. Shigar Fort, now a heritage hotel, is one of the finest examples of Baltistani architecture still standing.

Most trekkers pass through Skardu quickly on their way to K2. That's a mistake. Give it three days minimum.

Getting There

How to Reach
Skardu

Book flights early. PIA and AirSial both fly Islamabad–Skardu. Flights cancel for weather frequently in winter. Buy a flexible ticket or allow 2 extra days buffer.
By airDirect flights from Islamabad to Skardu (SKT), ~1h 15m. Rs. 10,000–18,000 one way. Book weeks in advance in peak season.
By roadFrom Islamabad via KKH to Raikot, then south via Chilas — approximately 20–24 hours. Scenic but extremely long. Break at Chilas overnight.
From GilgitShared jeep Rs. 700–1,000 (4–5h via Jaglot). Private jeep Rs. 12,000–18,000. NATCO bus runs daily Rs. 500.
From HunzaGo via Gilgit (~3h) then Skardu (~5h) = full day journey. Or fly Gilgit–Skardu in 20 minutes.
NOC permitsRequired for Deosai National Park entry (obtainable at the park gate). K2 trekkers need climbing/trekking permits from Ministry of Tourism in Islamabad.
