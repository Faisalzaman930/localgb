What to Order

Skardu Food
to Try

TroutRiver trout fried or curried. Rs.400-800/fish. Best thing to eat in Skardu.
Balti KarahiSlow-cooked mutton or chicken, subtler spicing than Punjab karahi. Rs.600-1,200.
ChapshuroMeat-stuffed flatbread from street stalls. Rs.150-300.
SkiuHandmade pasta in broth. Traditional winter dish. Ask specifically.
Daal ChawalUniversal fallback. Rs.200-350.
