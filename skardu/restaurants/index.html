I Ate Around Skardu to Find Its Best Restaurants for 2026

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026

Skardu's food is its own quiet pleasure: fresh trout from the rivers, slow-cooked Balti karahi, apricot-sweet desserts, and a handful of cafes with a Shangri-La view. I'm from Gilgit-Baltistan, so here is the honest list of where to actually eat in 2026, not a copy-paste of the same three names.

I've ranked real, currently-open places on the food, the setting, value and consistency, and shown each one's real rating where it has a public one.

How I picked these Skardu restaurants

I went through the places that are actually open and worth your time in Skardu, judging each on the food, the setting, value and consistency rather than hype. 7 of 9 have a real public rating, which I show with the review count and a maps link; the rest are local spots I rate from experience, shown with a dash rather than an invented score.

The 9 best, reviewed

#1

The Grind Cafe and Eatery

Cafe / Continental / Malay-Chinese fusion · Heart of Skardu city
📍 Heart of Skardu city🍴 Cafe / Continental / Malay-Chinese fusion💷 $$★ 5.0 Tripadvisor (4)

Must try: Yak burger and specialty coffee with cake

What's good

  • Rare proper espresso-based coffee and baked cakes in Skardu
  • Novel yak burger you won't find easily elsewhere
  • Clean, cozy, comfortable with WiFi

Could be better

  • Very few reviews so far (new-ish spot)
  • More of a Western cafe break than a local-food experience
My take: The best Western-style coffee-and-cake stop in town and a fun yak-burger novelty - ideal for a mid-day break.
#2

Food Street Api Xhoq

Balti / Pakistani / Chinese / fast food (BBQ) · Skardu (open-air venue)
📍 Skardu (open-air venue)🍴 Balti / Pakistani / Chinese / fast food (BBQ)💷 $$★ 5.0 Tripadvisor (3)

Must try: BBQ platter in the open-air setting

What's good

  • Open-air, natural setting with BBQ and bonfire facilities
  • Wide menu spanning Balti, Pakistani, Chinese and fast food
  • Relaxed atmosphere good for groups

Could be better

  • Very few reviews to go on
  • Open-air format is weather/season dependent
My take: A laid-back open-air BBQ spot - nice for an evening with a fire if the weather is good.
#3

Mamtu De Skardu

Balti traditional (mamtu/dumplings) · Skardu (small local eatery)
📍 Skardu (small local eatery)🍴 Balti traditional (mamtu/dumplings)💷 $★ 4.6 Google (91)

Must try: Mamtu (steamed Balti meat dumplings) with sauce

What's good

What to eat in Skardu

  • Trout - river or farmed, usually grilled or fried; the regional signature.
  • Balti karahi and mamtu (steamed dumplings), prapu and balay (buckwheat noodles).
  • Apricot everything - oil, jam, dried fruit and the famous apricot-kernel desserts.
  • Organic, slow food - Skardu cooks for taste, not speed; order ahead for trout or karahi.

FAQ

What food is Skardu famous for?

Fresh trout, Balti karahi, steamed mamtu dumplings, buckwheat prapu/balay, and apricot-based sweets. Most of it is organic and cooked slowly, so order ahead.

Where do tourists eat in Skardu?

Around the main bazaar and the Satpara/Kachura road, plus the view cafes and hotel restaurants. See the ranked list above.

Is Skardu good for vegetarians?

Yes - daal, seasonal vegetables, naan and rice are everywhere, though trout and karahi are the highlights. Tell the cook in advance for the best vegetarian spread.

How much does a meal cost in Skardu?

A simple local meal runs a few hundred rupees; trout and full karahi at a mid-range place cost more. Prices are marked $ (budget) to $$$ (upscale) in the table.
