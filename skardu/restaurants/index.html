I Ate Around Skardu to Find Its Best Restaurants for 2026

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026

Skardu's food is its own quiet pleasure: fresh trout from the rivers, slow-cooked Balti karahi, apricot-sweet desserts, and a handful of cafes with a Shangri-La view. I'm from Gilgit-Baltistan, so here is the honest list of where to actually eat in 2026, not a copy-paste of the same three names.

I've ranked real, currently-open places on the food, the setting, value and consistency, and shown each one's real rating where it has a public one.

Try something you can't get elsewhere

The dishes worth crossing Skardu for - genuinely local to Gilgit-Baltistan, not the usual Pakistani menu:

  • Chapshoro and mamtu made the traditional way - at Food Street Api Xhoq Skardu
  • Yak burger (local yak meat) - at The Grind Cafe and Eatery
  • Mamtu (Balti steamed dumplings) - at Mamtu De Skardu
  • Balay (handmade Balti wheat-noodle soup) and traditional Balti prapu-style dishes - at Dewanekhas Restaurant Skardu
  • Grilled Kachura trout - at Shangrila Resort - Pagoda Restaurant

What to eat in Skardu

  • Trout - river or farmed, usually grilled or fried; the regional signature.
  • Balti karahi and mamtu (steamed dumplings), prapu and balay (buckwheat noodles).
  • Apricot everything - oil, jam, dried fruit and the famous apricot-kernel desserts.
  • Organic, slow food - Skardu cooks for taste, not speed; order ahead for trout or karahi.

FAQ

What food is Skardu famous for?

Fresh trout, Balti karahi, steamed mamtu dumplings, buckwheat prapu/balay, and apricot-based sweets. Most of it is organic and cooked slowly, so order ahead.

Where do tourists eat in Skardu?

Around the main bazaar and the Satpara/Kachura road, plus the view cafes and hotel restaurants. See the ranked list above.

Is Skardu good for vegetarians?

Yes - daal, seasonal vegetables, naan and rice are everywhere, though trout and karahi are the highlights. Tell the cook in advance for the best vegetarian spread.

How much does a meal cost in Skardu?

A simple local meal runs a few hundred rupees; trout and full karahi at a mid-range place cost more. Prices are marked $ (budget) to $$$ (upscale) in the table.

Where can I try Chapshoro and mamtu made the traditional way in Skardu?

Food Street Api Xhoq Skardu is the spot locals point to. See the full list above for other genuinely local dishes and where to find them.
