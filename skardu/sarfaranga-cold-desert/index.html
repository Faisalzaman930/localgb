Sarfaranga
Cold Desert

What it isA separate cold desert from Katpana: it lies across the Indus, where the Shigar River meets the Indus on the road to Shigar.
Unique featureSand dunes at about 2,300m surrounded by snow peaks. In winter snow falls on the sand itself.
AccessOn the Skardu-Shigar road, an easy add-on to a Shigar day trip (confirm drive time locally). Free entry.
ActivitiesWalking the dunes, jeep rides (agree the price first), camping (bring warm gear). The Sarfaranga jeep rally has run most years since 2017; dates vary.
PhotographyGolden hour for dramatic dune shadows. Early morning for snow on dunes in winter.
What to See

Sand Dunes
Under Snow Peaks

Sarfaranga is often confused with Katpana, but it is a separate cold desert across the Indus, where the Shigar valley opens into the Skardu valley on the road to Shigar. What makes it strange and worth the trip is the contradiction: genuine sand dunes sitting at around 2,300m, ringed by snow-capped peaks. In winter, snow actually falls on the sand itself, which is the kind of sight that does not really make sense until you stand in it.

There is no entry fee, so it is an easy, low-commitment outing. You can simply walk the dunes, take a short jeep ride across them (agree the price first), or camp out - though if you camp, bring genuinely warm gear, because the altitude bites once the sun drops. Photographers should aim for golden hour, when the low light throws long shadows across the dunes, and early winter mornings for snow lying on the sand.

FAQ

Common
Questions

Is Sarfaranga the same as Katpana Desert?

No. They are often mixed up, but Sarfaranga is a separate cold desert at the Shigar-Indus confluence, across the river from Katpana (which is near Skardu airport).

How far is it from Skardu?

It sits on the Skardu-Shigar road, so it fits easily into a Shigar day trip - confirm the drive time locally.

Is there an entry fee?

No, entry is free.

What can you do there?

Walk the dunes, take a jeep ride across them (agree the price first), or camp - bring warm gear if you stay overnight.

When is the best time for photos?

Golden hour gives dramatic shadows across the dunes, and early winter mornings can show snow lying on the sand.
