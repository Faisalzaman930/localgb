What to Do
in Skardu

Fort · Free · 45min

Kharpocho Fort

Climb 45 minutes to this 16th-century fort for 360° views: Indus River, cold desert, and K2 on clear days.

Resort · 35km

Shangrila Lake Boat Trip

Row a boat on Lower Kachura Lake inside Shangrila Resort grounds. Entry Rs.500, boats Rs.300-500.

Desert · 17km

Katpana Cold Desert

Pakistan's only cold desert: sand dunes at 2,226m, glacial peaks overhead. Free entry. Dawn and dusk visits best.

Plateau · 2.5-3h drive

Deosai National Park

Brown bears, wildflowers, cobalt glacial lakes at 4,114m. The most extraordinary day trip from Skardu.

Heritage · 60km

Shigar Fort Visit

400-year-old Balti royal fort with exceptional carved woodwork. Day visit Rs.500.

History · City

K2 Museum

Document K2 expedition history, summit attempts, and Karakoram mountaineering culture. Rs.100-200.
