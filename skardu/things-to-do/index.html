What to Do
in Skardu

Fort · Free · 45min

Kharpocho Fort

Climb 45 minutes to this 16th-century fort for 360° views: Indus River, cold desert, and K2 on clear days.

Resort · 35km

Shangrila Lake Boat Trip

Row a boat on Lower Kachura Lake inside Shangrila Resort grounds. Entry Rs.500, boats Rs.300-500.

Desert · 17km

Katpana Cold Desert

Pakistan's only cold desert: sand dunes at 2,226m, glacial peaks overhead. Free entry. Dawn and dusk visits best.

Plateau · 2.5-3h drive

Deosai National Park

Brown bears, wildflowers, cobalt glacial lakes at 4,114m. The most extraordinary day trip from Skardu.

Heritage · 60km

Shigar Fort Visit

400-year-old Balti royal fort with exceptional carved woodwork. Day visit Rs.500.

History · City

K2 Museum

Document K2 expedition history, summit attempts, and Karakoram mountaineering culture. Rs.100-200.

How to Plan

Building Your
Days

Skardu is a destination most visitors underestimate, and the activities span a wide range of effort. Some, like climbing Kharpocho Fort for its 360-degree views or wandering the Katpana cold desert at dawn, are free and close to the city. Others — a boat trip on Lower Kachura Lake at Shangrila, or the K2 Museum in town — are easy add-ons that cost little.

The two activities that need a full day to themselves are Deosai National Park and Shigar Fort. Deosai is a 2.5 to 3 hour drive by 4WD onto a high plateau where brown bears, wildflowers, and glacial lakes wait at over 4,000m, and it is the standout day trip. Shigar Fort, 60km out, rewards anyone interested in heritage and craftsmanship. Mix one big excursion with a couple of close, free stops and you have a full, varied few days.

FAQ

Common
Questions

What free things are there to do in Skardu?

Climbing Kharpocho Fort for its views and visiting the Katpana cold desert are both free.

What is the best day trip from Skardu?

Deosai National Park, a 2.5 to 3 hour drive by 4WD, with brown bears, wildflowers, and glacial lakes at over 4,000m.

Can you go boating in Skardu?

Yes. You can row a boat on Lower Kachura Lake inside Shangrila Resort. Entry is Rs.500 and boats run Rs.300 to Rs.500.

How much does the cold desert jeep cost?

A jeep return to the Katpana cold desert runs about Rs.2,000 to Rs.3,500.

Is there anything to do in Skardu town itself?

Yes, the K2 Museum covers expedition history and Karakoram mountaineering culture, with entry around Rs.100 to Rs.200.
