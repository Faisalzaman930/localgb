I Checked Out Where to Stay in Skardu - the Best Hotels for 2026

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026

Where you stay in Skardu shapes the trip: a lakeside resort, a town hotel near the bazaar, or a budget guesthouse close to the jeep stands for Deosai and K2. I'm from Gilgit-Baltistan, so here's the honest ranked list for 2026 across every budget.

I've ranked real, open places on location, comfort, value and view, and shown each one's real public rating where it has one.

How I picked these Skardu hotels

I went through the places actually worth booking in Skardu across luxury, mid-range and budget, judging each on location, comfort, value and the view. 8 of 10 have a real public rating, shown with the review count and a maps link; the rest I rate from local knowledge and mark with a dash rather than invent a score.

The 10 best, reviewed

#1

Arish Luxury Suites

Luxury · Skardu (near town, with airport transfers)
📍 Skardu (near town, with airport transfers)🏨 Luxury💷 $$$9.2/10 Booking.com

Why stay: Skardu's best-reviewed modern luxury hotel: spacious, elegant rooms and strong food

What's good

  • Very spacious, elegantly decorated rooms with large bathrooms
  • Exceptional staff who arrange tours and drivers
  • Tasty, varied breakfast and dinner

Could be better

  • Premium pricing for Skardu
  • Books out fast in the July-August peak
My take: If you want polished modern luxury inside Skardu itself rather than a heritage fort, this is the standout, with a 5/5 Tripadvisor rank of #4 to match.
#2

Byarsa Hotel Skardu

Mid-range · Ponedas, Skardu
📍 Ponedas, Skardu🏨 Mid-range💷 $$8.8/10 Booking.com

Why stay: Stylish newer hotel with a garden and terrace, strong on design and breakfast

What's good

  • Beautifully designed, modern rooms (some with mountain views)
  • Delicious, varied breakfast praised by guests
  • Garden, terrace and friendly staff

Could be better

  • Some mixed reviews on staff/policies across platforms
  • Booking score (8.8) cited is the couples rating
My take: A good-looking, well-reviewed modern option that punches above typical Skardu mid-range stays on design and food.
#3

Serena Shigar Fort

Luxury · Shigar village (~30 km / 45 min from Skardu)
📍 Shigar village (~30 km / 45 min from Skardu)🏨 Luxury💷 $$$8.7/10 Booking.com (45)

Why stay: Restored 17th-century Raja's fort run as an Aga Khan heritage hotel, with apricot gardens against the Karakoram

What's good

  • Genuine 400-year-old fort architecture, beautifully preserved
  • Excellent food and fresh apricots from the garden
  • Friendly, attentive staff and serene location

Could be better

  • It's in Shigar, not Skardu town, so you trade convenience for atmosphere
  • No AC/TV and patchy in-room internet
My take: The most atmospheric stay in the region and the clear top pick for a splurge, as long as you're happy to be based outside Skardu town.
#4

Hotel One Skardu

Mid-range · Near Skardu Airport / town
📍 Near Skardu Airport / town🏨 Mid-range💷 $$★ 4.0 Tripadvisor (67)

Why stay: Consistent, business-grade chain reliability and the #1 Tripadvisor rank in Skardu

What's good

  • Dependable, standardised rooms and service (part of a known PC chain)
  • Friendly staff, good food and included breakfast
  • Convenient airport location

Could be better

  • Only ~12 rooms, so it fills quickly
  • Functional rather than scenic; views aren't the draw
My take: The safe, no-surprises mid-range choice and currently Tripadvisor's #1 in Skardu, best if you value consistency over character.
#5

Hotel Himalaya Skardu

Guesthouse · Skardu (garden setting with mountain views)
📍 Skardu (garden setting with mountain views)🏨 Guesthouse💷 $$★ 4.0 Tripadvisor (11)

Why stay: Warm, family-run feel with a garden and Karakoram/Himalaya views

What's good

  • Friendly, cooperative staff and comfortable rooms
  • Beautiful garden and mountain views
  • Well located for Skardu's main sights

Could be better

  • Small review count, so the rating is less robust
  • Specialty lodging, so fewer hotel-grade amenities
My take: A pleasant, personable guesthouse-style stay; one reviewer rated it above Skardu's 5-star option, though sample sizes are small.
#6

Hotel Mountain Lodge Skardu

Mid-range · Hillside above Skardu valley (out of town)
📍 Hillside above Skardu valley (out of town)🏨 Mid-range💷 $$★ 4.0 Tripadvisor

Why stay: Hillside perch with some of the best valley views in Skardu, quiet and away from town noise

What's good

  • Elevated position gives sweeping sunrise/sunset valley views
  • Clean, well-run with polite staff and its own restaurant
  • Free airport shuttle and parking

Could be better

  • Out-of-town location means you need transport for the bazaar
  • Ranks mid-pack (#29) on Tripadvisor
My take: The pick for travellers who prize the view and quiet over being walkable to town; reliable mid-range comfort.
#7

Shangrila Resort Skardu

Luxury · Lower Kachura Lake (~20 km / 35 min from Skardu)
📍 Lower Kachura Lake (~20 km / 35 min from Skardu)🏨 Luxury💷 $$$★ 3.0 Tripadvisor (132)

Why stay: Pakistan's most photographed resort, on the heart-shaped Lower Kachura Lake with the famous grounded-aircraft restaurant

What's good

  • Iconic, genuinely spectacular lakeside setting
  • The aeroplane restaurant and lake views are one-of-a-kind
  • Established (1983) and reliably operating

Could be better

  • Widely seen as overpriced for the dated rooms and patchy service
  • Ranks low (#37 of 44) on Tripadvisor despite the fame
My take: Go for the unbeatable scenery and the experience, not the rooms; many find a day visit or meal better value than an overnight.
#8

Baltistan Continental Hotel

Budget · Skardu (between downtown and outlying sights)
📍 Skardu (between downtown and outlying sights)🏨 Budget💷 $★ 3.0 Tripadvisor

Why stay: Budget-friendly hotel with an in-house antique museum and buffet dining

What's good

  • Good-value pricing with breakfast/dinner buffets
  • In-house antique museum and mountain-view terrace rooms
  • Convenient base for exploring the city

Could be better

  • Mixed reviews: some report congested rooms and unclean washrooms
  • Food quality is hit-or-miss
My take: A serviceable budget choice with a bit of character; manage expectations on cleanliness and ask to see the room first.
#9

Hotel Mashabrum

Mid-range · Skardu city centre
📍 Skardu city centre🏨 Mid-range💷 $$

Why stay: One of Skardu's longest-running hotels, walkable to the main bazaar and Kharpocho Fort

What's good

  • Genuinely central; walk to the bazaar and Kharpocho Fort
  • Spacious rooms with private balconies
  • Helpful staff and an established track record

Could be better

  • Food quality gets mixed reviews
  • Decor is dated/modest rather than stylish
My take: The pick if you want to be right in town and walk everywhere; a dependable old-hand hotel rather than a stylish one.
#10

K2 Hotel by Greenpak

Budget · Skardu (terrace and garden)
📍 Skardu (terrace and garden)🏨 Budget💷 $

Why stay: Historic expedition base with an on-site K2/Karakoram museum and great terrace views

What's good

  • Iconic mountaineering history and a genuine K2 museum on site
  • Fantastic terrace and garden views
  • Long-established budget base for expeditions

Could be better

  • Rooms and bathrooms are dated and basic; some harsh reviews
  • Standards are inconsistent
My take: Choose it for the mountaineering heritage, views and price, not for room comfort; a budget pick with character.

How to choose where to stay in Skardu

  • Town vs lake - stay in Skardu town for food and transport, or out at Kachura/Shangrila for the view (but you'll drive in).
  • Book ahead in season - July-September and the K2/Deosai window fill fast; rates jump.
  • Heating & power - confirm room heating and backup power for shoulder-season and remote stays.
  • Location for jeeps - if you're doing Deosai, Khaplu or K2, a town base near the jeep stands saves time.

FAQ

Where should I stay in Skardu?

Skardu town if you want food, bazaar and transport on the doorstep; the Kachura/Shangrila lakeside if you want the view and don't mind driving in. See the ranked list above.

How much is a hotel in Skardu?

Budget guesthouses are cheap; mid-range town hotels are moderate; the lakeside resorts and top hotels are the priciest. Each place is marked $ to $$$.

Do Skardu hotels have heating and wifi?

The better hotels do, but in shoulder season and at remote/lake properties confirm heating, hot water and backup power before booking.

When should I book?

For July-September and the Deosai/K2 season, book weeks ahead - the best rooms and rates go early.
