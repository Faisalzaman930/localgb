I've ranked real, open places on location, comfort, value and view, and shown each one's real public rating where it has one.

How to choose where to stay in Skardu

  • Town vs lake - stay in Skardu town for food and transport, or out at Kachura/Shangrila for the view (but you'll drive in).
  • Book ahead in season - July-September and the K2/Deosai window fill fast; rates jump.
  • Heating & power - confirm room heating and backup power for shoulder-season and remote stays.
  • Location for jeeps - if you're doing Deosai, Khaplu or K2, a town base near the jeep stands saves time.

FAQ

Where should I stay in Skardu?

Skardu town if you want food, bazaar and transport on the doorstep; the Kachura/Shangrila lakeside if you want the view and don't mind driving in. See the ranked list above.

How much is a hotel in Skardu?

Budget guesthouses are cheap; mid-range town hotels are moderate; the lakeside resorts and top hotels are the priciest. Each place is marked $ to $$$.

Do Skardu hotels have heating and wifi?

The better hotels do, but in shoulder season and at remote/lake properties confirm heating, hot water and backup power before booking.

When should I book?

For July-September and the Deosai/K2 season, book weeks ahead - the best rooms and rates go early.
