Access

Getting to
Deosai

RouteSkardu → Sadpara Lake → Burji La (4,173m) → Deosai plateau → Sheosar Lake. 90km.
Vehicle4WD mandatory. The road includes stream fords and rough track.
Jeep hireRs.8,000-15,000/day from Skardu. Most guesthouses can arrange.
Entry feeRs.500/person at the NP checkpoint.
Key stopsSheosar Lake (cobalt blue, 4,142m), Bara Pani meadows (brown bear territory).
Best timeJuly-September for wildflowers and bears. September for clear skies.
Day vs campingDay trip possible but overnight camping at Sheosar strongly recommended.
