What to See

Asia's Second
Highest Plateau

Deosai is a vast high-altitude plateau, the second highest in Asia, sitting around 4,114m and about 2.5 hours from Skardu by 4WD. The road climbs from the city past Sadpara Lake and over the Burji La pass at 4,173m before opening onto the plains. The two set-piece stops are Sheosar Lake — a cobalt sheet of water at 4,142m — and the Bara Pani meadows, which are prime brown bear territory. The Deosai bears are the reason the plateau is a protected national park.

The season is short. The plateau is only open and snow-free roughly July to September; come for wildflowers and bears in midsummer, or September for the clearest skies. At this altitude the weather turns fast and nights are cold even in summer, so carry warm layers whatever the forecast. A day trip from Skardu is possible, but an overnight camp near Sheosar is strongly recommended — the plateau is at its best at dawn and dusk, exactly when day-trippers have already turned for home.

FAQ

Common
Questions

How long is the drive from Skardu to Deosai?

About 2.5-3 hours by 4WD, around 90km via Sadpara Lake and the Burji La pass.

Do I need a 4WD?

Yes, a 4WD is mandatory. The road includes stream fords and rough track. Jeep hire runs Rs.8,000-15,000 a day and most guesthouses can arrange it.

Is there an entry fee?

Yes, Rs.500 per person at the national park checkpoint.

When is the best time to visit?

July to September. Midsummer is best for wildflowers and bears, and September for the clearest skies.

Can I see brown bears?

Deosai is protected brown bear territory, with the best chances around the Bara Pani meadows, though sightings are never guaranteed.

Should I camp or do a day trip?

A day trip is possible, but an overnight camp at Sheosar Lake is strongly recommended for the dawn and dusk light.
