Thinking of Skardu in December? Here's the Honest Picture

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026

Thinking about Skardu in December? Here's the honest, on-the-ground picture for 2026 - the weather, what's actually open, how busy it is, and whether it's worth timing your trip for. In short, December is winter: cold, clear and atmospheric, with snow on the peaks and few visitors.

Weather in Skardu in December

  • Avg high ~0°C
  • Avg low ~-10°C
  • very cold, snow begins
  • Winter

Expect daytime highs around 0°C and nights near -10°C (very cold, snow begins). (Figures are Skardu station averages (climate-data.org); mountains and high villages are colder.)

What's open & getting around

  • ⛔ Deosai National Park is snow-bound and closed
  • ✅ Shangrila/Lower Kachura, Upper Kachura, Shigar Fort and Khaplu are open year-round
  • ❄️ Skardu flights and the road are most weather-affected now - keep buffer days

Crowds & prices

December is very quiet in Skardu. Prices are at their lowest and you'll often have sights to yourself.

Best things to do in Skardu in December

  • Snowbound Katpana/Sarfaranga desert and frozen lakes
  • Fresh trout and Balti karahi in a near-empty town
  • Shigar and Khaplu forts without a single other tourist

What to pack

A proper down jacket, thermals, gloves, hat and waterproof boots - nights are well below freezing.

Is December a good time to visit Skardu?

December is for travellers who want snow, solitude and the lowest prices - not for high-mountain access. See the full Skardu weather guide to compare months.
