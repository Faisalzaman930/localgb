Skardu
Weather

Cold desert climate. Down to -20°C on the coldest January nights, mid-30s°C on hot July afternoons. Very little rain, very clear skies. Here is what each month actually means for your trip.

Nights: -8°C
Extreme cold (record lows below -20°C). Road from Gilgit may close after heavy snow. Flights thinner and often cancelled. Only for extreme winter photography.

6°C

Best Season
May
23°C
Nights: 10°C
Excellent. Warm days, cool nights. Trekking season starting. Deosai usually still snowbound (it opens around mid-to-late June). Best all-round month for the valley.
Best Season
June
29°C
Nights: 13°C
Good conditions. Deosai usually opens around mid-to-late June (snowfall-dependent) and is at its wildflower best Jun-Jul. K2 trek season begins. Some afternoon heat in the valley.
July
32°C
Nights: 16°C
Hot in the city but surrounding mountains are cool. Peak tourism. Deosai wildflowers peak.
August
31°C
Nights: 16°C
Similar to July. Busy. Late July-mid August floods closed the Jaglot-Skardu road for days in 2025 and 2026 - keep 1-2 buffer days. Brown bear sightings in Deosai peak.
Best Season
September
27°C
Nights: 11°C
Excellent. Cooling. Clear skies. Fewer crowds. Deosai still accessible. One of the best months.
October
20°C
Nights: 4°C
Autumn colours. Deosai closes for season. Cold nights. Good for Skardu city and valley trips.
November
13°C
Nights: -2°C
Cold and quiet. Some facilities close. Shigar and Khaplu roads may become difficult.
December
7°C
Nights: -6°C
Winter returning. Very limited tourism. Only for those who specifically want cold desert winter landscapes.
Planning

When to
Visit Skardu

Best monthsMay-June and September for ideal all-round conditions and comfortable temperatures. For Deosai itself, aim for late June through September - it is usually snowbound until around mid-to-late June.
Peak seasonJuly-August. Busy, hot in the valley. Best for Deosai brown bears and K2 trek season.
Spring blossomApril. Apricot and cherry orchards. Beautiful but roads may still be clearing from winter.
WinterDecember-February. Extreme cold (-20°C possible on the coldest nights). Many facilities closed. Flights run on a thinner schedule and cancel often. For hardy travellers only.
FlightsMost reliable May-October. Winter cancellations can strand you for days.
