Honeymoon in Skardu: Forts, Lakes and the Best Romantic Stays

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated October 2026

Skardu has the most special honeymoon hotels in northern Pakistan: a 400-year-old fort in Shigar, a royal palace in Khaplu, and new lodges with glass walls facing the Indus. Add turquoise lakes, cold deserts and - in summer - the Deosai plateau, and it's a honeymoon you'll talk about for years.

Best time for a honeymoon in Skardu

  • April: apricot and cherry blossom in Shigar and Khaplu.
  • Mid-June to September: everything open, including Deosai (only open from about mid-June to late September or early October).
  • September to October: clear skies, golden light and quieter hotels - lovely for couples.
  • Winter: snowy and peaceful, but cold; the main Skardu hotels stay open.

Romantic things to do in Skardu

  • A boat on Lower Kachura (Shangrila) and a quiet walk at Upper Kachura Lake.
  • Sunset on the dunes of the Katpana or Sarfaranga cold deserts.
  • Breakfast in the gardens of Shigar Fort.
  • A day in Khaplu: the palace and the old Chaqchan Mosque.
  • In summer, a private jeep to Deosai for wildflowers and Sheosar Lake.

A 6-day Skardu honeymoon plan

Day 1Fly Islamabad to Skardu (about 1 h). Settle in; evening at Upper Kachura Lake.
Day 2Shangrila by boat, then Katpana desert at sunset.
Day 3To Shigar: Sarfaranga desert on the way, then a night at Shigar Fort.
Day 4On to Khaplu for the palace and Chaqchan Mosque (3-4 h from Skardu).
Day 5Back to Skardu - or, from mid-June to September, a day trip to Deosai.
Day 6Fly home. Keep the next day free in case the flight is cancelled.

Tips for couples travelling in Gilgit-Baltistan

  • Carry your CNICs or passports - hotels ask for ID at check-in, and so do checkposts on the way.
  • It's a conservative, friendly region: dress modestly and keep public affection low-key. In your hotel and on a private jeep, you'll have all the privacy you want.
  • Book the view room early for blossom (late March to mid-April) and autumn (late September to October) - the best rooms go first.
  • Carry enough cash; ATMs are limited outside the main towns.
  • Keep one spare day for weather, especially if you're flying.

FAQ

What is the best month for a honeymoon in Skardu?

April for blossom, or September to October for clear skies and golden light. Come between mid-June and September if you want to see Deosai.

Which is the most romantic hotel in Skardu?

Serena Shigar Fort, a restored 400-year-old fort-palace, is the most romantic. Himmel Skardu by Luxus and Arish Luxury Suites are the best modern luxury options; Shangrila has the famous lake setting but tired rooms.

How much does a Skardu honeymoon package cost?

The published 8-day honeymoon packages by road cost Rs 160,000 (breakfast and dinner) or Rs 190,000 (all meals). Flying in and planning privately can save time.

Should we fly or drive to Skardu for our honeymoon?

Fly if you can - it's about an hour instead of 16-18 hours by road. Flights are weather-dependent, so keep a spare day.
