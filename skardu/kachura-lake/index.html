Two Lakes

Choosing Between
Lower and Upper

Kachura is really two lakes with two very different moods. Lower Kachura, around 20km from Skardu, is the famous one - home to the Shangrila Resort with its red-roofed restaurant and manicured lake gardens. It is the postcard image most people have of Skardu, and on a calm morning the water mirrors the surrounding hills. It can get busy, so early is best.

Upper Kachura, a little further on (a 20-30 minute uphill walk from the lower lake), is the opposite: no resort, no crowds, just a quiet lakeshore where camping is possible. If you want the polished, easy experience, go to Lower Kachura; if you want stillness and space, push on to the upper lake. Many visitors do both in a single half-day loop, starting early at Lower Kachura while the light is best.

FAQ

Common
Questions

What is the difference between Lower and Upper Kachura?

Lower Kachura, about 20km from Skardu, has the Shangrila Resort, a restaurant and boats and can get busy. Upper Kachura, a short uphill walk beyond, has no resort or facilities and is much quieter, with camping possible.

How far is Kachura Lake from Skardu?

Lower Kachura is about 20km, roughly a 30-40 minute drive. Upper Kachura is a little further, about a 20-30 minute uphill walk from the lower lake.

Can I take a boat on the lake?

Yes. Rowboats and motorboats are available at Lower Kachura by the Shangrila Resort.

When is the best time to visit?

Early morning, around 7-9am, when the water is calm enough for mirror reflections and before the day crowds arrive at Lower Kachura.

Can I camp at Kachura?

Camping is possible at Upper Kachura, which has a quiet lakeshore and no facilities, so you need to bring everything including food and water.
