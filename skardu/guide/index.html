Best Time

When to
Visit Skardu

Skardu sits at 2,228m in a cold desert climate, which means a wide swing between roughly -20°C winters and 35°C summers. For most visitors the sweet spot is May to June and again in September, when the roads are reliably open, Deosai is accessible, and the temperatures are comfortable for both sightseeing and trekking.

How you arrive matters just as much as when. The Skardu flight is fast but weather-dependent, with a 40 to 60 percent cancellation rate, so always allow buffer days rather than planning a tight itinerary. The road is the dependable alternative — 270km and around 8 hours from Gilgit, or 600km and 20 to 22 hours from Islamabad via the Karakoram Highway. Many travellers fly one way and drive the other so they see the Indus gorge and still have a guaranteed route.

FAQ

Common
Questions

What is the best time to visit Skardu?

May to June and September, when roads are open, Deosai is accessible, and temperatures are comfortable.

How high is Skardu?

Skardu sits at 2,228m and has a cold desert climate, with winters around -20°C and summers up to 35°C.

How do I get to Skardu?

By air from Islamabad or Lahore, though flights cancel 40 to 60 percent of the time, or by road — 270km from Gilgit or 600km from Islamabad via the KKH.

Is Skardu the gateway to K2?

Yes. The Askole trailhead for the K2 Base Camp trek is about 2 hours from Skardu by jeep.

What are the must-see places near Skardu?

Kharpocho Fort, Shangrila Resort, the Katpana cold desert, Deosai Plains, and Shigar Fort.
