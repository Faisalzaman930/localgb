Choosing

Which Length
Suits You

The three itineraries on this page cover very different trips. The 3-day City & Lakes route is a tight weekend: Kharpocho Fort at sunset, the bazaar, the cold desert, Satpara Lake and a run out to Shigar Fort. It is the right choice if your flights are short or uncertain. The 5-day version keeps that core and adds a full day on the Deosai plateau — 4WD across the plains, Sheosar Lake and a real chance of spotting brown bears. The 7-day Full Baltistan circuit then pushes east to Khaplu Palace and Manthokha Waterfall, returning through Shigar Valley.

Costs scale with both length and group size: roughly Rs.25,000-40,000 per person for the 3-day, Rs.40,000-65,000 for the 5-day, and Rs.55,000-85,000 for the 7-day, with per-person prices falling as you add travellers to share the jeep and rooms. Packages typically bundle transport, accommodation and entry fees. The honest caveat for Skardu: build in a buffer day. Flights into Skardu cancel with weather, and the Deosai and Khaplu legs depend on road and track conditions.

FAQ

Common
Questions

How much does a Skardu tour package cost?

Roughly Rs.25,000-40,000 per person for the 3-day, Rs.40,000-65,000 for the 5-day, and Rs.55,000-85,000 for the 7-day, with prices per person falling as group size grows.

What is included?

Most packages bundle transport, accommodation and entry fees.

Which itinerary should I pick?

Choose the 3-day for a short city-and-lakes weekend, the 5-day to add a full Deosai day, or the 7-day for the full Baltistan circuit including Khaplu.

Does the price change with group size?

Yes. Per-person cost drops as you add travellers, since transport and rooms are shared.

Should I leave extra days?

It is wise to. Flights into Skardu cancel with weather, and the Deosai and Khaplu legs depend on road conditions, so a buffer day helps.
