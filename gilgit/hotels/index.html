I Checked Out Where to Stay in Gilgit - the Best Hotels for 2026

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026

Most GB trips pass through Gilgit, and it has the region's widest range of places to stay: a polished luxury hotel, reliable mid-range business hotels near the airport, and cheap, central guesthouses. Here's the honest ranked list for 2026.

I've ranked real, open places on location, comfort, value and quiet, with each one's real public rating where it has one.

How I picked these Gilgit hotels

I went through the places actually worth booking in Gilgit across luxury, mid-range and budget, judging each on location, comfort, value and the view. 5 of 7 have a real public rating, shown with the review count and a maps link; the rest I rate from local knowledge and mark with a dash rather than invent a score.

The 7 best, reviewed

#1

Gilgit Serena Hotel

Luxury · Jutial
📍 Jutial🏨 Luxury💷 $$$★ 4.0 Tripadvisor (332)

Why stay: garden, mountain views & service

What's good

  • Ranked #1 of hotels in Gilgit on Tripadvisor; the only true 5-star in the city
  • Large landscaped garden, Rakaposhi-facing rooms and free airport shuttle
  • Well-trained, attentive staff and impeccable cleanliness (Booking.com 8.8/10 over 178 reviews, with 9.1 cleanliness)

Could be better

  • Easily the most expensive option in town
  • Restaurant menu selection is fairly limited
My take: The default safe choice for foreign and business travelers who want secure, comfortable, internationally-run lodging. You pay a premium, but nothing else in Gilgit matches it.
#2

Grand Continental Hotel

Mid-range · Chinar Bagh
📍 Chinar Bagh🏨 Mid-range💷 $$★ 4.0 Google (257)

Why stay: river-view location

What's good

  • Strong Google score (4.0 over 257 reviews) for a mid-range property
  • On Riverview Road in Chinar Bagh, near the Gilgit River
  • Free breakfast, WiFi and parking

Could be better

  • Listed as a B&B/inn tier, not a full-service hotel
  • Finishes and upkeep are modest
My take: A reliable, well-reviewed mid-range pick in the leafy Chinar Bagh riverside area, good if you want decent comfort without Serena pricing.
#3

PTDC Gilgit Motel

Mid-range · bazaar
📍 bazaar🏨 Mid-range💷 $$★ 4.0 Google (87)

Why stay: large lawn & central location

What's good

  • One of the oldest established hotels in Gilgit, centrally located near the river
  • Big lawn/garden and well-regarded restaurant with good dinner and breakfast service
  • Garden-view rooms are notably nicer than the standards

Could be better

  • State-run upkeep is inconsistent; some rooms and bathrooms feel dated
  • WiFi is unreliable
My take: A dependable, centrally-located government-run motel with a pleasant lawn and good food. Ask specifically for a garden-view room, as quality varies.
#4

Madina Hotel 2

Mid-range · Airport Rd
📍 Airport Rd🏨 Mid-range💷 $$★ 4.0 Tripadvisor (23)

Why stay: central courtyard garden & traveler hub

What's good

  • Booking.com 8.6/10 over ~109 reviews; rooms set around a peaceful garden courtyard
  • Two-minute walk from the airport, ~10 minutes to the bazaar
  • Owner Mr. Yaqoob is a long-standing local fixer who helps arrange treks and transport

Could be better

  • A backpacker/traveler hub rather than a polished hotel
  • Rooms are simple; don't expect luxury finishes
My take: Gilgit's classic traveler base camp: clean, friendly, great value and a good place to meet other trekkers and sort onward logistics.
#5

Riveria Hotel

Budget · Chinar Bagh
📍 Chinar Bagh🏨 Budget💷 $★ 3.0 Tripadvisor (10)

Why stay: riverside garden & food

What's good

  • Excellent flower-filled garden and a restaurant well known locally for its food
  • Sits right by the Gilgit River with rooftop terrace and garden views
  • Friendly, helpful staff and genuinely cheap rates

Could be better

  • Mixed cleanliness reports: dusty, musty rooms noted by some guests
  • Hot water only available on demand roughly 7-10 am; limited heating
My take: A cheap, characterful riverside spot loved for its garden and food, but a budget stay with hit-or-miss housekeeping. Best in warmer months.
#6

Marco Polo Hotel

Budget · bazaar
📍 bazaar🏨 Budget💷 $

Why stay: low price & central Khomer location

What's good

  • Very cheap, around PKR 3,500/night
  • Central at Yadgah Chowk, Khomer, close to bazaar amenities
  • Rooms include TV, room service, heater/geyser and 24-hour security

Could be better

  • Only ~15 rooms, so it books out fast in peak season
  • Basic standard-hotel finishes; no notable views
My take: A no-frills, budget-friendly base in central Gilgit. Fine for a functional, cheap night near the bazaar if you book ahead.
#7

North Inn Hotel

Budget · bazaar
📍 bazaar🏨 Budget💷 $

Why stay: long-established central inn

What's good

  • Long-running property established in 1987 at Khomer Chowk
  • Central location convenient to the bazaar and transport

Could be better

  • An older, basic property; expect dated rooms
  • Little verifiable recent review data online
My take: A veteran budget inn in central Gilgit. Worth considering as a fallback when the better-reviewed budget options are full, but inspect the room first.

How to choose where to stay in Gilgit

  • Near the airport - handy if you're flying in/out on a weather-dependent PIA flight that can shift.
  • Jutial & Chinar Bagh - quieter, greener parts of town for a calmer night.
  • It's usually a transit night - most travellers stay one night either side of Hunza/Skardu, so prioritise location and a good bed over resort features.
  • Confirm power & hot water - load-shedding happens; the better hotels have backup.

FAQ

Where should I stay in Gilgit?

Near the airport if you're flying and want flexibility; Jutial or Chinar Bagh for a quieter, greener stay; the bazaar area for budget and convenience. See the list above.

Is Gilgit just a transit stop?

For most travellers, yes - a night either side of Hunza or Skardu. So pick for location and a comfortable bed rather than resort extras.

How much is a hotel in Gilgit?

Budget guesthouses are cheap; mid-range hotels are moderate; the top hotel (Serena) is the priciest. Marked $ to $$$ in the table.

Do Gilgit hotels have backup power?

The better ones do - useful given occasional load-shedding. Confirm backup power and hot water when you book.
