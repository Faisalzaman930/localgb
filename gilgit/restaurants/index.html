I Tried Gilgit's Restaurants - Here Are the Best for 2026

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026

Gilgit is where most GB trips begin or end, and it has the region's broadest spread of food: river trout, hearty Pakistani karahi and BBQ, Hunzai and Shina local dishes, and the chai dhabbas locals actually use. Here is the honest ranked list for 2026.

I ranked real, open places on food, value, setting and consistency, and shown each one's real public rating where it has one.

How I picked these Gilgit restaurants

I went through the places that are actually open and worth your time in Gilgit, judging each on the food, the setting, value and consistency rather than hype. 10 of 10 have a real public rating, which I show with the review count and a maps link; the rest are local spots I rate from experience, shown with a dash rather than an invented score.

The 10 best, reviewed

#1

Dawat Restaurant @ Indus Lodges

Pakistani / Chinese / BBQ · River View Rd (riverfront, near Food Street)
📍 River View Rd (riverfront, near Food Street)🍴 Pakistani / Chinese / BBQ💷 $$★ 4.9 Google (355)

Must try: Charcoal BBQ platter and grilled trout on the rooftop

What's good

  • Rooftop terrace with direct Gilgit River and mountain views
  • Consistently rated the highest-scoring sit-down spot in the city
  • Fresh food, attentive service noted repeatedly in reviews

Could be better

  • Rooftop fills up fast at sunset/dinner rush
  • Pricier than the bazaar dhabbas
My take: The current darling of Gilgit dining - go at sunset for the river view and order the BBQ or trout. Book ahead in peak season.
#2

Mawaid

Pakistani / Middle Eastern · Ghazi Market, near FCNA
📍 Ghazi Market, near FCNA🍴 Pakistani / Middle Eastern💷 $$★ 4.9 Tripadvisor (10)

Must try: Tawa Chicken; Yak Karahi with nan

What's good

  • Tawa chicken repeatedly singled out as the best in Gilgit
  • Unusual Yak Karahi for adventurous eaters
  • Calm ambience, ample parking, quick courteous staff

Could be better

  • Small review base so far - newer spot
  • Tucked in a market, not a scenic riverfront setting
My take: Go for the tawa chicken and yak karahi - locals rate the food highly even if it lacks a view. A rising favorite.
#3

Cafe 1972

BBQ / Chinese / cafe · The Mall of Gilgit, Sakwar
📍 The Mall of Gilgit, Sakwar🍴 BBQ / Chinese / cafe💷 $$★ 4.5 Google (80)

Must try: BBQ platter; cafe-style mains

What's good

  • Modern cafe ambience, popular with younger crowd
  • Solid BBQ and Chinese options
  • Inside the Mall so easy to combine with errands

Could be better

  • Mall location lacks the river/mountain view of riverfront spots
  • Smaller review base than the top riverfront names
My take: A reliable, modern cafe-restaurant in town for BBQ and a coffee - good if you want indoor comfort over a riverside view.
#4

Dumani (Gilgit Serena Hotel)

Pakistani / Continental / Chinese · Sherullah Baig Rd, Jutial
📍 Sherullah Baig Rd, Jutial🍴 Pakistani / Continental / Chinese💷 $$$★ 4.5 Tripadvisor (19)

Must try: Local trout and the Pakistani mains; valley-view dining

What's good

  • Among the best cooking standards in Gilgit
  • Excellent valley views, very clean and hygienic
  • Reliable for travelers wanting upscale, safe dining

Could be better

  • Serena prices are the highest in town
  • Breakfast buffet criticized as below 5-star standard
My take: The splurge option - Serena's restaurant is the most polished, hygienic, view-blessed dining in Gilgit, at a price to match.
#5

Gilgit Food Yard

Local Gilgiti / Central Asian (Chapli kebab, Mantu, Laghman) · River View Rd (alongside the Gilgit River)
📍 River View Rd (alongside the Gilgit River)🍴 Local Gilgiti / Central Asian (Chapli kebab, Mantu, Laghman)💷 $$★ 4.3 Google (269)

Must try: Wild-caught trout and chapli kebab

What's good

  • River-side setting framed by mountains
  • Strong on local specialties like Mantu and Laghman, not just generic Pakistani fare
  • Reasonable prices for the quality and location

Could be better

  • Casual outdoor seating - less polished than hotel restaurants
  • Can get busy in the evening rush
My take: Best pick if you want genuine Gilgiti/Central Asian dishes by the river rather than standard karahi. Order the trout and Mantu.
#6

Sivana

Local / Continental / Chinese · Food Street, River View Rd (Indus bank)
📍 Food Street, River View Rd (Indus bank)🍴 Local / Continental / Chinese💷 $$★ 4.2 Google (34)

Must try: Shinwari-style Mutton Karahi with tandoori roti

What's good

  • Rooftop seating right on the river bank
  • Shinwari mutton karahi and quick, caring service praised
  • Hygienic, family-friendly

Could be better

  • Reviewers say the interior/ambiance needs work - the river view carries it
  • Smaller, less-known than the top riverfront spots
My take: Come for the riverside rooftop and the Shinwari mutton karahi; the setting outshines the decor.
#7

Pizza King GB

Pizza / fast food · FCNA Market, Jutial
📍 FCNA Market, Jutial🍴 Pizza / fast food💷 $$★ 4.1 Google (858)

Must try: Pizza and fast-food combos

What's good

  • By far the most-reviewed eatery in town - a genuine local fast-food institution
  • Go-to for families and a break from desi food
  • Quick, consistent, kid-friendly

Could be better

  • Fast-food quality, not gourmet pizza by city standards
  • Busy, functional setting with no view
My take: Where Gilgit goes when it wants pizza - not fine dining, but dependable and the local fast-food default.
#8

Pakiza Hotel and Restaurant

Desi / Pakistani · Shaheed-e-Millat Rd
📍 Shaheed-e-Millat Rd🍴 Desi / Pakistani💷 $★ 4.1 Google (150)

Must try: Chicken karahi and tandoori roti

What's good

  • Solid no-frills desi food at budget prices
  • Central, easy to find on the main road
  • Decent karahi and BBQ for the money

Could be better

  • Basic dining room, nothing scenic
  • Standard menu - few local specialties
My take: A dependable budget desi stop in the center of town for karahi and roti without fuss.
#9

Indus Hotel & Restaurant

Local / Pakistani · Indus Market, Col Hassan Rd, Cinema Bazar
📍 Indus Market, Col Hassan Rd, Cinema Bazar🍴 Local / Pakistani💷 $★ 4.1 Google (71)

Must try: Karahi and local Pakistani staples

What's good

  • Right in the bazaar - convenient old-city location
  • Honest local Pakistani cooking at low prices
  • Good for a quick bite while exploring Cinema Bazar

Could be better

  • Basic bazaar setting, no views
  • Limited menu range
My take: A handy bazaar eatery for cheap, straightforward local food when you're in the old city.
#10

Paradise Hotel & Restaurant

Pakistani / Gilgiti · River View Rd, Sonikot
📍 River View Rd, Sonikot🍴 Pakistani / Gilgiti💷 $$★ 3.8 Google (989)

Must try: Chicken Handi

What's good

  • One of the most-reviewed restaurants in Gilgit - well established
  • Chicken Handi singled out by diners
  • Riverfront-area location

Could be better

  • Mid-3s rating - quality and service reported as inconsistent
  • Large operation can feel impersonal at peak times
My take: A long-standing, high-traffic name; the Chicken Handi is the pick, but expect uneven consistency given the mixed rating.

What to eat in Gilgit

  • Trout - Gilgit and its rivers are trout country; grilled is best.
  • Karahi & BBQ - the city does a strong chicken/mutton karahi and tikka.
  • Shina & Hunzai local food - daal, local breads, chapshuro and seasonal vegetables.
  • Chai dhabbas - roadside tea stops for doodh-patti and parathas, a Gilgit ritual.

FAQ

What food is Gilgit famous for?

River trout, chicken and mutton karahi, BBQ/tikka, Shina and Hunzai local dishes, and strong roadside chai. It is the most varied food scene in Gilgit-Baltistan.

Where do tourists eat in Gilgit?

Along Airport Road, Jutial and the main bazaar, plus hotel restaurants and the trout spots. See the ranked list above.

Is Gilgit good for a quick or budget meal?

Very - karahi houses, BBQ stalls and chai dhabbas are cheap and quick. Budget places are marked $ in the table.

How much does a meal cost in Gilgit?

Street and dhabba food is very cheap; a trout or full karahi dinner at a mid-range restaurant costs more. The table marks each place $ to $$$.
