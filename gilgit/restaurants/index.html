I Tried Gilgit's Restaurants - Here Are the Best for 2026

By Faisal Zaman·Local, Gilgit-Baltistan·Updated June 2026

Gilgit is where most GB trips begin or end, and it has the region's broadest spread of food: river trout, hearty Pakistani karahi and BBQ, Hunzai and Shina local dishes, and the chai dhabbas locals actually use. Here is the honest ranked list for 2026.

I ranked real, open places on food, value, setting and consistency, and shown each one's real public rating where it has one.

How I picked these Gilgit restaurants

I went through the places that are actually open and worth your time in Gilgit, judging each on the food, the setting, value and consistency rather than hype. 12 of 12 have a real public rating, which I show with the review count and a maps link; the rest are local spots I rate from experience, shown with a dash rather than an invented score.

#1

Try something you can't get elsewhere

The dishes worth crossing Gilgit for - genuinely local to Gilgit-Baltistan, not the usual Pakistani menu:

  • Charsi (Shinwari) lamb karahi - at Dera Shinwari Gilgit
  • Grilled river trout - at Dawat Restaurant (Indus Lodges)
  • Grilled trout - at Dumani (Gilgit Serena Hotel)
  • Yak karahi - at Mawaid
  • Wild-caught river trout, mantu, laxman - at Gilgit Food Yard
  • Chapshuro (Gilgiti meat-filled bread) - at Paradise Hotel & Restaurant

What to eat in Gilgit

  • Trout - Gilgit and its rivers are trout country; grilled is best.
  • Karahi & BBQ - the city does a strong chicken/mutton karahi and tikka.
  • Shina & Hunzai local food - daal, local breads, chapshuro and seasonal vegetables.
  • Chai dhabbas - roadside tea stops for doodh-patti and parathas, a Gilgit ritual.

FAQ

What food is Gilgit famous for?

River trout, chicken and mutton karahi, BBQ/tikka, Shina and Hunzai local dishes, and strong roadside chai. It is the most varied food scene in Gilgit-Baltistan.

Where do tourists eat in Gilgit?

Along Airport Road, Jutial and the main bazaar, plus hotel restaurants and the trout spots. See the ranked list above.

Is Gilgit good for a quick or budget meal?

Very - karahi houses, BBQ stalls and chai dhabbas are cheap and quick. Budget places are marked $ in the table.

How much does a meal cost in Gilgit?

Street and dhabba food is very cheap; a trout or full karahi dinner at a mid-range restaurant costs more. The table marks each place $ to $$$.

Where can I try Charsi in Gilgit?

Dera Shinwari Gilgit is the spot locals point to. See the full list above for other genuinely local dishes and where to find them.
